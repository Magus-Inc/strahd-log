## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The staff of the druids' leader, which controlled the blights that plagued the Wizard of Wines vineyard. Davian told us it must be destroyed or stolen ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/)). The druids had caught ravens, believing them spies for the shadow lord. One druid boasted that he held the staff. We cut him down and destroyed it ([Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)).
