## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

One of the green gemstones of the Wizard of Wines winery, with magic that makes plants grow. Of the winery's gems, one was lost ten years ago, one was stolen by Baba Lysaga and one was taken by the druids ([Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)). We destroyed Winter Splinter and took the gemheart from it, but at a heavy cost: it burst apart as it died, and nothing was left of Veyren's body ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
