## Description

![Argynvost, a vast ghostly silver dragon, over Argynvostholt with kneeling knights below](/images/argynvost-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The silver dragon who founded the Order of the Silver Dragon. His last journal page at Argynvostholt vowed to defend the valley with his life ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)). Strahd stole his skull, and we took it down from over the doors of the Hall of Bones ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/), [Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)). When Aera set it back in the mausoleum, he rose as a vast ghostly dragon, gave Vladimir Horngaard his rest, freed his knights, blessed Olfin's longsword and became a silver beacon over the valley ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).

## From the adventure log

*Compiled from the session posts. Each line links to the session it comes from.*

* Strahd stole his skull. ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/))

* Rose as a ghostly silver dragon when his skull was set on its mount in the mausoleum. ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/))
* Freed his knights, blessed Olfin's longsword and asked the party to bear the Order's legacy. ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/))
* Burned his spirit into a silver beacon in Argynvostholt's tallest tower, lasting perhaps about seven days. ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/))
