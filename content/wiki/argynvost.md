## Description

![Argynvost, a vast ghostly silver dragon, over Argynvostholt with kneeling knights below](/images/argynvost-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini).*

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

The silver dragon who founded the Order of the Silver Dragon. Letters called him Protector of the Balinok Mountains. His last journal page at Argynvostholt, signed only "A", was written as his knights fell and the land was lost: he would die defending the valley, its home and the Order's ideals, throw off his guise to show his true dragon form, and be remembered as a dragon of honor and valor. He had laid down his belongings beneath the tower's watchful gaze so others could carry on his legacy, and wrote that his spirit would live on wherever the oath and fire of the Silver Dragon survive ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)). Strahd stole his skull, and we took it down from over the doors of the Hall of Bones ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/), [Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)). When Aera set it back in the mausoleum, he rose as a vast ghostly dragon, gave Vladimir Horngaard his rest, freed his knights, blessed Olfin's longsword and became a silver beacon over the valley ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
