The Abbot's first try at a perfect vessel, made before Vasilka: a body meant to hold a pure soul. It failed, so he chained it beneath the Abbey of Saint Markovia and forgot it. Behind a red door none of us had noticed, below a hall of niches holding a keepsake from every soul that passed through the Abbey, we found it standing over Aera, who had been flung into its room and hurt. With the Abbot changed, hundreds of trapped fragments of souls had flooded into it and woken it. It stood nine feet tall, with six mismatched arms, broken pieces of angel's wings, dozens of closed eyes stitched across its body and a golden halo nailed into its skull, and it spoke in the voices of the dead, Doru among them.

It struck Olfin hard, but fire, light, steel and acid wore it down, and Liren's battleaxe went straight through it. It burst apart and hundreds of spirits streamed up through the ceiling. Liren and Gumbo, who had come back to fight beside us one last time, faded into small flames that sank into its remains, and Aera found them there in a little silver reliquary ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
