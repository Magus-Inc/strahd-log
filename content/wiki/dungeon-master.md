## At the table

Ryan Mathews is the Dungeon Master of this Curse of Strahd campaign. The campaign began with [Session Zero](/posts/session-zero/) in November 2023. He plays Strahd and everyone else in Barovia, describes the land as we travel it, and rolls for whatever waits in the fog.

Malron writes this log from inside Barovia and does not know there is a table at all, so the stories never name Ryan. Where a post talks about the table, he is "the DM".

### What he shared with the table

Besides running the sessions, Ryan posted texts on the group's Discord for the party to read. Each date is the session it belongs to.

* **16 April 2024**, [Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/): Van Richten's manuscript, *A Study of the Werewolf's Curse*.
* **13 August 2024**, [A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/): an elf survivor's memory of how the dusk elves lost their kingdom to King Barov and Rahadin.
* **29 October 2024**, [Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/): how Van Richten came to Vallaki as Rictavio, with Arabelle hidden in his wagon.
* **14 January 2025**, [The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/): the Forest Folk druids, and why Lady Fiona Wachter is banished from Berez.
* **22 April 2025**, [Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/): a card reading, and the rules for the Obsidian Whistle.
* **3 June 2025**, [Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/): the knightly vow of the Order of the Silver Dragon, Argynvost's last letter, Andral's letter, and the items on the hidden shelf.

He also shared the rules for items and companions as the party found them: the Talisman of Echoes, Zorya's stat block, the Clairvoyance Stone, the Symbol of Ravenkind, the Staff of Khazan and Olfin's Longsword. Many wiki pages, from the Dusk Elves to the Order of the Silver Dragon, were filled in from his texts.
