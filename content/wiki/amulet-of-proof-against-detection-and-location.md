## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

An amulet that hides its wearer from any magic that seeks them. Van Richten wears one, and he gave a spare to Arabelle. He had once given it to one of his students and later had it back; it had been Ezmerelda d'Avenir's, though he would say no more about that. He would not trust the [tower](/wiki/tower-of-khazan/) at Lake Baratok to hide Arabelle instead, because its field that blocks magic is just what Strahd would expect, and it would be among the first places he looked ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).
