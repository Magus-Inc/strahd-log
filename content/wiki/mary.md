## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A woman of the Village of Barovia and the mother of Gertruda, whom Strahd lured to his castle. Freeing Gertruda from the King's Bedchamber was one of the goals of our heist ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)). Mary still lives in the village ([The Slipper and the Shadow of the Bat](/posts/the-slipper-and-the-shadow-of-the-bat/)), and when our work in [the castle](/wiki/castle-ravenloft/) was done we chose to take Gertruda home ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)).
