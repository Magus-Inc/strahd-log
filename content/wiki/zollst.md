## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A trapper we met with Matilda on the road back north to town. They said they came from Krethi, though Szoldar had never heard of them. They told of a lady who slays with a blade that thirsts for the blood of innocents, warned that we had forgotten one thing, silver, and then turned into werewolves. We fought hard and killed them both ([The Staff is a lie](/posts/the-staff-is-a-lie/)).
