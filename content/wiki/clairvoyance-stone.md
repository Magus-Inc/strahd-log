## Item text

*As shared at the table on 10 February 2026:*

> This smooth, polished, palm-sized stone is engraved with a carving of a circular, avian eye. While holding the stone to their eye, the stone’s holder can use their action to whisper a location permitted by the clairvoyance spell. The holder then gains the effects of the clairvoyance spell.
>
> The stone can be used to cast the clairvoyance spell three times. The third time the spell is cast, the stone crumbles to dust after the spell ends.
