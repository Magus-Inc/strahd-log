*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A misty spirit realm full of restless spirits. Erasmus believes a gallows speaker haunts Stella there, near Vallaki. Victor's spell had trapped her in it, and he has a book from Mordenkainen that can carry up to nine creatures there on a night of the full moon, with a hag's heart stone ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)). Lady Fiona dropped her wards to let us go in. Victor used the heart stone to carry us across, and a teenage voice cried "Victor!" behind us. We rescued Stella ([Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)).
