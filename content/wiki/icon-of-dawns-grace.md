## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A silver statuette, alive, a shard of the divinity of an angelic servant of the Morning Lord. Esmerelda's séance first told us of it ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)). I found it in Saint Markovia's robes in her crypt, and it spoke to me mind to mind ([The Echoing Choirs of Ravenloft](/posts/the-echoing-choirs-of-ravenloft/)). It told us the Abbot's history and warned us to ready our steel ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)). Through it we reached the Abbot's soul and Saint Markovia restored him ([The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)). It has not spoken since ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
