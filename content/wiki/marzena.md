## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A woman at the Abbey of Saint Markovia who seems able to fly: the Abbot became an eagle and took to the sky to fetch her. She brought us a necklace of flowers and told us of another creature like her in the east wing, where she is not allowed to go. She thinks of it as her sibling and wanted it to have the necklace as a gift. I promised to deliver it, and I gave the garland to the flesh golem in the east wing ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).
