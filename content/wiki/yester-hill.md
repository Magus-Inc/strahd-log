## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The Forest Folk hold Yester Hill sacred, at the southwest edge of the valley, and worship Strahd ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)). A mountain woman warned us to keep clear of them ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/)). Davian told us the druids had the vineyard's last gem and meant to call up Winter Splinter on the hill before dawn ([Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)).

Lightning struck the summit as we came. A fifty-foot statue of a cloaked, fanged man stood inside two rings of stones, thirteen druids chanting around it, bound in thorny vines. Strahd floated above to watch, and his bride Ludmilla met us; we slipped past her rather than fight ([Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)). We destroyed Winter Splinter and took the gemheart, and Veyren died in its bursting ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
