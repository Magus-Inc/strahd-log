## Description

![Two hag sisters, Bella and Offalia, coming down the stair beside the millstone in the windmill](/images/belladonna-sunbane-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

One of [Morgantha](/wiki/morgantha/)'s two daughters, who lived with her at [the windmill](/wiki/the-windmill/) ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)). When we planned our raid, she and her sister Offalia were up in the attic with the stolen children while their mother cooked below ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)). On the night itself Morgantha called them down to the first floor to meet Shabasta and Olfin, which let our rogues climb the tower and free the children. Her true name, Belladonna Sunbane, was written in Abyssal on the coven's contract; Lady Fiona read it, and with it we bound the coven and beat it ([The Windmill Heist](/posts/the-windmill-heist/)).
