## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Flooded prison cells deep beneath Castle Ravenloft, past the Larders of Ill Omen. We waded through three to five feet of murky, ice-cold water, roped together to keep from losing one another. A hidden plate carried Olfin off to the torture chamber, and Aera led the rest of us past the traps to the cells, where we heard a prisoner's teeth chattering. Emil was held here ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)).
