## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

One of the Belviews, the mongrelfolk the Abbot keeps at the Abbey of St. Markovia ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).

He and Zygfrek met us at the abbey's north gate, excited and anxious at once. They believed their brother Cyrus had been sent to Castle Ravenloft to be perfected, made whole by Strahd, and they pressed us for news: had we seen him? Was he strong? Was he beautiful? We knew Cyrus is little more than a servant there, but Shabasta told them what they wanted to hear, and they believed him ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).
