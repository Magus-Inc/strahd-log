## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A werewolf who lives in the woods near the [werewolf den](/wiki/lake-baratok-cave/) with her husband Kiril, the Black Terror, who leads the pack. We met her by a tower, playing a lyre she had taken from a wagon. She told us Kiril has been full of rage since Strahd came back, where the pack keeps its prisoners, and how many stay behind to guard. She seemed torn between her love for Kiril and her own survival ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).
