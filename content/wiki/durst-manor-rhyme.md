## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

When the last of us stepped into [Durst Manor](/wiki/durst-manor/), the doors slammed shut, every light went out and six chimes rang. By torchlight we found this verse written in blood on a wall of the first floor ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). The beast it warns of slept beneath the house.

## The rhyme

*As it was written on the wall:*

> Beneath this dwelling lurks a beast  
> Who hungers for a bloody feast.  
> He sleeps until the midnight chime  
> Then wakes to feed his dark design.  
> If morsels seek to flee their doom,  
> Then bring toward his secret room  
> A gift to soothe his savage mood  
> But mind the servants of his brood.
