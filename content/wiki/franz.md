## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A refugee at the camp outside Vallaki, tormented by nightmares. His wife died three months ago and left him with their two children, and he used Morgantha's dream pastries to dream of her. When his money ran out he sold the children to her for endless pastries. He fell sick three nights before we met him, and in his nightmares he hears windmill blades turning. His sister Bianca cares for him ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)).
