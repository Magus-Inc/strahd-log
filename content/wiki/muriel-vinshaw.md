*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A worker at the Wizard of Wines vineyard. Urwin arranged for her to guide us there, and on the road southwest she warned us of strix. Near the vineyard she stumbled into a snare, and when a dark-cloaked figure beckoned to us, she knew him: Davian ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/)). She stayed behind to guard our way out while we went through the tunnel ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/)).

On the road to Yester Hill, Strahd took a curious interest in her hairline, as though wondering where she came from. When Ludmilla told us of a wereraven among those who attacked Castle Ravenloft, whom she had meant to cut open alive, Muriel trembled: she fears it was one of Davian's sons, her lover ([Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)). She later told us Strahd went to Madam Eva for a reading before we did ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)).
