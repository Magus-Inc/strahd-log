Anna Krezkova, Baron Dmitri Krezkov's wife, who leads Krezk with him. When we first came to Krezk she was gathering wolfsbane to hold back the werewolf curse that runs in her husband's family. The Abbot had worked his way into their house in the shape of Dmitri's aunt and talked their son Ilya out of the potion; the boy turned that night, killed his little sister, and ran off into the woods. Then the Abbot promised to bring the girl back if Anna would give up her heart a month on ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)).

Long after, while we rested in Barovia, word came that a snow-white feather from the [Abbey](/wiki/abbey-of-saint-markovia/) had landed in Krezk: the Abbot meant to collect her heart at dusk the next day. Dmitri found us in Vallaki and begged us to save her, and at the gates of Krezk Anna met us herself, grateful and terrified ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)). The Abbot meant to kill her that night and give her heart to Vasilka, his bride for Strahd. We went up to the Abbey to stop him, and Aera, remembering what he wanted with Anna's heart, shot him through his own ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).
