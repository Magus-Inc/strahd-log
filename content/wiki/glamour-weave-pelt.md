## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A wolf pelt that Aera bought for Shabasta for fifty gold pieces in the shack of Szoldar and his brother Yevgeni. Only afterward did she find it was made of glamour weave, a rare and magical cloth ([Looking for Khazan's Tower](/posts/looking-for-zoldars-tower/)).

## What it does

*Standard rules from the book; the DM linked them on 10/1/2024, and they may differ at our table.*

* Glamerweave is clothing woven with harmless illusion magic. It comes as a common or an uncommon item.
* **Common:** as a bonus action, the wearer makes a moving illusory pattern play across the cloth.
* **Uncommon:** the illusion can rise off the cloth, such as harmless flames around a gown or butterflies around a hat. Once per dawn, the wearer can add a d4 to a Charisma (Performance) or Charisma (Persuasion) check.
