Where the Old Svalich Road crosses the Luna River west of Vallaki: roads and trails lead on to
Lake Baratok, the Raven River crossroads and Krezk, Argynvostholt, and south along the river.

* Two ominous scarecrows and a broken signpost stood here. ([Looking for Khazan's Tower](/posts/looking-for-zoldars-tower/))
* The party crossed a bridge to a four-way intersection on the way to the vineyard. ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/))
