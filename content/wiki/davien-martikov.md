## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Master of the Wizard of Wines and a leader of the Keepers of the Feather, father of Irwin at the Blue Water Inn. He led us down a trapdoor near the vineyard, told us of his son Elrich, who died marching on the castle, and of the three gems that make his vines grow ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/), [Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/), [Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)). His younger son Elvier joined Doru's assault too ([Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)). When we brought back the druids' gem he gave us an obsidian whistle and told us the history of the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)).
