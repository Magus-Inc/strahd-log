## Description

![Rose and Thorn Durst, two ghost children side by side at the manor gate, Thorn hugging a stuffed toy](/images/rose-and-thorn-durst-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Gustav and Elisabeth Durst's little son, Rose's brother. We met them outside the manor, afraid to go in. Thorn thought he heard noises from below, though neither child believed the house had a cellar ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). He was right.

We found his spirit with Rose's behind a padlocked attic door, with a toy chest painted with windmills and two small skeletons: the children had been locked in and left to starve. Rose mended a toy he had broken. Thorn asked if they could come with us, as spirits joined to our bodies, and he joined Olfin ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/)). He was still with us when the house fell, and the next day we buried his remains by the tower in the forest ([Walter, we are coming for you!](/posts/walter-we-are-coming-for-you/), [Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)).
