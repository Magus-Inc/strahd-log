A night hag's heart stone. Victor had a book from Mordenkainen that could carry up to nine creatures into the Ethereal Plane on a night of the full moon, but only with a hag's heart stone, and Stella had seen a night hag near the [refugee camp](/wiki/refugee-camp/) ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)). Victor taught us that a hag gives one up only willingly, in a deal, or has it taken from her body, and we planned to face Morgantha's coven at the windmill ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)). In the fight the hags offered to lend us one, but we refused and fell on them ([The Windmill Heist](/posts/the-windmill-heist/)). With the hag's heart stone Victor carried us into the ethereal realm to rescue Stella ([Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)). It was used up opening the way to the Ethereal Plane.
