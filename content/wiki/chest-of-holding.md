## Description

![The Chest of Holding open on a carriage seat, a starry space inside](/images/chest-of-holding-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Strahd's black carriage carried a puzzle chest. We solved its riddle of animal tiles and found velvet-trimmed gloves, wolf-trimmed cloaks and this chest, which holds far more than its size, up to five hundred pounds ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/), [Velvet, Vintages, and Veiled Intentions](/posts/velvet-vintages-and-veiled-intentions/)). We planned to smuggle the castle's relics out in it ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)), and Argynvost's shrunken skull rode out of the Hall of Bones inside it ([Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)).
