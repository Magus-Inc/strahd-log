## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

One of the Belviews, the mongrelfolk the Abbot keeps at the Abbey of St. Markovia ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).

With Otto he met us at the abbey's north gate, eager for word of their brother Cyrus, whom they believed had gone to Castle Ravenloft to be perfected. Was he strong? Was he beautiful? Shabasta told them he was, and they believed him ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).
