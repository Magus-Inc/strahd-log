## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

The writer of a letter we found hidden behind a painting at Argynvostholt, addressed to Lord Argynvost, Protector of the Balinok Mountains and founder of the Order. The letter praised Argynvost's courage, but feared that Markovia's counsel would lead the Order to ruin. Andral granted that Lord von Zarovich was imperfect, yet wrote that his rule had brought order to a land of chaos and songs of prayer back to halls once given over to madness, and begged Argynvost and his knights to lay down their arms and make peace ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)).
