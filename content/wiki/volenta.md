## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

One of Strahd's brides. She escaped us at Heinrich's shop in Vallaki, leaving mist and a glowing pentagram ([The Quest for the Missing Bones](/posts/the-quest-for-the-missing-bones/)). Ludmila scorned her ([Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)). Her white dress took the spilled wine at Strahd's reception ([Velvet, Vintages, and Veiled Intentions](/posts/velvet-vintages-and-veiled-intentions/)). She held the key to Emil's collar and met us in the flooded torture chamber with a jar of oil. She fought with poisoned daggers and shards of ice, took a winged form and set the water burning, and died pinned to the wall ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/), [The Fall of Volenta Popofsky](/posts/the-fall-of-volenta-popofsky/)).
