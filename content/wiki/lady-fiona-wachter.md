## Description

![Lady Fiona Wachter in a dark gown in her candlelit study, her imp on the chair](/images/lady-fiona-wachter-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The lady of Wachter Haus in Vallaki. Her name first reached us on an invitation to the Dursts' mill anniversary that fell from a coat in Durst Manor ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). She invited Aera to dinner, told us of her faith in Ezra, and gave us poisoned wine and a plan to kill Izek ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/), [Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)). We brought her his head, and she paid us from her armory and read us her family's account of Sergei and Tatyana's wedding ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/)). After that she held the town ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)).

Her mentor was Lysa, a woods-witch who worshipped Mother Night, now known to us as Baba Lasaga ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/), [Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/)). Her daughter Stella was trapped in the spirit realm, and the gallows speaker that haunted Stella killed Lady Fiona's parents. She lowered her binding circle for us, translated the hags' contract and held a circle around them, and dropped her wards so we could cross over and bring Stella back ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/), [The Windmill Heist](/posts/the-windmill-heist/), [Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)). When Argynvost's beacon flared, it found her in tears on her doorstep ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
