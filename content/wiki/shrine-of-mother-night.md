## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A cursed altar at the back of the [werewolf den](/wiki/lake-baratok-cave/), where the pack keeps its prisoners. In it lay a ruby medallion, gems and gold, all under a terrible curse. There we also found Ilya and Zuleika, held in collars of silver thorn ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)).
