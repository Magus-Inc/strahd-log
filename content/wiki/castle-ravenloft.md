*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Strahd's castle of stone stands high above the village of Barovia. The first time we saw it, from a hill on the road, the fog rolled in and hid it while leaving the village plain to see ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)). Later, from the village, we watched a procession of souls climb from the graveyard and sink into the castle from the top ([Rest for the Wicked](/posts/rest-for-the-wicked/)). Its front is guarded by a thousand-foot chasm, crossed only by a drawbridge, and by dragon and gargoyle statues we take for sentries ([Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/), [Shadows and Steel](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).

### As Strahd's guests

Strahd's black carriage first brought us in as guests. Rahadin and Anastrasia met us in the grand hall among the vampire court: Escher, Volenta and Ludmila, and brides who looked plainly unhappy to be there. A servant with his fingers cut off at the first knuckle poured for us. Sent below with mops, Zorpheus and Sasha saw a man like Cyrus in the kitchen and a woman hung upside down in the wine cellar, being drained ([Velvet, Vintages, and Veiled Intentions](/posts/velvet-vintages-and-veiled-intentions/)).

On Strahd's own tour I saw a mannequin dressed as Strahd, hung on pulleys in a second-floor hallway. Lightning struck the south gate tower and put the bridge out of use, so we were locked in a tower guest room with Escher at the door. A map hidden in the clock there put the castle's heart in the north tower, and then sleep took us all ([The Boon, the Bride, and the Beating Heart](/posts/the-boon-the-bride-and-the-beating-heart/)). That night became a nightmare that looped: furniture that fought us, Pidlwick under the bed, a crossing over the rooftops with a black dragon behind us, Varushka's room, the Hall of Bones, and Rahadin's office, where Aera stole the key while Strahd fed. Aera climbed high enough to see the great red crystal heart, and Strahd himself caught her when she nearly fell ([Varushka's Key](/posts/varushkas-key/)). In the morning Rahadin showed us out ([Shadows and Steel](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).

### The break-in

We came back uninvited on a night we watched Strahd ride out on Becephalus. Aera flew the chasm, lifted the alarm horns from the wights on the ramparts, and we lowered the drawbridge and went in by the servants' entrance ([Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/)). Behind a mirror in the servants' rooms a ladder led to a landing over the Heart of Sorrow, which hangs in a hollow tower with a stair that has no railing. Past the kitchen, where Cyrus Belview is the cook, we took Argynvost's skull from over the doors of the Hall of Bones, and the room sealed itself and raised its bones against us ([Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)).

Below that lie the Larders of Ill Omen, with a hall of forty zombies, and the flooded Drowning Cells, where Emil sat in a silver thorn collar ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)). In the torture chamber beneath the balcony we destroyed Volenta and took her key to free him ([The Fall of Volenta Popofsky](/posts/the-fall-of-volenta-popofsky/)). Behind the balcony's velvet curtain is the Brazier Room, whose white flame and seven colored stones carry Strahd and his brides across Barovia. In the catacombs we took the Staff of Kazan from Kazan's tomb and the Spear of the Huntress from King Dostran's, and found a crypt meant for Ireena Kolyana standing empty and swept ([The Master of Runes and the Hellborn's Spear](/posts/the-master-of-runes-and-the-hellborn-s-spear/)). Saint Markovia's tomb gave me the Icon of Dawn's Grace. We found Strahd's own tomb and left it alone, and Rahadin came for us out of the dark with his choir of screams ([The Echoing Choirs of Ravenloft](/posts/the-echoing-choirs-of-ravenloft/)).

We drove Rahadin off, climbed to the Rooms of Weeping and found Gertruda in the King's Bedchamber, under a portrait of Tatyana that could be Ireena. Anastrasia came for her and died there as a great bloated bat; Olfin died too, and I called him back ([The Slipper and the Shadow of the Bat](/posts/the-slipper-and-the-shadow-of-the-bat/)). We left by the brazier, an indigo stone in the flame, and came out in the cemetery of Barovia with Gertruda. Strahd's answer was waiting on the road to Vallaki ([The Shard's Warning and the Abbot's Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)).

### Still in the castle

Strahd, Rahadin (wounded, not destroyed) and Ludmila, the last of the three brides we met. Sasha, who wants Strahd to fall and gave us the servants' password. Cyrus in his kitchen. The Heart of Sorrow, still beating. And Sasha's whisper, "Sixth pedal from the left on the organ," and we have yet to learn what it opens.
