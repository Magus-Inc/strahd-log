## Description

![The false meadow: a vision St. Markovia showed us, with a door out of Barovia standing in the grass](/images/false-meadow-ai.jpg)

*A vision St. Markovia showed us beneath the abbey, with its door out of Barovia. AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Past the spiked gate under the Abbey of St. Markovia, light swallowed us one by one, and we stepped into a sunlit meadow full of birdsong and warmth, with no feel of Barovia anywhere. Each of us saw the life we might have had if we had never entered the mists. Liren and Gumbo saw that their lives had gone on without us, their families alive and well ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).

Then St. Markovia stood among us and told us we had suffered enough, and that there was no shame in leaving. She snapped her fingers and a door stood in the meadow: a new life outside the mists, no more battles, no Ravenloft, everything we had hoped for. Only Aera walked through it. It was a lie. The door threw her into the next room and wounded her mind, and the meadow faded back into stone ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
