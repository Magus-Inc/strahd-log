## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A companion who heals herself of any wound. On our way out of [the vineyard](/wiki/wizard-of-wines/) she had secretly arranged to meet a contact named Kavan, whose reputation Davian does not like. Veyren read druidic runes about Kavan the Blood Drinker, and Mira called him up by dripping her blood. He appeared holding a long wooden spear ([Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)).
