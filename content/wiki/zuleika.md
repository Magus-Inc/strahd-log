A werewolf we found imprisoned with Ilya in the [cave](/wiki/lake-baratok-cave/) at Lake Baratok, wearing a collar of silver thorn made by Baba Lysaga that keeps her from turning into a wolf. She swore to free Emil, the pack's leader, whom Kiril had captured with help from Strahd and Baba Lysaga, and asked for our help. We took an oath to rescue him from the dungeons of Castle Ravenloft, and in return she gave Olfin the Symbol of Ravenkind ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)).
