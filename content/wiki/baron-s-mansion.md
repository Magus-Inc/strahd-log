*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The Baron of Vallaki's home. We first came here to reach his library and learn the secret of Kasimir's signet ring, and we got in by claiming an appointment. We sat among dusty books in the waiting room while we waited ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). Its attic hides a teleportation circle ([Whispers in the Barons Mansion](/posts/whispers-in-the-barons-mansion/)). In a mirror up there we saw a woman, and later we met her at Wachterhaus: she was Stella ([Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)). The Baron's son Victor runs his teleportation experiments in the house ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)).
