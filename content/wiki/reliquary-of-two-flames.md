## Description

![The palm-sized silver reliquary holding the two flames of Liren and Gumbo, on a cloth beside two old copper coins](/images/reliquary-of-two-flames-ai.jpg)

*A silver reliquary small enough to sit in a palm. AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

All that is left of Liren and Gumbo. When the spirits rose out of the abbey and our two friends began to fade, they became small flames and sank into the remains of the Abbot's first vessel. Aera searched there and found a silver reliquary the size of her palm, shaped like a tiny gothic chapel, with two small silver flames inside that give off no heat: the lingering echoes of the friends Barovia gave back to us for a little while ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).

The flames can turn a stumble aside a couple of times a day, and once, and only once, the reliquary can be crushed to give one of them up so that a fallen companion lives again ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
