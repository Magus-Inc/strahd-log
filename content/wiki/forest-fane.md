## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

An ancient holy place on Yester Hill. When we took the Spear of the Huntress from King Dostron's tomb, we learned that its power had faded, since Kavan and the Devourer corrupted it long ago. Consecrating the Forest Fane again is one way to make it whole ([The Master of Runes and the Hellborn’s Spear](/posts/the-master-of-runes-and-the-hellborn-s-spear/)).
