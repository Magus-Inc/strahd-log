*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Madam Eva's cards sent us here first: a Vistana searching for a mentor, to be found at Saint Markovia's Abbey near the mists ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Ismark had hoped it could shelter Ireena ([Leaving Village Barovia](/posts/leaving-village-barovia/)).

### The Abbot's house

The road up is steep and cold. We found the Belview family, Otto, Zygfrek and Clovin, warped by the Abbot's attempts to cure them, and his flesh golem, which we gave a crown of flowers. Ezmerelda's wagon was hidden nearby, and her séance told us of a silver statuette in Castle Ravenloft that could cure the Abbot. The Abbot was making Vasilka, a bride for Strahd ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)). We came back with the Icon of Dawn's Grace and found him in the asylum, a lightless hall of cells, scolding Vasilka. Clovin gave Olfin and Aera bone wings from the tower for a kind lie about Cyrus ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).

### The Abbot redeemed

The Abbot became Ethereal, a winged celestial, and the golem took our side. When he fell he rose again as the Fallen Star, and Olfin died striking the last blow before I called him back. Through the Icon we found the Abbot weeping in a void of mist, and Saint Markovia herself forgave him. He lay asleep on the abbey floor as a white-winged youth ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/), [The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)).

That night fog rose from the courtyard well, and Liren and Gumbo came back to us. Behind a red door we had never seen were the Hall of Niches, full of lost things, a false meadow offering a way out of Barovia, and the Abbot's first, forgotten vessel. When it fell, hundreds of trapped spirits went free, and Liren and Gumbo faded into a silver reliquary ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
