## Description

The church of Vallaki, kept by Father Lucian. Ismark first named it to us as a sanctuary for Ireena on the road to the Abbey of Saint Markovia ([Leaving Village Barovia](/posts/leaving-village-barovia/)).

When we brought Ireena there, Henrik was delivering materials and Milivoj was up a stepladder mending a broken chapel window. The church is hallowed ground, protected from the undead (though not from Strahd's living servants) by bones kept in its crypt, and a ritual at each feast renews the ward. The bones had just been stolen. On the altar we found dark hair, a torn piece of gray wool, a rust-stained smear, and boot prints with grass and white petals. Father Lucian offered Ireena shelter there for good ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)). We tracked the bones from Milivoj to Henrik's shop and won them back from Volenta ([The Quest for the Missing Bones](/posts/the-quest-for-the-missing-bones/)), and gave them to Father Lucian to bless and renew the ward. Ireena stayed on under its protection ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)).
