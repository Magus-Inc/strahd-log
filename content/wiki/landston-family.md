## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A family of two parents and five children who went missing from the Village of Barovia. The scouts Kreza and Conga were searching for them. In a clearing with an ancient oak we found seven figures, two larger than the rest, around an armored eighth with white eyes and red mist pouring from his mouth. We fought and killed them all. Surely they were the Landstons, but we believe they were long gone, and we spared them a fate worse than death ([Leaving Village Barovia](/posts/leaving-village-barovia/)).
