## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The book of Brother Merik, once chaplain of the Order of the Silver Dragon. At Argynvostholt the spirit Minodora told us it lies in the west bedchambers and that we must read its prayer aloud ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)). We have yet to learn what the prayer does.
