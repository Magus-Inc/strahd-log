## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A necklace of flowers that [Vasilka](/wiki/vasilka/) made at the Abbey of Saint Markovia as a gift for the creature in the east wing she thinks of as her sibling, since the Abbot made them both. I took it and promised to deliver it, and I gave it to the flesh golem, which stared at us in silence ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).
