## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

A parchment scroll from the hidden shelf behind the painting at Argynvostholt: Andral's letter to Lord Argynvost. It warns that Markovia's counsel will lead the Order into darkness, calls Lord von Zarovich imperfect, as all men are, but credits him with bringing stability to a land of chaos and restoring songs of prayer, and begs the knights to lay down their arms before more bloodshed tears the land apart. It is signed "Faithfully yours, Andral" ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)).
