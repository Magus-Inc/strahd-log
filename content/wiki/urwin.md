## Description

![Urwin Martikov behind the bar of the Blue Water Inn, a raven on the beam above](/images/urwin-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Urwin Martikov keeps the bar at the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki, with his wife Danika, and welcomed us the first night we came to town ([Welcome to Vallaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)). When Izek ransacked the inn looking for Ireena, breaking a window and stealing coins, we found Urwin sweeping up the glass, only relieved that we were safe; we gave gold to help put things right ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)).

Later he called each of us to the bar in turn with a worry: the inn's wine comes from the Wizard of Wines vineyard, the last shipment was sixteen days late, and he feared for his father, Davian Martikov. He sent Muriel Vinshaw to guide us there ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/)). We found Davian driven from the vineyard by the druids and their blights, and learned that father and son both belong to the Keepers of the Feather, who work against Strahd ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/), [Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)).
