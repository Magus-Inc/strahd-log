## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A part of Castle Ravenloft that joins the garrison, the Hall of Bones and, by the South Tower Stair, the main entry. Cyrus drew it for us on his map as thanks for his spoon. The stair climbs past the royal apartments to the witches' coven on the fifth floor, and goes down to the dungeons too ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)).
