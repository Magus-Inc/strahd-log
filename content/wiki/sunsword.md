## Description

![The Sunsword, a blade of blazing sunlight in an amber-lit vault](/images/sunsword-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A blade of pure sunlight, and our great hope against Strahd. In Madam Eva's reading the Bishop card told us the sword of sunlight lies in a pile of treasure beyond a set of amber doors ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Lady Fiona had heard of a glowing sword, perhaps this one, but doubted it exists ([Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)). It is said to have been Strahd's father's weapon, perhaps hidden in the Amber Temple ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)), and by the time we reached the depths of Castle Ravenloft it had become our main aim for the fight with Strahd ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)). When Argynvost blessed one of our weapons, the boon went to Olfin's plain longsword until we find the Sunsword. Next we go to the Amber Temple on Mount Ghakis to look for it ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
