## Description

![King Dostron's ghost rising red over his sarcophagus and the Spear of the Huntress](/images/king-dostron-the-hellborn-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

An ancient king whose resting place is on the Hill of Ravenloft, where a legendary spear waited ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)). His name is part of Strahd's oldest password, "I come in Dostron's memory", which Sasha whispered to us and which only Rahadin is old enough to remember ([The Boon, the Bride, and the Beating Heart](/posts/the-boon-the-bride-and-the-beating-heart/)). His crypt, number 34 in the catacombs beneath Castle Ravenloft, was one of the places we had to reach to break the land's curse ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).

When we found it, a spectral voice warned us to stop. We did not. Olfin and I pried open his lead sarcophagus, cased in gold, and inside lay the Spear of the Huntress, a blood spear of great power ([The Master of Runes and the Hellborn’s Spear](/posts/the-master-of-runes-and-the-hellborn-s-spear/)).
