Clovin Belview's reward for news of his brother. He led us up into the tower above the padlocked shed at the Abbey of St. Markovia, to a wooden stand in the corner covered with black cloth. When he drew the cloth back, two frames of animal bone hung there, like long backbones fitted for a pair of shoulders. One set is a bird's, thick with hundreds of large, downy feathers from a giant eagle; the other is a bat's. They are not cloaks that change shape, as such things usually are: the bones themselves clack and unfold into working wings when their bearer speaks the word, and carry them through the air for a short while, once each day. Olfin took the eagle wings and Aera the bat wings ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).

Olfin flew on the eagle wings against the Fallen Star ([The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)).
