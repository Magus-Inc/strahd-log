## Description

![Father Lucian in his church, holding a reliquary of holy bones](/images/father-lucian-ai.jpg)

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The priest of the Church of Saint Andral in Vallaki. When we asked his help to keep Ireena safe, as Ismark wished, he offered her shelter for good. His church is hallowed ground, guarded against the undead by bones kept in its crypt, but the bones had been stolen, so the ward could not be renewed at the next feast. He suspected Yeska, the boy who works at the church ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).

He believed Milivoj innocent but led us to his home all the same, and he stood at our side when Volenta and two vampires attacked us in Henrik's shop ([The Quest for the Missing Bones](/posts/the-quest-for-the-missing-bones/)). We brought the bones back to him to bless and renew the ward, and Ireena stayed at the church under its protection ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)). When Milivoj and Henrik were condemned to hang, he argued with the guards in the town square ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/)).
