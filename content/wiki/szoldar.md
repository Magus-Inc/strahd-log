## Description

![Szoldar the hunter at the door of his small shack by a misty lake at dusk, wolf pelts drying beside it](/images/szoldar-ai.jpg)

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

One of Vallaki's most skilled hunters. Victor sent us to him as a guide to Lake Baratok, at the mouth of a river west of town, and at dusk we found his small, crudely built shack at the water's edge ([Whispers in the Barons Mansion](/posts/whispers-in-the-barons-mansion/)). He lives there with his brother Yevgeni. They sold Aera a wolf pelt for Shabasta that proved to be glamour weave, warned us of a werewolf pack with a new and reckless alpha, and led us to the Tower of Khazan ([Looking for Khazan's Tower](/posts/looking-for-zoldars-tower/)). He had never heard of the trappers Zollst and Matilda before they turned on us ([The Staff is a lie](/posts/the-staff-is-a-lie/)), and afterward he went back to his hut ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).
