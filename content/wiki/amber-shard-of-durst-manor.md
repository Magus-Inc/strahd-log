## Description

![A raw amber shard glowing on grain in Elisabeth Durst's open silver jewelry box, beside her rolled note](/images/amber-shard-of-durst-manor-ai.jpg)

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

In the Durst Manor ballroom vision, an amber shard at Elisabeth Durst's neck flashed with her fury ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). In the master bedroom Aera opened a silver jewelry box glowing amber and found a shard with a note: Elisabeth had left it to weaken the Beast and soothe its fury ([Deeper into the Manor](/posts/deeper-into-the-manor/)). Aera set it in a heavy wooden chest ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/)), and later I held it up and called Walter's name, with no effect ([Walter, we are coming for you!](/posts/walter-we-are-coming-for-you/)). Argynvost asked whether any of us carries an amber shard. Aera does, and he warned her to beware how fiercely she gazes into its abyss ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)). It is not our only shard: Aera also carries the [Amber Shard of Seriach](/wiki/amber-shard-of-seriach/) we took from Izek.
