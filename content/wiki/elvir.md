## Description

![Elvir Martikov with a torch among the rebels marching up a misty road to Castle Ravenloft, a raven above him](/images/elvir-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The younger son of [Davian Martikov](/wiki/davian-martikov/) of the Wizard of Wines. Three months before we came to the vineyard he joined Doru's march on Castle Ravenloft from Barovia, against his father's wishes, for the Keepers of the Feather do not fight openly. He died in the castle, and his death shattered the family ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/), [Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)). He was betrothed to [Muriel Vinshaw](/wiki/muriel-vinshaw/), and it was Elvir who gave her the wereraven's curse; he never came back to her after the attack ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
