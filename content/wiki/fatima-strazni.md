## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The mother of Izek and Ireena Strazni. In the Baron's book of Vallaki's births and deaths I found that her daughter Ireena died more than eighteen years ago at the age of four, presumed devoured by wolves. Fatima and her husband Grygori died soon after, and their deaths are marked as suicide by hanging ([Whispers in the Barons Mansion](/posts/whispers-in-the-barons-mansion/)).
