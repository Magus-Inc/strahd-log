## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Madam Eva's cards spoke of a den of wolves in the hills above a mountain lake, with a treasure that belongs to Mother Night ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Ezmerelda's séance pointed us there ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).

Ezmerelda hid us in a hollow near the mouth and lit a fire that gave no smoke. The guards caught Aera, and five werewolves and their wolves came at us; fire carried the night ([Echoes of the Black Terro](/posts/echoes-of-the-black-terro/)). Inside were the Shrine of Mother Night with its cursed ruby medallion, and Ilya and Zuleika in collars of silver thorn. They told us how Emil was taken to Ravenloft's dungeons, and Zuleika gave Olfin the Symbol of Ravenkind when we swore to free him ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)).
