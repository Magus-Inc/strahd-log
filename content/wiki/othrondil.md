## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

The Forest of Twilight, home of the dusk elves before they came to this valley, ruled by a council of princes led by Erevan Löwenhart. King Barov claimed it as part of old Zarovia and demanded the elves' fealty. When Erevan refused, Barov's armies, with the traitor Rahadin among them, laid it waste swiftly and brutally. Kasimir told us all this in the Vistani camp by Vallaki, nearly five centuries after it happened ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)).
