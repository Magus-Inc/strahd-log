## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The crypts beneath Castle Ravenloft, and the main goal of our heist. Van Richten's maps put King Dostron in Crypt 34 and Saint Markovia in Crypt 40, and both held things we needed to break the land's curse ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)). In Crypt 15, the tomb of the archmage Khazan, Olfin lifted a heavy portcullis and we solved a puzzle of runes to win the Staff of Khazan. Crypt 34 gave up the Spear of the Huntress, and Crypt 18 waits swept and empty for Ireena ([The Master of Runes and the Hellborn’s Spear](/posts/the-master-of-runes-and-the-hellborn-s-spear/)). In Crypt 40 I found the Icon of Dawn's Grace in Saint Markovia's robes, Crypts 37 and 31 held spell scrolls, and Rahadin attacked us down there ([The Echoing Choirs of Ravenloft](/posts/the-echoing-choirs-of-ravenloft/)).
