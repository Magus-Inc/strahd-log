## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Fog-wreathed gates flanked by headless statues, on the road toward Castle Ravenloft. A black carriage waited for us there, and inside was a scroll: "It is customary for a lord to provide safe escort and a gift to guests for his home." The gift was Strahd's puzzle chest ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)).
