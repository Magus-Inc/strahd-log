## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A strange locket that gives off a child's laughter. Liren found it in Lady Fiona's armory at Wachterhaus, where we went for our reward after bringing her Izek's head ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/)). Last seen with Liren.

## What it does

* **Rare wondrous item.** It needs attunement.
* **Mirror of Revelation.** As an action, open it and turn the mirror on a creature you can see within 60 feet: it shows that creature's true form through any illusion, transformation or shapeshifting, such as a vampire's human guise or a werewolf in hiding.
* **Laugh in the Face of Danger.** Once per dawn, when you save against being frightened, you can laugh instead of rolling and are immune to fear until the end of your next turn.
* **Echoing Cackle.** When you land a critical hit, the locket laughs: creatures you choose within 30 feet make a DC 15 Wisdom save or are frightened until the end of your next turn.
* **Curse.** On a natural 1 on an attack, check or save, the locket laughs at you. Roll a d6: on 1-3 you have disadvantage on your next attack or save; on 4-6 you gain temporary hit points equal to your level.
