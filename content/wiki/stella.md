Lady Fiona Wachter's daughter. At Wachterhaus a young woman in a nightgown appeared in the hallway with a hollow stare, the same woman we had seen in the mirror in the Baron's attic. Her brother Nikolai walked her around the grounds while Lady Fiona apologized for the disturbance. She had been ill for six months, ever since she began visiting Victor, the Baron's son, and came home in a terrible state ([Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)).

Victor showed us her spirit in a mirror. He had been working on a teleportation circle to escape the valley; it worked with rabbits, but with Stella it went wrong and trapped her in the spirit realm, where Erasmus believed a gallows speaker haunts her. She told us she had seen a night hag near the refugee camp ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)). Lady Fiona at last told us the truth about the spirit and her daughter ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)).

We went into the spirit realm for her. The boy Erasmus had been helping her hide. As Victor drew his circle, the spirits came to steal her body and the gallows speaker came to claim her and Lady Fiona. We won her back, at the cost of Shabasta's life, and Lady Fiona was grateful ([Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)).
