## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A restless spirit at Argynvostholt. She begged us not to help Strahd and gave us a riddle: "Brother Merik upstairs east galley. Find his book in the west bedchambers. Read aloud the prayer." She spoke too of Saint Markovia and an angel, whom we think is the Abbot of the town to the west ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)).
