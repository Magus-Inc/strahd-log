## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The cairn of Kavan, a place of ancient worship. We went back to it to hear the rest of Kavan's story: how his people worshipped the Seeker, the Weaver and the Huntress, how he went into the Whispering Wall nearly a thousand years ago, and how the mountain folk and the forest folk split from the first folk ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
