## From the adventure log

*Compiled from the session posts. Each line links to the session it comes from.*

* Is building a reanimated corpse needing a willing victim's heart. ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/))

* Deceived Illya. ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/))

* After Aera shot him through the heart he rose as the Fallen Star: lion head, ram body, bat wings, crocodile tail. ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/))
* Was killed by Olfin's smite and redeemed in the void with St. Markovia's help. ([The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/))

* Had collected a lost item from everyone who travelled through Barovia. ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/))
