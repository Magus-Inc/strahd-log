## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Rudolph van Richten, a vampire hunter whose child a vampire murdered. We found his chest at the ancient oak and read his study of werewolves at Tser Pool ([We meet again for the first time...](/posts/we-meet-again-for-the-first-time/), [Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). In Vallaki he went as Rictavio, a showman with a carnival wagon ([Welcome to Vellaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)). His journal in the Tower of Kazan gave him away ([The Staff is a lie](/posts/the-staff-is-a-lie/)). He told us his story: Doru blackmailed him into leading the revolt on the castle, it was betrayed, and he fled. He had rescued Arabelle from Escher and hidden her in his wagon. He gave me the Talisman of Echoes ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)). His castle maps made our raid possible ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).
