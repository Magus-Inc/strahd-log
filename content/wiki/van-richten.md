## Description

![Van Richten as Rictavio, a colourful half-elf showman in a big hat beside his yellow wagon](/images/van-richten-ai.jpg)

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Rudolph van Richten, a vampire hunter whose child a vampire murdered. We found his chest at the ancient oak and read his study of werewolves at Tser Pool ([We meet again for the first time...](/posts/we-meet-again-for-the-first-time/), [Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). In Vallaki he went as Rictavio, a showman with a carnival wagon ([Welcome to Vallaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)). His journal in the Tower of Khazan gave him away ([The Staff is a lie](/posts/the-staff-is-a-lie/)). He told us his story. He came to Barovia hoping to destroy Strahd alone, in his coffin while he slept, but when an undead dire wolf attacked three Barovian children, Doru watched him fight and learned who he was. Doru blackmailed him into leading the revolt on the [castle](/wiki/castle-ravenloft/), and Escher betrayed it. Van Richten knew the fight could not be won and fled. On his way to warn the villagers, Madam Eva stopped him: "The message you bring shall be borne by another. Should you return to the village while light lingers in the valley, you shall surely meet your grave." He turned back for Lake Baratok instead, weeping for the sons and daughters of Barovia. He had rescued Arabelle from Escher and hidden her in his wagon. He gave me the Talisman of Echoes ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)). His castle maps made our raid possible ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).

The whole of what he told us in Vallaki is on its own page, [Van Richten’s Story](/wiki/van-richtens-story/) ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).
