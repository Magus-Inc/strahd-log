## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

An elder of the Vistani. He welcomed us to the camp at Tser Pool and told the first story at the fire: his people helped a wounded prince home, and the prince promised them safe passage in his land before a curse turned him into a tyrant. They need heroes to put his soul to rest. Later he told us Arturi's true story, which began twenty years ago ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Near the Tser Pool Crossroads we met his caravan again. He knew we had laid Varushka's spirit to rest, gave Aera a Clairvoyance Stone, and showed us a stone crest with a three-pointed star that he must deliver to Lady Fiona himself ([Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/)).
