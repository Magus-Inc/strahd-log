## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A river near Vallaki. A dusk elf found Yann's severed head near the camp, toward the Luna River, and we read footprints along its bank ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). Van Richten saw a Vistana bring a wriggling sack to the golden-haired vampire spawn Escher on its bank. He attacked, killed the Vistana and scarred Escher, and in the sack was Arabelle ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).
