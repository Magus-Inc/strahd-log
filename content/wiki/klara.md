*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The Durst children's nursemaid. The first we saw of her was a piece of the children's needlework, a boy and a girl holding the hands of a young woman, stitched "FOR MISS KLARA", with her face slashed out. Lace sewn with a "K" lay hidden in the pantry beside a note from "G.", and in the office we found her letter asking Mr. and Mrs. Durst for leave because of her "condition", promising a friend to watch the children and looking forward to "bringing another member of that family into this world". When we played the Waltz for Kara, her ghost danced with Gustav Durst until Elisabeth's fury scattered them all ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)).

On the third floor Aera saw her in a mirror, horribly disfigured, as if she had been tortured and cut to pieces. She answered our questions and told us the gift for the beast was on that floor ([Deeper into the Manor](/posts/deeper-into-the-manor/)). Rose later told us Klara was not the one who looked after the baby Walter, and we came to believe Walter was Klara's own son by Gustav ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/)).
