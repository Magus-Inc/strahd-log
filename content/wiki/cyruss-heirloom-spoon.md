## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Cyrus Belview's favorite cooking spoon, which he asked us to fetch from the ossuary near [the castle](/wiki/castle-ravenloft/) larder ([Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)). The first time we brought him the wrong one, an ordinary wooden spoon from the Bone Hole. His heirloom, he told us, is "bigger than normal" and quite "lonely." Olfin grabbed the real spoon from a hall guarded by forty zombies, and Cyrus thanked us with a map of the castle ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)).
