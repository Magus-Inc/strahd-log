Aera's pseudodragon companion. Aera found her when the party first explored Argynvostholt ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/), 3 June 2025).

## What she is

*Ryan shared Zorya's stat block on 27 May 2025. In short:*

* **Tiny undead, neutral good.** AC 13, 7 hit points, walks 15 feet and hovers in flight at 60 feet.
* **Hard to hurt.** Immune to most elemental damage, poison and nonmagical weapons, and to nearly every condition; advantage on saves against magic.
* **A ghost.** She drifts through creatures and objects, and once a day, instead of dropping to 0 hit points, she slips into the Ethereal Plane and returns a minute later.
* **Senses and speech.** Keen senses (Perception +3, Stealth +4, darkvision, short blindsight). She understands Common and Draconic but can't speak; she sends simple ideas, feelings and images telepathically within 100 feet.
* **Silver fire.** Her breath is a 10-foot cone of icy silver fire (2d4 cold, Constitution save halves) that even works on cold-immune incorporeal creatures.
* **Misty Step** (reaction): when she or someone beside her is attacked, she teleports, taking one willing creature along, who takes half damage from that attack.

## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

When we first explored Argynvostholt, a pseudodragon found Aera and began to follow her, drawn to her somehow ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)). Zora has been with her ever since. In the chaos of the fight with Rahadin in Castle Ravenloft, Aera sent her to scout for Gertruda ([The Echoing Choirs of Ravenloft](/posts/the-echoing-choirs-of-ravenloft/)).

Back at Argynvostholt with the dragon's skull, she flew to Aera and tried to shape a skull with her claws, but none of us could follow her gestures and squeaks, not even Crusty, who speaks Draconic. Then, while Hornguard held forth at us in the cemetery mausoleum, she flew in and harried his face, and Aera climbed the wall and set the skull on its mount. Because of her, we never had to fight him at all ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
