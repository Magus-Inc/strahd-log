*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The toymaker of Vallaki. Madam Eva sent Aera with ten gold to buy a toy at his shop for her granddaughter Arabelle ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Among his cluttered shelves we found a doll that looked eerily like Ireena, and he whispered to us of a clockwork man inside Castle Ravenloft ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)).

When a doll went missing, Izek set his shop on fire. We found Blinsky unconscious in the burning store, and Shabasta rallied a crowd to put out the flames and demand justice from the Baron. Blinsky told us he had lately sold a noisemaker toy to Henrik. Shabasta then put on Blinsky's likeness to send the crowd home and collect money for the repairs ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)).
