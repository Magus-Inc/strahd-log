## What it does

*Ryan shared the Keeper's Whistle rules on 22 April 2025. In short:*

* **Once a day**, blowing it calls the nearest wereraven agent of the Keepers of the Feather within five miles, if there is one. The agent flies in, in raven form, which can take a while.
* **One task, within reason:**
  * scout a nearby place from a safe distance and report numbers, traps or layout;
  * carry an item or a spoken or written message to someone in Barovia it can find;
  * fetch a small, light item that is easy to find, buy or pick up nearby;
  * distract nearby enemies, for example with its mimicry, without risking being grabbed;
  * tail a person or watch a place discreetly, without going inside, and report back after an agreed time.
* The agent leaves once the task is done. It is wary of anyone who might carry silver weapons or spells.
