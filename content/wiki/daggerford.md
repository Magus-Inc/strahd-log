*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A town on the Sword Coast, where our adventure started and our party first met. On a dark and gloomy evening each of us came looking for shelter, and the only place left was the straw-covered loft of a barn. We traded introductions there by a magical light. Then the mists took us, and when I looked back there was no path to show the way home ([Session Zero](/posts/session-zero/)). From the balcony of Durst Manor we could see that Daggerford is nowhere near. There is only endless mist beyond the grounds ([Deeper into the Manor](/posts/deeper-into-the-manor/)).
