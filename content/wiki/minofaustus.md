A traveling merchant whose wagon is a magical market full of rare treasures. We met him on the way back to the Wizard of Wines. He warned us not to haggle too hard, and not to steal, and his strangest offer was a discount if we would take Zorpheus off his hands; it seems our rogue was once a thief. Gumbo paid gold and took Zorpheus as his servant, with a cloak of elvenkind; Liren and I got gleaming half plate; and Aera traded for a Shades Ring, owing him another five hundred gold with interest ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)). Oogway later tried to buy a cloak of invincibility from him, could not, and settled for healing potions ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)).
