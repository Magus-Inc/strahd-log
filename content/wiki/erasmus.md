## Description

![The faint spirit of a boy at a frosted inn window in Vallaki](/images/erasmus-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The spirit of a fourteen-year-old boy. He first reached us at an inn in Vallaki, when words appeared on a frosted window: "Help Victor", and his own name ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/)). At the Burgomaster's mansion we learned that he and Stella doubt Victor, and that Erasmus believes a gallows speaker haunts Stella in the spirit realm near Vallaki; he told us what van Richten's guide says of the creature ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)).

When we crossed over to find Stella, boyish laughter came out of the mist and his spectral silhouette appeared. He had been helping her hide. He warned us that the spirits knew we were there, that the gallows speaker waited outside for the ward to fall, and that nothing we had would slide past its defenses, and he told us to strike any spirit that came near Stella ([Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)).
