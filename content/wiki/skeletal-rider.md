## Description

![A skeletal rider in chain mail on a misty road, holding a lantern that gives no light](/images/skeletal-rider-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A skeleton on a war horse that rides the roads in the mists. We first met it on the path out of the Vistani camp at Tser Pool, coming out of the mists, and wondered if it was the poor soul we had found dead by the roadside, caught in the loop of the mists ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). We saw it again soon after, in chain mail with a rusty lantern that sheds no light. It paid us no mind and rode south along an old hunting path. We followed for over half an hour before deciding nothing good could come of it, and turned back to the main road ([Skeletal Rider](/posts/skeletal-rider/)).

Much later, on the road east, a skeletal rider answered our questions, and we came to believe it was Prince Sergei von Zarovich ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)). Whether it was the same rider we saw before, we cannot say.
