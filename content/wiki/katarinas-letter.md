## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A farewell letter from Katarina, found near the ancient oak carved with "K & V" on the Old Windmill Road. "My heart aches as I pen these words," she wrote, "knowing they may be the last I ever write you." We learned that Katarina is Madam Eva, the only one Strahd spared when he became a vampire ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)).
