## Description

![Father Donavich praying in his church beside the latched trapdoor](/images/father-donavich-ai.jpg)

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The priest of the [church](/wiki/barovia-village-church/) in the village of Barovia, and Doru's father. Doru gathered a party to clear the [castle](/wiki/castle-ravenloft/) on the hill and never came back; then an elf came to the village saying he had woken Strahd ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)).

We found Father Donavich in his church, out of sorts and low. He had not been himself since he lost his Bronze Sunburst, the holy symbol of the Morning Lord. Doru had come home a vampire and taken it, and his father had trapped him beneath the church. He gave us the key to the trap door, and below we fought Doru and staked him ([Rest for the Wicked](/posts/rest-for-the-wicked/)). Schlopkht brought him back the sunburst and spared him the part about his son. He performed the burial rites for Ismark's father, praying to the Morninglord, and when I spoke with him we found we serve the same god under different names. He blessed me with the strength of a new dawn for three mornings ([Leaving Village Barovia](/posts/leaving-village-barovia/)).
