*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A visitor at the Vistani camp at Tser Pool, though he looked Vistani himself; it had been a while, he said, since he sat at a Vistani fire. He asked after Rudolph van Richten and claimed to know him. He is, I gather, impressively hard to kill. He told a tale of a wise fox who could not cure the sparrows' child, whose kit the grieving sparrows stole and gave to the wolf, and who helped the rats hunt the sparrows down, so the whole flock suffered for the crime of six. Then he walked out of the camp into the darkness, and no one in the camp was troubled by it: he keeps away from the camp, it seems, because he is chased by creatures that would hurt the innocent. Olfin and Weeble crept after him and found him up a tree, surrounded by twelve zombies. Stanimir later told us his true story, which began twenty years ago and is the fox's story: Arturi searches for van Richten to learn whether his curse can be lifted ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)).
