*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Master of Durst Manor, husband of Elisabeth and father of Rose and Thorn. The children told us he was up on the third floor ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). An invitation in the coat closet bore his name and his wife's, for a dinner marking a year of the Durst Mill. The house soon gave away his secret: lace sewn with a "K" and a note signed "G." hidden in the pantry, the nursemaid Klara's letter asking leave to have a child, and, when we played the Waltz for Kara, his ghost dancing with Klara while Elisabeth watched in fury ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). Strahd's letter to Elisabeth put it plainly: her husband took solace with another woman and fathered a son, the baby Walter ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/)).

Below the house what was left of him came for us. A great worm dropped from a room of gnawed bones, and it looked to be his remains; it knocked Schlopkht out before Aera shot it through the beak. Further on, a boneless thing in a footlocker that looked like him wrapped itself around Weeble until Aera cut it down ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/), [Walter, we are coming for you!](/posts/walter-we-are-coming-for-you/)).

When Walter fell and the house began to collapse, Gustav's ghost blocked the stairs and told us we must stay to appease Elisabeth. I reminded him that Rose and Thorn were with us, and that this was his last chance to be a good father and give his children the peace they deserved. He let us pass. "Just run!" ([Walter, we are coming for you!](/posts/walter-we-are-coming-for-you/))
