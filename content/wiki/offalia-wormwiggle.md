## Description

![Two hag sisters, Bella and Offalia, coming down the stair beside the millstone in the windmill](/images/belladonna-sunbane-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The other of [Morgantha](/wiki/morgantha/)'s two daughters, who lived with her at [the windmill](/wiki/the-windmill/) ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)). She and her sister Bella kept the stolen children in the attic ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)), until Morgantha called them down to meet Shabasta and Olfin. Her true name, Offalia Wormwiggle, was on the coven's contract, and Lady Fiona's binding circle held her with the others until we beat them ([The Windmill Heist](/posts/the-windmill-heist/)).
