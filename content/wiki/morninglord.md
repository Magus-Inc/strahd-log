## Description

![The Morninglord's gold sunrise emblem against a dawn breaking through the mists](/images/morninglord-ai.jpg)

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The god of the dawn, whom the people of Barovia call the Morninglord or the Morning Lord, and whom I serve as Lathander. In Durst Manor I set a book of my own life alight with Lathander's glow to ward off an evil presence ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). In the village of Barovia, Father Donavich had lost his Bronze Sunburst, the Morning Lord's holy symbol, to his son Doru ([Rest for the Wicked](/posts/rest-for-the-wicked/)). When he prayed to the Morninglord at the burial of Ismark's father, I wondered whether this was Lathander, the Lord of Birth and Renewal; when we spoke, we agreed we serve the same god under different names, and he blessed me with the strength of a new dawn ([Leaving Village Barovia](/posts/leaving-village-barovia/)). By Lathander's grace a scroll beyond my training cured Gumbo of lycanthropy ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)), and at the Abbey of Saint Markovia we found a holy disc engraved with a sun, perhaps his ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).

In Saint Markovia's tomb beneath Castle Ravenloft I found the Icon of Dawn's Grace, which named itself a shard of the divinity of Ethereal, an angelic servant of the Morning Lord ([The Echoing Choirs of Ravenloft](/posts/the-echoing-choirs-of-ravenloft/)). That angel was the fallen Abbot himself, and when the Abbot asked how he could be forgiven for all he had done, I told him: "Because that's what the Morning Lord would want. Second chances, rebirth, a new dawn" ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/), [The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)).
