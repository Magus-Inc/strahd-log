## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Holy relics kept in the crypt of St. Andral's Church in Vallaki. They power the ward that protects the church, and a ritual at each feast renews it. They were stolen not long before we came ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)). Milivoj, who took them, confessed he had sold them to Henrik the day before, and we found them in a bag in Henrik's wardrobe ([The Quest for the Missing Bones](/posts/the-quest-for-the-missing-bones/)). We gave them to Father Lucian so he could bless them and renew the ward ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)).
