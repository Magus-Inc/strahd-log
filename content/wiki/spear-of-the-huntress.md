A blood spear of great power. At the Whispering Wall, Kavan told us the Spear of the Huntress must be recovered ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)). Before the heist we believed it held part of the Ladies Three's power, and that it lay in the south crypts of the [castle](/wiki/castle-ravenloft/) ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)). In Crypt 34, the tomb of King Dostron the Hellborn, a spectral voice warned us to stop. Olfin and I pried open the gold-cased lead sarcophagus anyway and took the spear. It can be made whole again by communing with the Spirit of the Huntress, or by consecrating the Forest Fane anew ([The Master of Runes and the Hellborn’s Spear](/posts/the-master-of-runes-and-the-hellborn-s-spear/)).

## What it does

* It is a blood spear. When its wielder drops a creature to 0 hit points with it, the wielder gains 2d6 temporary hit points.
