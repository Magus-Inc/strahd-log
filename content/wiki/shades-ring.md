## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A magic ring that Aera got from Minofaustus at the Whispering Wall. She must repay him with ten percent interest, another 500 gold owed ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).

## What it does

* **Rare ring.** It needs attunement. A slender ring of blackened silver with a pale moonstone.
* **Once a day** it casts invisibility with no concentration. It lasts 1 minute, or until you attack or cast a spell.
* **In dim light or darkness** you have advantage on Stealth checks.
* It shimmers faintly when worn, easy to hide under a glove. The DM put its price at 3,000 to 4,000 gold.
