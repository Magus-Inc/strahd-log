## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

We found this vow inscribed on a table at [Argynvostholt](/wiki/argynvostholt/) ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)). Aera recited it to wake the gargoyle on the fourth floor ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)), and when Argynvost's skull came home we swore the Order's oath ourselves ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).

## The vow

*As it is inscribed on the table:*

> I solemnly vow to uphold the virtues of the Order of the Silver Dragon, with honor as my shield and truth as my guide.

> In the shadow of evil, I shall be the light; in the face of despair, the beacon of hope. No challenge shall shake my resolve; no defeat shall quell my heart. With sword and spirit, I shall stand against darkness and forge a brighter dawn.

> So do I swear in the name of the Silver Dragon, from this to the end of days.
