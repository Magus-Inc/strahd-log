## Description

Argynvostholt is a half-collapsed mansion in a valley, home of the Order of the Silver Dragon and its revenants ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/)). We had already met one of its knights, Sir Godfried William, guarding a bridge after his commander banished him ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)).

### Exploring the ruin

Sir Godfried told us how the Order fell and how Strahd took the skull of Argynvost. He warned us off the commander's chamber, the fourth floor and the phase spiders ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)). Armor from the graveyard turned the ghosts aside. Aera found a pseudodragon, Zorya, who has followed her since. In a cold tower Minodora begged us not to help Strahd. Behind a painting we found the Order's relics, and a letter from Andral begging Argynvost to make peace ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)). On the fourth floor, where Horngaard guards a gargoyle statue, Aera recited the knight's oath and the statue woke and spoke. As we read its words, bringing the skull out of Castle Ravenloft could weaken Strahd's hold on Barovia ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)).

### The skull returns

We came back with the skull at a run, fought a revenant bound in black wire in the foyer, and found Vladimir Hornguard waiting in the cemetery. Shabasta stepped through the air into the mausoleum with Aera, Zorya harried Hornguard's face, and Aera set the skull on its mount without a fight. Argynvost rose as a great silver ghost, Hornguard took his hand and went to his rest, and the knights were freed. We swore the Order's oath. Argynvost now burns as a silver beacon in the tallest tower, for some seven days ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
