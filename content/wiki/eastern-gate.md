## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Vallaki's eastern gate, where refugees sick with longing for dream pastries try to get in. On his last day Izek went there, ordered a dream pastry for himself for the next morning, and sent the two guards away before he went on to the Zarovich gate and the lake ([The Fall of Izek](/posts/the-fall-of-izek/)).
