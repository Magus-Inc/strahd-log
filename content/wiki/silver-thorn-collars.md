## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Collars of silver thorn made by Baba Lysaga. They keep the wearer from turning into a wolf. We found Ilya and Zuleika wearing them as prisoners in the [werewolf den](/wiki/lake-baratok-cave/) ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)). Forcing one open would kill the wearer, and Volenta held the key ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)). We took the key from Volenta's body and opened them ([The Fall of Volenta Popofsky](/posts/the-fall-of-volenta-popofsky/)).
