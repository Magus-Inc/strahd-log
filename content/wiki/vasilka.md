## Description

![Vasilka, a stitched bride in a white gown, tending a mongrelfolk in the asylum](/images/vasilka-ai.jpg)

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The "perfect bride" the Abbot of Saint Markovia built from flesh for Strahd. The Abbot believes Strahd lacks love, and that Vasilka can save him and break the land's curse ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)). He meant to give her Anna Krezkova's heart. She made the crown of flowers we once gave the Abbot's [flesh golem](/wiki/the-flesh-golem/), and it wears it still.

We found her in the [Abbey](/wiki/abbey-of-saint-markovia/)'s asylum, a lightless corridor of cells, being scolded by the Abbot for tending the blighted mongrelfolk on the very day of her "operation", while the flesh golem loomed behind them. He soothed her with talk of the Morning Lord's grace and everyone's proper place. When the fight began and he threw off his robes for his celestial form, Vasilka fled in terror ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)). Beneath the Abbey we later met the vessel he had made before her, and failed ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
