## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A room in Castle Ravenloft with a brazier of white flames, seven colored stones and a frozen hourglass. Each stone cast into the fire carries travelers to a place in Barovia, which explains how Strahd moves about so freely ([The Master of Runes and the Hellborn’s Spear](/posts/the-master-of-runes-and-the-hellborn-s-spear/)). When our business in the castle was done we gathered here and chose to take Gertruda home. We cast an indigo stone into the flames, stepped through one by one, and came out in the cemetery of the Village of Barovia ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)).
