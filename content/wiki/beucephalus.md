*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Strahd's nightmare. Strahd has ridden up to us on a fiery steed more than once: in the middle of our parley with Kavan, and on the road through the forest to Yester Hill ([Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/), [Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)), and we saw him on his nightmare steed in the spirit realm ([Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)). We learned its name the night we came back to steal from Castle Ravenloft: from the edge of the precipice we watched Strahd ride out of the castle on Beucephalus, and with the master gone we crossed the chasm and took the drawbridge ([Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/)).
