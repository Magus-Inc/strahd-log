*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The Abbot's hulking servant at the Abbey of Saint Markovia, stitched together from the dead. When we first came to the Abbey it stood in the east wing with no light in its eyes, and the Abbot told it to take us to Ezmerelda. [Vasilka](/wiki/vasilka/) had made a necklace of flowers for her "sibling" in the east wing, since the Abbot made them both, and I gave the garland to the golem. It stared at us in silence ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)). It wears that garland still, as a crown.

When we came back to stop the Abbot, it loomed behind him and Vasilka in the asylum. Aera used an illusion to make a length of rope look like a new crown of flowers, walked up and gave it to the golem. Olfin asked it, "We're here to destroy your master. Will you help us?" When the Abbot rose up in his celestial form, the golem turned on him, hurling a chalice, candlesticks and a table. ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)). It stayed on our side against the Fallen Star, though its throws mostly missed, and when the Abbot lay sleeping as a white-winged youth, I asked it to look after him ([The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)). We have talked of taking it with us across Barovia.
