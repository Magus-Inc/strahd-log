## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Gemstones Strahd gave us at the Whispering Wall. Crushed, a silver stone carries its owner a few steps in a breath of mist. He offered gray stones too, and each of us chose one. Liren and I took silver ones ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).

## What it does

* Crush one as a bonus action to cast *misty step*.
