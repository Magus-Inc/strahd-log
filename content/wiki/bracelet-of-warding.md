## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Another treasure from the hidden shelf behind the painting at Argynvostholt. It can shield its wearer from harm and bind a friend's protection to them, and its strength returns each dawn ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)).

## What it does

*From the item card shared at the table (3 June 2025). In short:*

* A rare wondrous item with 3 charges.
* As a bonus action, spend 1 charge to cast sanctuary (DC 15), or 2 charges to cast warding bond with a range of 30 feet.
* As a reaction when an enemy attacks a creature in range, cast either one on that creature for 1 extra charge; it then lasts only until the start of your next turn.
* It regains 1 charge each dawn. Spend the last charge and roll a d20: on a 1 it loses its magic.
