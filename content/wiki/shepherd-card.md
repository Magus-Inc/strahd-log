## Description

![The Shepherd card: a woman in white among sheep under a rising sun](/images/shepherd-card-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A single Tarokka card that drifted down out of the void and landed before us when the shadows began to leave the Abbot. It is the four of glyphs, the Shepherd, and it shows a middle-aged woman in a plain white robe among a flock of sheep on a hilltop, under a rising sun. Both are wrong for that card: the Shepherd is usually young, and a rising sun belongs to the Priest. It was a message from St. Markovia, and with her name and this card we called her to the Abbot's side ([The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)).
