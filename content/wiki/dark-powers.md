*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Writing to Elisabeth Durst, he denied that he was a messiah sent to her by the Dark Powers, or that she was the one who brought him to Barovia ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/)). What they are, we do not know.
