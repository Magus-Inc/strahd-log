**Traitor**: Look for a wealthy woman, a staunch ally of the devil. Lock and key with the bones of an ancient enemy.

**Myrmidon**: Look for a den of wolves in the hills overlooking a mountain lake. Treasure belongs to mother night.

**Bishop**: What you seek lies in a pile of treasure, beyond a set of amber doors.

**Mists**: A vistano wanders alone searching for a mentor. Doesn’t stay in one place for long. Seek her out in St. Markova Abbey near the mists

**Marionette**: Look to Great heights, find the beating heart of the castle.

## A later reading (22 April 2025)

*Read at the table during [Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/):*

> The Darklord—the master of shadows, the beast in the labyrinth that tears at his chains.
>
> The Six of Stars, the Evoker—the power you covet, a force untamed by mortal hands, raw and wild with burning fury.
>
> The Artifact—the token you seek, the key to power. Divinity’s heart waits, but where?
>
> The Innocent. I see a maid of raven hair and twilit eyes. She is one way to the token.
>
> But there is another—the Broken One. The path of sacrifice opens another door. The wall that whispers awaits your tribute.
