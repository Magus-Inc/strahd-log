## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A letter Strahd left for us. A black carriage waited on the road outside the abbey, its door open and no driver in sight, and I read the letter aloud: "How delightful. Even the dead return for an encore. I do hope you enjoyed your reunion. There are so few left to come back." ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
