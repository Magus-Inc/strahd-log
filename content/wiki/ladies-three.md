## Description

![A standing stone carved with a three-pointed star and a raven](/images/ladies-three-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Old figures of worship in the valley. We have found their monuments twice: a six-foot stone slab carved with a three-pointed star and a raven, honoring the Seeker and the Ladies Three ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)), and another ancient slab to the Dreamer and the Ladies Three ([Looking for Khazan's Tower](/posts/looking-for-zoldars-tower/)).

The Spear of the Huntress is a shard of their ancient power, and one of the things we must recover before we face the Count ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).
