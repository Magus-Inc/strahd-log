## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Three winter wolf teeth, enchanted by the mountain folk, that can loose a freezing blast or shield the bearer from cold. We found them under [the vineyard](/wiki/wizard-of-wines/) in a box marked with a snowflake, hanging from a rusted iron hook, and used one to blast through brown mold ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/)).

## What it does

* **Breath.** As an action, pierce your stomach with a tooth and breathe a 15-foot cone of freezing wind. Each creature in it makes a DC 12 Dexterity save, taking 18 (4d8) cold damage on a failure or half as much on a success.
* **Ward.** As an action, pierce your bicep with a tooth to gain immunity to cold damage for 1 minute.
* Either use spends the tooth: it melts into frigid mist.
