## Description

![Arrigal, a Vistani man in a tavern corner, folding a secret note](/images/arrigal-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A man who had been tailing us around Vallaki. He showed himself the day we found Arabelle with Rictavio. Rictavio, who is van Richten, gave me the Talisman of Echoes, and when we questioned Yann's severed head with it we judged that Arrigal had no part in taking Arabelle. He and van Richten did not agree on how best to keep her safe ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).

Much later, at the Blue Water Inn, someone slipped Aera a note signed "A": "Spy in castle. Seek a meeting to discuss an opportunity. Find the victim amidst the court. Burn this." We took it as his, and as a lead to a spy inside Castle Ravenloft ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)). At Strahd's court, Sasha's manner seemed to echo it ([Velvet, Vintages, and Veiled Intentions](/posts/velvet-vintages-and-veiled-intentions/)).
