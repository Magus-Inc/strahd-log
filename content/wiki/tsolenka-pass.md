## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

A mountain pass whose gatehouse is barred by a curtain of green flame and guarded by petrified vrock statues. The carved white stone we found behind the painting at Argynvostholt is its key: shown to the curtain, it quiets the flame for a minute, and while it is quiet the vrocks do not wake ([Echoes of the Silver Dragon](/posts/echoes-of-the-silver-dragon/)).
