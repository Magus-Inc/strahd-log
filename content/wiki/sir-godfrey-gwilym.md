## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

An honorable revenant, a knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon banished by his commander, Vladimir Horngaard, who guarded the bridge at Tser Falls and asked whether we served Strahd ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)). At Argynvostholt he told us how the Order fell ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)). When Argynvost rose he was restored to the golden-haired knight he had been, knelt to us, and told us what he knew of Kasimir and the Amber Temple ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
