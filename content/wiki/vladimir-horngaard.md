## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Commander of the Order of the Silver Dragon, who banished Sir Godfrey and holds that Barovia's suffering is penance ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/), [The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)). Sir Godfrey told us Rahadin killed him ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)). Enraged that we broke into Castle Ravenloft, he called his knights home to destroy us and waited for us in the cemetery. Argynvost rose before he could, and Horngaard took the dragon's hand and turned to ash ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).

## From the adventure log

*Compiled from the session posts. Each line links to the session it comes from.*

* Named as Sir Godfrey Gwilym's commander. ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/))
* Was killed by Rahadin; his chamber should be avoided. ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/))
* Guards a gargoyle statue on Argynvostholt's fourth floor and believes Barovia's suffering is penance. ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/))
* Is enraged by the party's assault on Ravenloft, according to a Revenant. ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/))
* Called every knight of the Order back to Argynvostholt by noon, enraged that the party broke into Castle Ravenloft. ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/))
* Waited in the cemetery mausoleum, but Zora's distraction let Aera mount the skull before any fight. ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/))
* Took Argynvost's hand after Shabasta's words and crumbled to ash when the dragon told him his watch had ended. ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/))
