## Description

![Morgantha, an old hag at her pastry cart on a forest road, the windmill behind her](/images/morgantha-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

An old woman who sold dream pastries from a cart, and a hag. We met her at the windmill on the road to Vallaki, where she lived with her daughters Bella and Offalia. The cat ate one of her pies and slept five hours, so Aera bought her cart for five gold to carry him, and on her warning that the Vallaki guards take silver we hid ours in it ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)). The Vallaki guards told us to be careful what we tell her ([Welcome to Vallaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)).

In the [refugee camp](/wiki/refugee-camp/) we met Franz, who had sold his children to her for endless pastries and still hears windmill blades turning in his nightmares. She has not been back since she took them ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)). We stole into the windmill to rescue the children and seize the hags' contract. They offered to lend us a heart stone, then begged and bargained, but we learned their true names, Morgantha Stormreaver, Belladonna Sunbane and Offalia Wormwiggle, and with Lady Fiona and Victor's binding circle we bound and beat them ([The Windmill Heist](/posts/the-windmill-heist/)).
