## Description

![A decrepit wooden windmill with broken sails on a forest road at dusk, a rope hanging from its tower, a hand cart and a stone well by the door](/images/the-windmill-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Morgantha sold us dream pastries at the windmill, where she lives with her daughters Bella and Offalia ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)). At the [refugee camp](/wiki/refugee-camp/) Franz told us he had sold his children for her pies, and hears the windmill's blades in his nightmares. A clue said three black hearts beat as one in the millstone under the windmill ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)).

Shabasta and Olfin talked us inside as would-be partners of the hags, and our rogues climbed the tower on a rope. We freed the children, found the coven's contract, learned their true names (Morgantha, Belladonna Sunbane and Offalia Wormwiggle), and beat them while Lady Fiona and Victor held a binding circle around them ([The Windmill Heist](/posts/the-windmill-heist/)).
