## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

Kasimir's uncle, who led the council of princes that ruled the elves in Othrondil, the Forest of Twilight. He was a master of bladesong who fought in the style of the lion and bore the lion's sigil. When King Barov demanded the elves' fealty and tribute, claiming the old borders of Zarovia that his forebears had ruled, Erevan would not bow, and his refusal began the war. Kasimir, then a mage and scribe at his court, saw Rahadin shatter Erevan's blade and put his family to death, which ended the elves' royal line ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)).
