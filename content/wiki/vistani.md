*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The wandering people of Barovia, who travel in painted wagons and are welcome where others are not. We first saw three of them in the Blood on the Vine Tavern, and Ismark warned us that Strahd's spies include bats, wolves, humans and even Vistani ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)). Not long after, we passed a clearing by a lake with five tents, wagons, horses and one large tent, perhaps their camp ([We meet again for the first time...](/posts/we-meet-again-for-the-first-time/)).

At their camp at Tser Pool, Stanimir welcomed us, fed us and let us sleep and heal, and Madam Eva read our fortunes in her tent ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Their camp near Vallaki seethed with tension when we brought a toy for Arabelle: broken pottery and cracking whips, Alexi begging us to find his missing daughter, and Yann, accused of taking her, dead. There Kasimir told us how the Vistani took in the dusk elves after Strahd crushed their revolt, and have sheltered them from his wrath for five centuries ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). Ezmerelda d'Avenir keeps a purple and gold Vistani wagon by the tower at Lake Baratok ([Looking for Khazan's Tower](/posts/looking-for-zoldars-tower/)). Near the Tser Pool crossroads, on our way back to the castle, we met Stanimir's caravan, with Radu and Elizabella telling stories. We were wary of each other, but civil; Stanimir knew we had laid Varushka's spirit to rest, and gave Aera a Clairvoyance Stone to help us get inside ([Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/)).
