## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Gemstones Strahd gave us at the Whispering Wall. Crushed, a gray stone leads its owner to what they desire. He offered silver stones too, and each of us chose one. Gumbo, Aera and Zorpheus took gray ones ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)). We planned to use them to find what we came for during the heist on Castle Ravenloft ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).
