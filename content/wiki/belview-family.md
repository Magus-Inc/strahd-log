## Description

![Two of the Belviews waiting outside the abbey's north gate for news of Cyrus](/images/belview-family-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini).*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The Abbot's mongrelfolk at the Abbey of St. Markovia: Otto, Zygfrek, Clovin and the rest, each of them misshapen by the Abbot's experiments. Some of them are kept locked in a stone shed in the courtyard and fed cold porridge ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/), [The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).

Cyrus Belview is one of them. We met him, misshapen like the rest, on the larder level of Castle Ravenloft, a small foothold among the castle's household ([Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)). The family believed the Abbot had sent him there to be perfected, and when they asked us how he had been transformed, Shabasta and Olfin let them keep believing it. In thanks Clovin gave us two sets of bone wings ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).
