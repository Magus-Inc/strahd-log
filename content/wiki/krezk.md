## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Krezk is a walled village below the Abbey of Saint Markovia. Baron Dimitri Krezkov and his wife Anna Krezkova lead it ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/), [The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).

On our first visit we learned their grief. A curse of the wolf runs in Dimitri's family, and Anna gathers wolfsbane to hold it back. The Abbot, in the shape of Dimitri's aunt, talked him out of the potion; he turned, and killed his sister that night. The Abbot promised to bring her back if Anna gives up her heart, and their son has run off into the woods ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)).

The Abbot's sign came later: a snow-white feather landing in Krezk, meaning he would take Anna's heart at dusk the next day. Dimitri found us in Vallaki, and Anna met us at the gates, grateful and terrified ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)). We rested at the Burgomaster's house and climbed the winding road to the Abbey ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)). From Krezk to Argynvostholt by the Raven River crossroads is about three hours at a run ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
