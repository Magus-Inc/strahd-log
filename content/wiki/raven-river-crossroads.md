Where the road from Vallaki to Krezk crosses the Raven River on a bridge. We rested there a short while on our way to Krezk, and found a revenant of the Order of the Silver Dragon standing guard at the bridge. He greeted us warmly, but a shadow crossed his face as he warned that dark tidings were on the wind and he might be called from his post sooner than he expected. Commander Vladimir Horngaard was enraged by our raid on Castle Ravenloft, likely told of it by the phantom warriors who serve as his eyes and ears in the spirit world. As we left he bid us a solemn farewell and, almost wistfully, commended us to the Morning Lord's grace ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)).
