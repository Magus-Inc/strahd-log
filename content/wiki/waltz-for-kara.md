## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Sheet music hidden in the harpsichord of Durst Manor's ballroom. A stuck key led us to it. When one of us played the waltz, the ballroom filled with dancing ghosts, among them Gustav Durst and Klara, while Elisabeth watched in a fury until every ghost vanished ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)).
