*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Behind the red door beneath the Abbey of St. Markovia. When the Abbot was redeemed, a pounding began under the abbey in time with the bell, and a mongrelfolk in the courtyard whispered that the Abbot always said never to open the red door. Then there it was, a red door none of us had noticed before, half hidden under fallen leaves in a back corner, over stairs leading down. Aera and Gumbo worked its lock, and fifteen feet of stairs took us down into pitch dark ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).

The walls of the chamber below are lined with dozens of niches, each holding some small thing: a child's shoe, a wedding ring, a soldier's medal, a wooden toy, a broken holy symbol. Each belonged to someone whose soul passed through the abbey. Liren spotted something she had lost on the roads of Barovia, and we understood that the Abbot had been collecting something from each of us too. Shabasta found his second glamour weave there. Then six shadows peeled off the walls and came for our strength ([The Red Door: A Reunion Beneath the Abbey](/posts/the-red-door-a-reunion-beneath-the-abbey/)).
