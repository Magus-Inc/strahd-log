*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The cold cellar level forty feet beneath Castle Ravenloft's kitchens, a damp foyer of rot, stagnant ice-cold water, rotting debris and heaps of blinded beans, and the doorway to the castle's depths. It holds the elevator trap. When we first came down to the larder level, the castle's misshapen servant Cyrus Belview greeted us with weary familiarity and asked us to fetch his favorite cooking spoon; then a crystal dinner bell rang by the elevator shaft and two vampire spawn dropped on us from the ceiling, and Aera and I had to leap again and again to keep our footing on the elevator's moving counterweights ([Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)).

Back in the Larders, our first steps were a comedy of errors. The spoon we brought Cyrus from the Hall of Bones was an ordinary one; his heirloom, he said, is "bigger than normal" and "lonely". It lay at the far end of a corridor of forty motionless zombies, and Olfin dashed in disguised as one of them and snatched it while I pulled him back with a conjured hand. Cyrus traded us a map of the Serpent's Wing for it, and among the blinded beans we found a candle that gives a long night's rest in a short one. From here the road ran down into the flooded Drowning Cells ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)).
