## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Strahd's own tomb in the catacombs of Castle Ravenloft. In our heist plan it was the final target, which should be empty at the new moon, though the crypts' magic might hold him there if we lingered ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)). We found it stained with blood, and we chose not to go in ([The Echoing Choirs of Ravenloft](/posts/the-echoing-choirs-of-ravenloft/)).
