*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Strahd's three vampire brides: Volenta, Ludmilla and Anastrasya. We first crossed Volenta in Henrik's shop in Vallaki, where she escaped us in a cloud of mist ([The Quest for the Missing Bones](/posts/the-quest-for-the-missing-bones/)). At Yester Hill Ludmilla made no secret of her contempt for Volenta, and Strahd toyed with a chess pawn in front of her, to her displeasure ([Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)). At Strahd's dinner Anastrasya greeted us beside Rahadin, Volenta and Ludmilla made up the eerie company, and several women dressed as brides looked plainly uncomfortable ([Velvet, Vintages, and Veiled Intentions](/posts/velvet-vintages-and-veiled-intentions/)). As Strahd's carriage took us out of the [castle](/wiki/castle-ravenloft/), we saw his three brides watching from a window ([Varushka's Key](/posts/varushkas-key/)).

Two of them are dead now. We pinned Volenta to the wall of a flooded dungeon chamber beneath the castle and took the key to free Emil ([The Fall of Volenta Popofsky](/posts/the-fall-of-volenta-popofsky/)), and Anastrasya came for Gertruda in the King's Bedchamber, shed her skin for a giant bat, and I burned her to ash ([The Slipper and the Shadow of the Bat](/posts/the-slipper-and-the-shadow-of-the-bat/)). That leaves Ludmilla.
