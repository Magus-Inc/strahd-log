Baron Dmitri Krezkov, who leads Krezk with his wife Anna. He carries a werewolf's curse handed down the generations, and Anna gathers wolfsbane to hold it back; we don't know which of his children, if any, carry it too. The Abbot, in the shape of Dmitri's aunt, talked him out of the wolfsbane potion, and Dmitri, not knowing it was what kept him himself, stopped taking it, turned, and killed his sister that night. The Abbot then offered to bring her back in exchange for Anna's heart ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)).

When the Abbot sent word he would take Anna's heart at dusk the next day, Dmitri was waiting for us at the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki, desperate and untouched by the merriment around him, and begged us to save her ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/), [The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).
