## Description

![The gallows speaker, a shrouded spectre trailing nooses in the ethereal mist](/images/gallows-speaker-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A spirit of the Ethereal Plane near Vallaki. Erasmus believed it was haunting Stella, trapped there by Victor's spell gone wrong, and told us what van Richten's guide says of such creatures ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)). It was the malevolent spirit behind the deaths of Lady Fiona's parents ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)).

When we went into the spirit realm, Erasmus warned us it was waiting outside the ward for it to drop. It bore us no ill will, it said, but demanded Stella and Lady Fiona. After a long, hard battle we defeated it, but Shabasta died in the fight and none of us had the prayer to bring him back ([Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)).
