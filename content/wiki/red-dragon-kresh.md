## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A bottle of poisoned wine. In the morning Lady Fiona's agent told us about Izek's drinking and gave us the bottle and a sack for his head. The plan was to swap it for the second bottle of his lunch, then ambush him at the lake ([Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)). It worked. At the lake Izek took out a doll of Ireena and drank the poisoned wine ([The Fall of Izek](/posts/the-fall-of-izek/)).
