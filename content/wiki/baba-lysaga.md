A woods-witch of the Berez swamps who worships Mother Night. As a girl, Lady Fiona was lost in the woods, and Baba Lysaga, whom she calls Lysa, rescued her and became her mentor ([Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/), [The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)). With the druids she drove the Martikov family from the Wizard of Wines and stole one of its gems, then helped Ludmilla take the winery ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/), [Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/), [Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)). She empowers Kiril, the Black Terror, and made the silver thorn collars that held Emil and the werewolf prisoners ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/), [Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)).
