## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A Vistani woman we met just outside the tent at the Tser Pool camp, with Arturi Vedonavich. Over a hearty meal she was very interested in our names and our journey, so interested that I feared she was part of the shadow we had been warned about. Around the fire she sang that each raven holds a lost soul, of Luda, of the Morninglord, of raven kind. I could not catch it all, but the key, I think, was a Holy Symbol of Ravenkind ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Long after, on our way from Vallaki to the [castle](/wiki/castle-ravenloft/), we met her again near the Tser Pool Crossroads, travelling in Stanimir's caravan with Radu, telling stories. We were wary of each other, but civil ([Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/)).
