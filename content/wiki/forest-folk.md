## Description

![Ragged druids of the forest folk in a misty wood, one holding a raven, twig blights at their feet and another druid watching from a branch](/images/forest-folk-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

Druids of the Svalich Woods, a loose society of hermits who shun the towns. They hold Yester Hill, at the southwest edge of the valley, sacred, and they worship Strahd as a god for his power over the land and the weather. One of them watched us from a tree on the road. Lady Wachter first learned of them as a student of Lysa ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)). A mountain woman warned us to keep clear of them, and we heard that the mountain folk and the forest folk split long ago, the one worshipping ancient goddesses and the other dark powers ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/), [The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).

With their blights, and with Baba Lysaga, they drove the Martikovs from the Wizard of Wines. They worship Lorgoth, caught ravens as spies, and took the vineyard's last gem to call up Winter Splinter on Yester Hill ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/), [Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)). We found thirteen of them chanting around the great statue on the hill, and destroyed Winter Splinter ([Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/), [The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
