## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A flint and steel that makes a fire with no smoke. Before we went into the [werewolf den](/wiki/lake-baratok-cave/), Ezmerelda led us to a hidden hollow near its mouth and lit a fire with it, so no one would see our camp ([Echoes of the Black Terror](/posts/echoes-of-the-black-terro/)).
