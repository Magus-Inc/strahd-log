## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A cluttered toy shop in Vallaki. Madam Eva gave Aera ten gold pieces to buy something there for her grandchild, Arabelle ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). On its shelves was a doll that looked eerily like Ireena, and Aera stole it. Blinsky also whispered to us of a clockwork man inside Castle Ravenloft ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). Later Izek set the shop on fire ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)).
