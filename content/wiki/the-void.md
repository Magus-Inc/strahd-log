*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Where the Icon of Dawn's Grace took us when the Fallen Star lay beaten. We blinked, and the abbey was gone. In its place a dark, endless emptiness stretched in every direction, its floor a softly rolling mist, its silence broken only by sobbing somewhere far off. An orb of golden flame hung quietly at our side, the shard of divinity from the icon, and it followed us and spoke into our minds ([The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)).

The sobbing came from the Abbot, as he must once have been: a gaunt, pale young man in worn-out monk's robes, his eyes bound by a thin band of mist, his cheeks red from weeping. His great wings lay limp and faded beside him, chained to the ground with shackles of mist, and shadow hung about him like a shroud. He rocked with his knees to his chest, muttering over and over that he had failed. When St. Markovia came and forgave him and he took the light, the void burst away in a flash of white and we stood in the abbey again ([The Fallen Star: Ape, Sacrifice and Redemption](/posts/the-fallen-star-ape-sacrifice-and-redemption/)).
