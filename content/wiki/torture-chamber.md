## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A flooded chamber of racks and iron maidens beneath Castle Ravenloft, under a velvet-draped balcony. In the Drowning Cells Olfin stepped on a hidden plate, and air and water burst around him. The rope in my hands went slack: he had been carried off into an iron maiden in the torture chamber ([Depths of Ravenloft](/posts/depths-of-ravenloft/)). We killed Volenta Popofsky here ([The Fall of Volenta Popofsky](/posts/the-fall-of-volenta-popofsky/)).
