## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

An ancient goddess, one of the Ladies Three, whose sign is a three-pointed star. As we neared [the vineyard](/wiki/wizard-of-wines/) we found an ancient monument to her, a slab with a pristine three-pointed star ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/)). Kavan told us his people worshipped the Seeker, the Weaver and the Huntress ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
