## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A wizard whose book Victor owns. With a hag's heart stone, the book can carry up to nine creatures into the Ethereal Plane on a night of the full moon, and Victor meant to use it to reach the Ethereal Plane for a spell to help Lady Fiona escape ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)). We have never met Mordenkainen.
