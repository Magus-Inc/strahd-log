## Description

![The Whispering Wall across misty mountains, Strahd waiting at its western door](/images/whispering-wall-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Strahd wagered with us on the way to Yester Hill: win, and meet him at the western foot of the wall for a gift and a secret ([Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)). He kept his word. The wall is an ancient tomb that makes Barovia's mist and shows those who enter what they most desire. Zorpheus heard the prophecy confirmed, I learned my reborn nature ties me to a line of elven kings, and Gumbo heard a crowd cheer. Each of us heard a woman whisper, "The way back comes but once." Ireena came out weeping and kept her vision to herself. Strahd gave us gray and silver stones and showed us the Twilight Crest ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)). A later reading said the Whispering Wall awaits a tribute ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)).
