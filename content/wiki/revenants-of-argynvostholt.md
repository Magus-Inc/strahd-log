## Description

![A revenant knight bound in black spiked wire in the foyer of Argynvostholt](/images/revenants-of-argynvostholt-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The dead knights of the Order of the Silver Dragon, held in their corpses by Vladimir Horngaard's hatred of Strahd. Not all of them are our enemies. One stood guard at the bridge over the Raven River, greeted us warmly and warned us, a shadow crossing his face, that Horngaard was enraged by our raid on Castle Ravenloft and had his phantom warriors watching where we went. He sent us off with the Morning Lord's blessing ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)).

Another, Harkus Grishenko, found us in Krezk with word that Horngaard had called them all home to Argynvostholt. In the foyer there one barred our way: gaunt and ashen in patterned chainmail, an ancient rusted longsword in hand, red eyes glinting solemnly behind a battered helm, head bowed as if in sorrow and shame. Strands of black, spiked wire wound around its limbs and armor and up to the hilt of its sword, and crawled on its flesh like a puppet's strings. It told us to meet our deaths with honor, as its general had commanded, and attacked. When Horngaard at last found rest, the revenants dissolved into mist and the spirits of the Order were freed ([The Beacon of Argynvostholt](/posts/the-beacon-of-argynvostholt/)).
