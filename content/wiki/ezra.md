## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Goddess of the mists, who teaches endurance. Lady Fiona follows her. She prays and hears Ezra answer, though I suspect the voice may be an imposter ([Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)). She never heard the voice of Mother Night, whom her mentor Lysa worshipped, and took up the faith of Ezra soon after she came back to Vallaki ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)).
