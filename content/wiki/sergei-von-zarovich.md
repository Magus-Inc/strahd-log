Prince Sergei von Zarovich, Strahd's brother. Lady Fiona read to us from her family history of his wedding to Tatyana ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/)). On the road east we met a skeletal rider we believe was Sergei, and he answered our questions: he does not work for Strahd, knows nothing of the spy in the castle, and is no part of breaking the curse. He told us Strahd murdered him and that Tatyana took her own life, which set Strahd's curse in motion, and that Strahd now seeks Ireena, believing she is Tatyana reborn ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)).

When we planned our way into Castle Ravenloft, we learned his tomb in the crypts is well lit, barred by a portcullis, and glows like an unnatural beacon beside Strahd's own ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).
