## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

People of the mountains who worship the ancient goddesses of the valley, not Strahd as the forest folk do. A revenant at a bridge told us so, and a mountain woman warned us away from the forest folk ([Act 3: The Broken Land](/posts/act-3-the-broken-land/)). Their magic was in the three winter wolf teeth we found in a box under [the vineyard](/wiki/wizard-of-wines/) ([Wizard of Wines vineyard](/posts/wizard-of-wines-vineyard/)). Kavan told us they and the forest folk split from the first folk when their loyalties divided. We need their help to reach Zoldoph ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
