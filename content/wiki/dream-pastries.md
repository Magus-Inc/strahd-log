Pies sold by Morgantha, who lives at the old windmill with her daughters Bella and Offalia. Our cat ate one and slept for five hours, so Aera bought Morgantha's cart for five gold to carry him. One of the pies put a wolf out of a fight ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/)). At Vallaki a quarter of the refugees outside the walls are addicted to them, and Schlopkht tried to get Ireena to feed the pies to the refugees to ease their minds ([Welcome to Vallaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)).
