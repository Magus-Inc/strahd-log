## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

Kasimir's people. They lived in Othrondil, the Forest of Twilight, under a council of princes, until King Barov conquered them. When Barov died and Strahd came to power they rose in rebellion, but Strahd crushed the revolt in a matter of days and slaughtered them until fewer than a hundred were left. He left the survivors to the Vistani, who took them into their caravans and led them to this valley. Within a year Strahd had beaten the last of his enemies and claimed the valley as well, naming it Barovia, and the elves found themselves trapped in the heart of their conqueror's realm. By then they had made a home here, so they stayed, hoping the Vistani's kindness would keep them safe, and here they have kept their silent vigil for nearly five centuries ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)).
