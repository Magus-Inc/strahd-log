## Description

![Winter Splinter, a giant tree blight, striding out of a burning forest toward the vineyard](/images/winter-splinter-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A giant twig blight the druids of the forest folk meant to call up on Yester Hill. At the Wizard of Wines the druids, who worship Lorgoth, warned us that Winter Splinter was coming to tear down everything in its path, and in their room grew a tree like the one on Yester Hill, the shape of the thing to come. We were urged to reach the hill before dawn to recover the gem, and we learned that radiant magic slows and burns the tree ([Finishing up at the vineyard](/posts/finishing-up-at-the-vineyard/)).

Strahd met us in the forest, openly excited about it, told us what it was and wagered a gift and a secret on our success. On the summit thirteen druids chanted in a ring around a fifty-foot statue while Ludmilla watched, and we slipped past her to break the ritual ([Stop Winter Splinter](/posts/stop-winter-splinter/)). We destroyed Winter Splinter and took its gemheart, but its bursting death took Veyren's life ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)).
