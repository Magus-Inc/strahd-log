## Description

![The Baron of Vallaki in a fur-trimmed coat and chain of office, leading a procession with Izek through the silent town square](/images/baron-of-vallaki-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Baron Vallakovich, the ruler of Vallaki. When we reached the town, the refugees from Barovia were [camped outside the gate](/wiki/refugee-camp/) and he would not even come out to them. Talk against him made the townsfolk uneasy, and we soon saw why: a procession led by the Baron and his enforcer Izek silenced the whole town, and an old woman whose son Udo sat in prison was put in the stocks ([Welcome to Vallaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)).

He received us in his mansion and told us a malevolent spirit plagues the house and has driven off the staff, though he wanted the women kept from knowing it. He would not let us see his books, only the kitchen ([Whispers in the Barons Mansion](/posts/whispers-in-the-barons-mansion/)). After Izek burned Blinsky's shop, Shabasta led a crowd to his door disguised as Blinsky; when the Baron questioned him and he faltered, the Baron had his guards shoot at him, and made it plain he was still Baron ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)). Lady Fiona told us he took Lukovich the shoemaker and may have executed him, and that Nikolai once cursed him ([Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)). Once Lady Fiona held the town, he and his son Victor still kept to the burgomaster's mansion ([Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)).
