## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Izek's magical demon arm. After we killed him at the lake, Shabasta asked whether it could be joined to him. Liren cut off Izek's right arm with her axe, and with Lathander's help I joined it to Shabasta ([The Fall of Izek](/posts/the-fall-of-izek/)). When we questioned Izek's head with the Talisman of Echoes, he told us what the arm can do: hurl a flame, or loose a volley of fire ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/)).
