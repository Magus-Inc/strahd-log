## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A room of preserved luxury in the Rooms of Weeping at Castle Ravenloft. We learned Gertruda was held there ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)). Following a slipper we had found on the stairs, we climbed up to it, and Aera's picks got us in. Above a blazing hearth hung a portrait of Tatyana, so like Ireena that we all stopped short. In it she wears the silver bracelet of the moon and stars that Olfin once recovered. Anastrasya fought us here ([The Slipper and the Shadow of the Bat](/posts/the-slipper-and-the-shadow-of-the-bat/)).
