## Description

![Clovin Belview carrying bowls of cold porridge to the padlocked shed](/images/clovin-belview-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini). Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

One of the Abbot's mongrelfolk at the Abbey of St. Markovia, and the brother of Cyrus Belview. We first met him on our early visit to the abbey ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)).

When we came back, we found him crossing the courtyard with bowls of cold porridge for the Belviews kept in the padlocked stone shed. He greeted us warmly, but what he wanted was news: had we seen Cyrus since he left the abbey? Was he well? Had he taken to his new form? Olfin told him the same kind tale Shabasta had told at the gate, and Clovin, grateful, kept the promise he had made us. Before taking us to the Abbot he led us up into the tower and gave us two sets of bone wings ([The Abbot Unveiled: Wings, Lies and the Fallen Star](/posts/the-abbot-unveiled-wings-lies-and-the-fallen-star/)).
