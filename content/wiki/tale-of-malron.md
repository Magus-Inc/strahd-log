## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A book I found on the shelves of Durst Manor, telling the story of my own life as best I remember it. I skipped to the end and found a passage about me picking up this very book while an evil presence watched. I lost my composure, set the book alight with Lathander's glow and held it up to ward the evil off. Nothing came of it, except some remarks from my companions about how brave their cleric is ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)).
