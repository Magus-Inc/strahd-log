*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The lady of Durst Manor and, as far as we ever learned, the true monster of the house. Outside, Rose told us her parents were Gustav and Elisabeth and that they were up on the third floor. Inside, when we played the Waltz for Kara, the ballroom filled with ghostly dancers. Gustav danced with the nursemaid Klara while a woman off to one side, surely Elisabeth, stared at them. Her fury burned so hot that the amber shard at her neck glowed and flashed, and every ghost vanished ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)).

In the master bedroom a shape behind the doors turned toward us with an amber glow and was gone. Her silver jewelry box held an amber shard and a note in her hand to Drasha, whom she had chosen to keep the Beast while she was away: show it the shard and speak its name to quiet it, and be out of the house before it wakes at midnight ([Deeper into the Manor](/posts/deeper-into-the-manor/)).

A letter from Strahd in the attic, addressed to his "most pathetic servant", told the rest. She had bled souls on a hidden altar and tortured visitors in her dungeon, hoping he would save her, and her husband's affair, and the son it gave him, had driven her from love to madness. Strahd wanted her just as she was. From that letter we took her for the monster of the house. In an attic mirror Schlopkht saw her reflection and felt a cold touch on his shoulder before he broke the glass ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/)).

When we destroyed Walter at the bottom of her cult's dungeon, her ghost appeared and swore to kill us for destroying her pet. The ground shook and the house sank into the earth behind us as we ran ([Walter, we are coming for you!](/posts/walter-we-are-coming-for-you/)). We have not seen her since.
