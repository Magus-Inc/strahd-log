## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A lake with a tower on its shore, where van Richten hid after he fled Castle Ravenloft, since Madam Eva's warning kept him from Barovia village. He kept his horse Drusilla and his wagon there. A field in the tower blocks all magic, Strahd's scrying with it, but van Richten judged that Strahd knew this and would look there first, so he did not hide Arabelle there ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).
