## Description

![Lake Baratok in the mist, a stone tower on one shore and a wolf at a cave mouth on the far shore](/images/lake-baratok-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, November 2026), drawn from this page and the session logs. Not official art.*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A lake with two places we came to know well. On its shore stands the [Tower of Khazan](/wiki/tower-of-khazan/), where van Richten hid after he fled Castle Ravenloft, since Madam Eva's warning kept him from Barovia village. He kept his horse Drusilla and his wagon there. A field in the tower blocks all magic, Strahd's scrying with it, but van Richten judged that Strahd knew this and would look there first, so he did not hide Arabelle there ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).

By the lake the werewolves keep their [den](/wiki/lake-baratok-cave/), where they held Ilya ([Shadows Over St. Markovia](/posts/shadows-over-st-markovia/)). Ezmerelda led us there; we fought the pack and found the Shrine of Mother Night and their prisoners inside ([Echoes of the Black Terror](/posts/echoes-of-the-black-terro/), [Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)).
