## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A mountain lake with two places we came to know well. On its shore stands the [Tower of Khazan](/wiki/tower-of-khazan/), where van Richten hid after he fled Castle Ravenloft, since Madam Eva's warning kept him from Barovia village. He kept his horse Drusilla and his wagon there. A field in the tower blocks all magic, Strahd's scrying with it, but van Richten judged that Strahd knew this and would look there first, so he did not hide Arabelle there ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).

In the hills above the lake the werewolves keep their [den](/wiki/lake-baratok-cave/), the den of wolves Madam Eva's cards sent us to ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)). Ezmerelda led us there; we fought the pack at its mouth and found the Shrine of Mother Night and their prisoners inside ([Echoes of the Black Terror](/posts/echoes-of-the-black-terro/), [Blood and Oaths Under Moonlight](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)).
