## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

### What we found

The mists brought us from Daggerford to this four-story house, where Rose and Thorn Durst waited outside, afraid to go in ([Session Zero](/posts/session-zero/), [Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). The doors slammed behind us and a rhyme written in blood warned of a beast beneath the house that wakes at midnight ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/)). Room by room the house told its story: stuffed wolves that moved, a nursemaid named Klara and her baby Walter, a waltz that brought the dancing dead, Strahd's letter to Elisabeth Durst, and the children's bones in a locked attic room ([Entering Durst Manor](/posts/entering-durst-manor/), [Deeper into the Manor](/posts/deeper-into-the-manor/), [Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/)).

A hidden stair led down into a cult's dungeon. We fought what was left of Gustav, a mimic and the cult's horrors, and at the bottom destroyed Walter in a mound of flesh. Gustav let us go to give his children peace, and the house fell into the ground behind us ([Finishing the Manor?](/posts/finishing-the-manor/), [Walter, we are coming for you!](/posts/walter-we-are-coming-for-you/)). We buried Rose and Thorn by a nearby tower the next morning ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)).

The Durst Manor is four stories tall and has a light mist around it.

Any attempt to climb the Manor will result in vines attacking and knocking intruders off.
