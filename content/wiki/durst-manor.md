The Durst Manor is four stories tall and has a light mist around it.

Any attempt to climb the Manor will result in vines attacking and knocking intruders off.

## As “Manor”

This is the first location in Barovia the party encountered.

The manor is four stories tall and has a balcony on the third floor.

A boy and a girl were found waiting outside of the manor.
