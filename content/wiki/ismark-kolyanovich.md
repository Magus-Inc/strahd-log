*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

We met Ismark alone in a corner of the Blood on the Vine Tavern. Strahd's forces had besieged the village and his father had died in the attack. He warned us of Strahd's spies and asked our help to see his sister Ireena safe in Vallaki, though Billdrath scorned him as "Ismark the lesser" ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)). After his father's burial he asked us to take Ireena on to the Abbey of Saint Markovia, by way of the Church of Saint Andral, and gave us a map ([Leaving Village Barovia](/posts/leaving-village-barovia/)). When we brought Gertruda home he gave us rest in the burgomaster's mansion, relieved and proud to see how his sister has grown ([The Shard’s Warning and the Abbot’s Madness](/posts/the-shard-s-warning-and-the-abbot-s-madness/)).

Son of the Mayor of Barovia. Feels obligated to protect the people
