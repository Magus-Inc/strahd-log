## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

On the fourth floor of [Argynvostholt](/wiki/argynvostholt/), where Vladimir Horngaard guards a gargoyle statue, Aera recited the knightly vow, and the statue woke and proclaimed these words ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)). I read it to mean that bringing Argynvost's skull home from Castle Ravenloft could weaken Strahd's hold on the valley.

## The riddle

*As the gargoyle spoke it:*

> “When the dragon dreams its dream within its rightful tomb, the light of Argynvost will beam and rid this land of gloom.”
