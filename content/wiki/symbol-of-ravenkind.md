*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A sunburst amulet of pale silver with a red crystal at its heart. Eliza's song of the ravens first pointed us toward it ([Tser Pool](/posts/tser-pool/)), and Davien told us its history and its tie to Strahd ([Welcome to Krezk](/posts/welcome-to-krezk/)). Zurika gave it to Olfin when we swore to free Emil ([Blood and Oaths Under Moonligh](/posts/blood-and-oaths-under-moonligh/)). Olfin carried it into Castle Ravenloft, where it is one of our best weapons against vampires, and he turned its light on Volenta ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/), [The Fall of Volenta Popofsky](/posts/the-fall-of-volenta-popofsky/)).

## What it does

*Ryan shared the item's rules on 10 March 2026. In short:*

* **Legendary wondrous item.** Only a creature of good alignment can attune to it.
* **Charges.** It holds 5 charges and regains 1d4 + 1 each dawn.
* **Dawn's Embrace** (reaction, 1 charge): someone within 60 feet who would drop to 0 hit points drops to 1 instead and can't be hurt until the start of their next turn.
* **Light of Hope** (action, 1 charge): ends blindness, charm, deafness, fear, paralysis, poison and stun on one creature within 30 feet.
* **Sun's Blessing** (action, 3 charges): for a minute, friendly creatures within 30 feet deal an extra 1d4 radiant damage when they hit with a weapon.
* **Focus.** It works as a spellcasting focus for cleric and paladin spells and adds +1 to their spell attacks and save DCs.
