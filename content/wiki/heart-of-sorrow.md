## Description

![The Heart of Sorrow, a giant red crystal heart hanging in a tower above a spiral stair](/images/heart-of-sorrow-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini).*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The great crystal heart of Castle Ravenloft. In the castle Gumbo searched a clock and found a map showing that the castle's beating heart lies in the north tower ([The Boon, the Bride, and the Beating Heart](/posts/the-boon-the-bride-and-the-beating-heart/)). Aera, climbing the stairs alone before dawn, saw it with her own eyes: a hovering, misshapen red heart some ten feet across. She nearly fell, and Strahd caught her ([Varushka's Key](/posts/varushkas-key/)). She told us it is a red crystal heart with darkness swirling inside, hanging in the high tower, and we chose to finish our work in the castle before trying to destroy it ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).

When we came back, a hidden ladder brought us to a landing with a dizzying view of it, huge and pulsing, in a hollow tower sixty-five feet across at the base. A spiral stair with no railing climbs the walls, and it shivered with the storm's thunder as if it meant to throw us into the pit. The Heart may be the castle's one weak point, but the climb was too great a risk, and we withdrew ([Retrieval of the Dragon Skull](/posts/retrieval-of-the-dragon-skull/)).
