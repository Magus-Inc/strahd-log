## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

Vallaki is a walled town a long day's walk west of the village of Barovia, and when the village was besieged some of its people fled there ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)). The road ends at iron gates hung with wolves' heads. Refugees who were never let in camp outside the walls, many of them lost to dream pastries ([Welcome to Vellaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)).

### Getting in

The gate is chained and padlocked. The guards checked our teeth, tested us with garlic, charged each of us a gold piece and took any silver we carried (Morgantha had warned us, so we hid ours in her pie cart) ([Dream Pies](/posts/dream-pies/), [Welcome to Vellaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/)). Inside, the streets were empty and the town fell silent for a procession led by the Baron and Izek. An old woman Izek pushed into the mud was taken to the stocks, where everyone wears a crude donkey head ([Welcome to Vellaki*](/posts/welcome-to-vellaki-/), [A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). The town was forever readying a festival: the Wolf's Head Jamboree, then the Festival of the Blazing Sun ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)).

### The places we came to know

We made the Blue Water Inn our home. Blinsky's toy shop sold us a doll that looked like Ireena and told us of a clockwork man in the castle ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). In the Baron's mansion we found the town's records, Viktor's attic and his teleportation circle ([Whispers in the Barons Mansion](/posts/whispers-in-the-barons-mansion/)). Father Lucien took Ireena into the Church of Saint Andral, whose ward against the undead failed when its bones were stolen; we traced them through Milivoj's house to Heinrich's coffin shop and fought Volenta there ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/), [The Quest for the Missing Bones](/posts/the-quest-for-the-missing-bones/)). Lady Fiona Wachter dined us at Wachter Haus ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/), [Shadows Over Vallaki](/posts/shadows-over-vallaki/)).

### How the town changed

Izek ransacked our inn and burned Blinsky's shop looking for Ireena, and a crowd gathered in the square ([Consequences in Vallaki](/posts/consequences-in-vallaki/)). We followed him out through the eastern gate and the Zarovich gate to the lake and killed him there ([The Fall of Izek](/posts/the-fall-of-izek/)). Rahadin rode in to judge Milivoj and Heinrich in the town square; Milivoj went free and Heinrich was branded with the castle's mark. After that Lady Fiona held the town, though Viktor and his father kept to their mansion ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/), [Helping Victor](/posts/helping-victor/)). From Wachter Haus we crossed into the spirit realm to save Stella, and Shabasta did not come back ([Entering the Spirit Realm](/posts/entering-the-spirit-realm/)).

Vallaki stayed our base after that: we rested and planned the castle raid there, eight and a half miles from Ravenloft ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/), [Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/)).
