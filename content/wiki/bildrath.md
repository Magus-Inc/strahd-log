## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A man we met in the Blood on the Vine Tavern in the Village of Barovia. "Fools errand," he boomed when he heard of Ismark's mission, and he told us not to follow "Ismark the lesser." Ismark explained that Bildrath had lost friends because Ismark was too proud to run when the village was threatened ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)).
