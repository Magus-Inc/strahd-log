## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A Vistana accused of taking Arabelle. Arabelle's father, Alexi, claimed Yann drugged him and took her. A dusk elf found Yann's severed head near the camp, toward the Luna River, and from the footprints we think he died fighting there ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). Van Richten had kept the head. We questioned it with the Talisman of Echoes and judged that Arrigal had no part in taking Arabelle ([Finding Arabelle!](/posts/finding-arabelle/)).
