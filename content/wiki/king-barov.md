## Description

*Written from the session logs and the texts shared at the table, in Malron's words.*

King Barov von Zarovich II, Strahd's father. Kasimir told us it was Barov, not Strahd, who first broke the elves' peace. He demanded their fealty and tribute to the borders of old Zarovia, the kingdom his forebears had ruled, and when Erevan Löwenhart refused he took Othrondil in a swift and brutal war, with Rahadin fighting on his side. He ruled the elves with an iron fist and hunted down those who resisted. They rose only when he died and Strahd came to power ([A doll for Arabelle](/posts/a-doll-for-arabelle/)). His tomb in the castle, shared with Queen Ravenovia, is Rahadin's sanctuary ([Shadows and Steel: The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan](/posts/welcome-back-to-vellanki/)).
