## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The forest of the valley, home of the Forest Folk. These hermits shun the towns, hold Yester Hill sacred and worship Strahd as a god for his power over the land and weather. We met one of their druids on the road ([The Hunt for the Heart Stone](/posts/the-hunt-for-the-heart-stone/)).
