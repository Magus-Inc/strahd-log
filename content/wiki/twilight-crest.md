## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

A moth-shaped artifact full of powerful magic. Strahd brought it out at the Whispering Wall, after he gave us his gray and silver stones ([The Whispering Wall](/posts/the-whispering-wall/)). By day it can strengthen our friends; by night it calls up guardian spirits of shadow. Why Strahd parted with it, we do not know.

## What it does

* **Uncommon wondrous item.** A petrified-wood medallion set with azurite that is deep blue by day and dark purple by night.
* **Charges.** It has 3 charges and regains 1 at dawn.
* **By day,** spend 1 charge as an action to cast aid.
* **By night,** spend 1 charge as an action to cast spirit guardians (save DC 15). The guardians are shadowy, ash-like moths and deal only necrotic damage.
* **All 3 charges,** at any time of day, cast enervation (save DC 15) as an action. The crest then regains no charges for the next three dawns.
