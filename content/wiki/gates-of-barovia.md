## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The gates we passed between Durst Manor and the Village of Barovia. The fog seemed to follow us and drive us on. We came to some stonework and a closed gate that opened on its own as we came near. Not daring to let the mist catch us, we went through, and it closed behind us ([Barovia, Barovia](/posts/barovia-barovia/)).
