## Description

![A portrait of Tatyana, red-haired, wearing a silver moon-and-stars bracelet](/images/tatyana-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini).*

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

The woman Strahd loved. Ireena dug a silver bracelet from her buried keepsake box, set with the moon and stars in lapis lazuli and inscribed with Tatyana's name and "Find your path in the darkest of places", and gave it to Olfin ([Leaving Village Barovia](/posts/leaving-village-barovia/)). Lady Fiona read to us of Tatyana's wedding to Prince Sergei ([Delivery for Lady Fiona](/posts/delivery-for-lady-fiona/)). Sergei's ghost told us Strahd murdered him and that Tatyana took her own life, which set Strahd's curse in motion; Strahd believes Ireena is Tatyana reborn ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)).

In the King's Bedchamber of Castle Ravenloft, her portrait hangs above a blazing hearth. She is so like Ireena that we all stopped short, and in the painting she wears the same silver bracelet of moon and stars that Olfin once recovered and that went to Arabelle ([The Slipper and the Shadow of the Bat](/posts/the-slipper-and-the-shadow-of-the-bat/)).
