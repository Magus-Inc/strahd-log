A servant of Castle Ravenloft. Before we ever met her, we found a letter at the ancient oak addressed to "Dearest Varushka" from Katarina, full of regret, with a gift and the hope that nothing is ever too broken to repair ([The Oath, the Spy, and the Silver Moon](/posts/the-oath-the-spy-and-the-silver-moon/)).

In the castle's guest tower we found her on a bed in a white dress and apron, with two crimson punctures on her neck and half of a moon-shaped necklace, and she looked like the woman we had seen hanging in the closet. Ten days earlier, the day the sky turned red and the mists rose, she had seen a great darkness speaking with the king in his chambers, heard a thousand whispering voices and glimpsed a flash of amber. Since then Strahd had come to her room and fed on her again and again. She had tried to flee, been caught and locked in the guest suite, and the key lay in the desk of Rahadin, the chamberlain. She had given up hope of escape, but swore Strahd would never make her a monster like him. Her half-locket was a gift from Katarina, whom she once hoped to share a future with.

Then her spirit floated before us, as she is now, whispering "No one leaves", and we each felt a pain in our necks. When Olfin showed her the half pendant from the oak, she wept, let go of her hold on the key and departed. We woke to see her shadow on the wall with no one to cast it; it unlocked the closet and beckoned us through a trap door to potions, a diamond ring, a copy of the Seer card and a note apologizing for never finding the courage to make real the future it once promised ([Varushka's Key](/posts/varushkas-key/)). The Vistani elder Stanimir knew we had laid her spirit to rest, and it earned us his goodwill ([Unwelcome Guests](/posts/unwelcome-guests/)).
