## Description

*Written from the session logs, in Malron's words.*

We found it on Izek after the fight at the lake, along with thirty-five gold and his silvered battleaxe ([The Fall of Izek](/posts/the-fall-of-izek/)). The DM called it the hell hound whisperer. It is the second amber shard we carry; Aera keeps it with the [Durst Amber Shard](/wiki/durst-amber-shard/) from Durst Manor.
