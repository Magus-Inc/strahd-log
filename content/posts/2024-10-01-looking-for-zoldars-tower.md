**Seeking Szoldar**  
We set out to find Szoldar, who could guide us to the tower that holds the legendary Staff of Khazan. Along the way we came upon a decrepit shack and an old stone slab.

**Szoldar and Yevgeni**  
In the shack we met Szoldar and his brother, Yevgeni. Aera bought a wolf pelt from them for fifty gold pieces and found it was glamour weave, a rare and magical cloth.

**The Ancient Monument**  
Before we left, we looked over the stone slab, an ancient monument to the Dreamer and the Ladies Three. The brothers warned us that a werewolf pack prowls the area, led by a new and reckless alpha.

**By the River**  
By a river we found a scrap of cloth with the initials RVR. Rudolph van Richten, perhaps, a name that keeps following us.

**The Crossroads**  
At a crossroads stood two ominous scarecrows and a broken signpost. Szoldar told us tales of scarecrows that roam the countryside. Aera took no chances and peppered them with arrows, which sprang an ambush of twig blights. It was a fierce fight, but we won it.

**The Tower of Khazan**  
Our path led to a tower in the woods beside a lake. A bright Vistani wagon, painted purple and gold, sat at its base, with a sign: “Keep out, home and property of Ezmerelda d'Avenir. Trespassers will be incinerated.” Shabasta found wolf tracks near the wagon, and a second set of tracks leading into the woods and ending in a hollow full of white and yellow paint.

**Unlocking the Tower**  
The tower door had no handles, only a red seal bearing the name Khazan. Olfin worked out the pattern on the signet ring, traced its lines on the seal and pressed the right places, and the door opened.

The way into the tower stands open.
