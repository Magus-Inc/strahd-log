## Summary:

![The battle map at the hags' windmill, with minis on the path between the cart and the well](/images/the-windmill-heist-1.jpg)

*At the table: the battle at the windmill.*

Lady Fiona warned us that once the binding circle is up, she and her people can hold it for only ten minutes. Victor will counter spells as needed to keep it standing.

First, the greeting. Shabasta knocked on the windmill door, and he and Olfin were welcomed in after saying they would like to go into business together. The talks seemed to go well, and Morgantha called her daughters down to the first floor.

Then, the break-in. Our rogues used their conjured hands to fix a rope to the top of the tower and climbed up the outside. The window at the top was locked, so a conjured hand opened it from the inside. Aera unlocked the cage of the boy while Gumbo drew a key from the gnarled arm, then looked around for anything it might fit while Aera unlocked the second child. Gumbo found a tube in the millstone that seemed part of a lock, and the key opened it. Inside was the hags’ contract, along with six pieces of cheap jewelry worth twenty-five gold each and a charm of mass heroism. The contract is written on human skin, and the names on it are in a strange tongue, Abyssal. The rogues climbed deftly down the rope with the children and slipped them to Victor, and all but those still inside the mill went to Lady Fiona. Shabasta and Olfin excused themselves before Morgantha grew too suspicious.

Then, the takedown. Lady Fiona reads Abyssal, and the true names on the contract are Morgantha Stormreaver, Belladonna Sunbane and Offalia Wormwiggle. She raised the binding circle with her followers. The hags offered a bargain: they would lend us their heart stone for three days. We refused, and the fight began. Shortly before we believed them beaten, they offered once more to answer any of our questions if we stopped and let them go. We answered by attacking harder.

As Morgantha died she told us Strahd’s most carefully guarded secret: a temple of forbidden lore hidden in the mountains, the Amber Temple, where he first met the entity with which he forged his terrible pact. She knew neither where it lies nor what the pact is.

We took the hag’s heart stone and claimed the Bonegrinder for our base. We found six more pieces of cheap jewelry, twenty-five gold each, in the beds. To use the heart stone with Victor we need a night of the full moon, and Victor needs a ley line for the ritual. Lady Fiona says the cellar of her manor has one. We will rest through the day and meet at her house tonight, to perform the ritual under the full moon and rescue Stella.

## Key Locations:

**The Windmill**: A decrepit windmill, the hags’ home, full of traps and obstacles.

## Encounters:

**The bargain at the door**: Shabasta and Olfin talked our way inside, posing as would-be business partners of the hags.

**The fight**: The hags offered to lend us a heartstone. We refused and fell on them. They begged and bargained for their lives, but we kept on until they were beaten.

## Loot:

**Six pieces of cheap jewelry**: Each worth 25 gold pieces, found by Gumbo.

**Six more pieces of cheap jewelry**: 25 gold each, found in the beds.

**The heart stone**: Taken from the hag.

**The Bonegrinder**: Claimed as our base.

**Charm of Mass Heroism**: Grants the benefit of a potion of heroism to any number of creatures within 30 feet, found by Gumbo.

## Notable NPCs:

**Lady Fiona**: Translated the contract, written in Abyssal, and began the binding circle to trap the hags.

**Victor**: Worked counter spells to hold the binding circle.

## Plot Points:

We learned the hags’ true names, Morgantha Stormreaver, Belladonna Sunbane and Offalia Wormwiggle, and used them to bind and defeat them.

Morgantha’s dying words: Strahd’s most guarded secret is the Amber Temple, hidden in the mountains, where he forged his pact.

The heart stone, a full moon and the ley line under Lady Fiona’s manor are what Victor needs to reach Stella.

## Session Highlights:

* Shabasta and Olfin talking our way into the windmill.

* Our rogues securing a rope with a conjured hand and climbing the tower.

* Gumbo finding the hags’ contract and the charm of mass heroism.

* The fierce fight with the hags, and our refusal of every bargain.
