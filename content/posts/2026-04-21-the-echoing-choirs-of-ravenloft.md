## Summary:
We pressed on through the catacombs beneath Castle Ravenloft toward our chief goal, Crypt 40, the tomb of Saint Markovia. Aera, with Emil’s help and Lathander’s guidance through me, heaved open the heavy stone door. That sprang a trap, and poisoned darts rained down on us. Stung and sickened, we went in anyway. I searched the Saint’s remains and found the **Icon of Dawn’s Grace** hidden in her dusty robes.

Note: [Before the session began, the players had a long talk about Stephen King's **11/22/63** and the musical turns of Sturgill Simpson (aka Johnny Blue Skies). During the door-opening, the group joked about how weak they all are: the Rogue was actually the "strongest" member of the party, with a +1 modifier.]

The Icon is alive, and it spoke to me mind to mind. It named itself a shard of the divinity of Ethereal, an angelic servant of the Morning Lord. It told me of its history with Saint Markovia and of its purpose now: it must go back to the Abbey of Saint Markovia in Krezk to cleanse the Abbot of his corruption and madness. It is a powerful weapon, and the key to curing the rot we saw in Krezk.

Note: [The DM gave a long lore dump on the Icon's history, spanning 400 years, and its tie to the Abbot's fall into madness. The players briefly debated the merits of Fortnite, and the value of eating a dead gecko for $20 (a story Crusty shared from his past).]

We searched on and came to Crypt 37, the tomb of the wizard Growmor Nimblenobs. With the wizard’s own brass-knobbed staff we worked a hidden mechanism, and the marble slab lifted on a leather case of potent spell scrolls: **Fireball**, **Lightning Bolt** and **Cone of Cold**. Nearby, in Crypt 31, I got past a pit hidden under a tarp and took a scroll of **Magic Circle**. Battered and low on magic, we decided to rest the night. Crusty conjured a small magic hut in a dark corner of the catacombs, and I called down Lathander’s aid to strengthen us all before we slept.

Note: [Malron upcast **Aid** to 4th level, giving everyone 15 extra maximum hit points. The players talked over which healing spells are most efficient, which led to the "Chicago" joke where Malron mixed up **Cure Wounds** and **Healing Word**.]

The rest did not last. As we made ready to leave the catacombs to find Gertruda, a deathly choir of a thousand screaming voices began to echo through the halls. I knew the sound at once: **Rahadin**, the chamberlain of Castle Ravenloft. He came out of the shadows and demanded we give up the treasures we had “stolen” from the crypts. We refused, and he fell on us with terrifying speed, his saber flickering like a dark wind as he ran through our ranks.

Note: [Realizing they faced a CR 14 opponent while still worn down, the players scrambled for a "cheese" plan with **Hold Person**, **Polymorph**, and a "Captain America" style volley of **Fireballs**. The DM said the fight was "very hard" for a group of their level.]

The battle was brutal. Rahadin’s very presence is agony to the mind, and his saber nearly felled me more than once. Crusty and Aera worked together, and in the end Crusty quickened Aera so she could pepper the chamberlain with arrows from her “turbo bow.” For all we did, and though I called up a guardian of faith, Rahadin stayed a ghostly, deadly threat, zigzagging through our defenses. As I write this, we are still fighting for our lives.

## Key Locations:

**Crypt 40 (Saint Markovia)**: The Saint’s resting place, guarded by a poison dart trap. It held the Icon of Dawn’s Grace.  
**Crypt 37 (Growmor Nimblenobs)**: The tomb of a wizard extraordinaire. A hidden space beneath the slab held three powerful spell scrolls of war.  
**Crypt 31**: A crypt with gold-painted walls and a pit trap, where a skeleton held a **Magic Circle** scroll.  
**Strahd’s Tomb**: A grand, bloodstained tomb we found but wisely did not enter.

## Encounters:

**Poison Dart Trap (Crypt 40)**: The darts fell as we opened the door. Most of us dodged the worst, but the poison still did us real harm.  
**Rahadin (The Chamberlain)**: A desperate battle against Strahd’s right hand. With his deathly choir and his speed he strikes several of us in a single breath. The fight is not over.

## Loot:

* **Icon of Dawn’s Grace**: Sentient lawful good statuette. Grants **Protection from Evil and Good** once per day. Held by **Malron**.
* **Growmor’s Staff**: Wooden staff with brass and marble knobs. Used to open the secret compartment in Crypt 37.
* **Scroll of Fireball**: Taken by **Aera** (for one-time use).
* **Scroll of Cone of Cold**: Held by the party.
* **Scroll of Lightning Bolt**: Held by the party.
* **Scroll of Magic Circle**: Recovered from Crypt 31 via **Mage Hand**.

## Notable NPCs:

**Icon of Dawn’s Grace**: An angelic shard of divinity. Courteous, alive, and determined to go back to the Abbey in Krezk to save the Abbot.  
**Rahadin**: Strahd’s loyal chamberlain. Cold, efficient, and wrapped in the screams of those he has killed. He takes us for common thieves.

## Plot Points:

* **The Abbot’s Cure**: We now carry the very divinity that may cure the Abbot of his madness.
* **Rahadin’s Watch**: We no longer move through the castle unseen; the chamberlain is hunting us.
* **Strahd’s Resting Place**: We know where Strahd’s own tomb lies, but we keep to our present goals.

## Session Highlights:

* **The Icon’s Plea**: “The vampire must be destroyed. Use me as your weapon.”
* **The Dragon’s Return**: Aera sending her spirit dragon, Zorya, to scout for Gertruda in the chaos.

Note: The Gecko Incident: Crusty once ate a dead gecko for 20 gold pieces. And the "Chicago" heal: Malron's muddled attempt to name his healing spells, to the whole party's confusion.
