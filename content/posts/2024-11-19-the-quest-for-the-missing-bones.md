**Characters:**  
- **Liren:** Dragonborn Barbarian  
- **Gumbo:** Half-orc Rogue  
- **Aera:** Elf Rogue  
- **Malron:** Reborn Cleric  
- **Shabasta** Porcupine Bard

**Summary:**  
Father Lucian helped us find the home of Milivoj, a leaning, decrepit house with a door hanging askew. The father believes Milivoj is innocent of the theft. A woman answered our knock with three children behind her. Shabasta played music for the children, but the older boy said Milivoj was too ill to see anyone. His sister talked him round, and Milivoj came out with more children on his shoulders.

Liren saw through him and frightened him into confessing. His parents are lost to dream pastries, and he must keep his brothers and sisters fed. He sold the bones to Henrik the day before.

On the way to Henrik’s, Shabasta, disguised as Father Lucian, persuaded the guards to arrest Milivoj for theft. At Henrik’s door he talked four guards into breaking in and arresting Henrik, who would not say where the bones were. We searched the shop and found a room with thirteen coffins, then went up to the second floor, where there was a kitchen and a bedroom.

I opened the wardrobe and a poisoned dart struck me. I could not move for ten minutes, and a noisemaker brought our enemies down on us. Even so, we spotted a bag that likely held the bones. We fought two vampires amid a great many fires, and Volenta, a bride of Strahd, escaped, leaving behind mist and a glowing pentagram. Aera handed me the bones and stepped into the last room of the building.

**Key Locations:**  
1. **Milivoj’s Home:** A decrepit house, where he confessed.  
2. **Henrik’s Shop:** Where we confronted the thief’s buyer and searched for the bones.

**Encounters:**  
- **Milivoj and his family:** Questions, music and a confession.  
- **Liren’s threats:** Enough to break Milivoj.  
- **Shabasta’s disguise:** The guards obeyed “Father Lucian”.  
- **The wardrobe:** A poisoned dart for me, and a noisemaker to wake the house.  
- **The fight:** Volenta and two vampires, with Father Lucian at our side.

**Loot:**  
- **Bones of St. Andral:** In a bag in the wardrobe.

**Notable NPCs:**  
- **Father Lucian:** Helped us in the search.  
- **Milivoj:** The thief, stealing to feed his family.  
- **Henrik:** Would not help us, and was arrested by the guards.  
- **Volenta:** A bride of Strahd, who escaped and left clues behind.

**Plot Points:**  
1. We learned why Milivoj stole the bones.  
2. Henrik was arrested and we searched his shop for the bones.  
3. Volenta escaped us, and we will surely meet her again.

**Session Highlights:**  
- Shabasta’s clever disguise to sway the guards.  
- Liren’s threats bringing out the confession.  
- The excitement and danger of my run-in with the wardrobe.
