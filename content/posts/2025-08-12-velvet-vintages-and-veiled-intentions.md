## Characters:

* **Aera shan Ravanór**: A cunning Wood-elf Rogue adept at stealth and navigating complex situations.
* **Gumbo**: A pragmatic Half-Orc Rogue with a knack for stealth and unexpected solutions.
* **Oogway**: A serene and wise turtle Wizard Bladesinger who wields magic and blade with precision.
* **Zorpheus**: Mysterious wanderer and bard drawn to fate and prophecy.
* **Malron Lichtstrahl**: A Reborn Cleric of Lathander with a connection to spirits and the undead.

## Summary:

The party arrives at Castle Ravenloft in a black carriage, having solved the puzzle chest and claimed its rewards: **velvet-trimmed gloves**, **wolf-trimmed cloaks**, and a **chest of holding**.

Upon arrival, they are greeted by **Rahadin** and **Anastrasia**, who usher them into the castle’s grand halls. The atmosphere is tense and theatrical, filled with a strange mix of elegance and dread.

They meet **Escher**, a blonde man with a suspicious past—once a friend of **Doru**, now believed to have betrayed him. Aera notices that several women dressed as brides seem visibly uncomfortable, hinting at deeper unrest.

**Volenta** and **Ludmila** are also present, adding to the castle’s eerie ensemble. Anastrasia introduces **Sasha** and Escher with a biting remark. Sasha trembles under her words, and Malron senses that Sasha may be a potential informant—perhaps even a spy.

A masked servant arrives, trembling, his fingers severed at the first knuckle. He offers **purple liquid** to the party while the vampires drink **red wine**. An accident occurs—he spills red wine on Volenta’s white dress, causing a stir.

Zorpheus and Sasha volunteer to help clean the mess, retrieving mops from the castle’s depths. In the **kitchen**, they encounter a man resembling **Cyrus**, and in the **wine cellar**, they find a woman hanging upside down, being drained of blood.

Sasha discreetly shares a cryptic tip with Zorpheus:

> **“Sixth pedal from the left on the organ.”**

Later, **Strahd** himself offers a tour and recommends the party visit the **Amber Temple** to the far west.

## Key Locations:

**Castle Ravenloft – Grand Hall**: Lavish and unsettling, filled with vampires and uneasy guests.

**Wine Cellar**: A grim scene of blood harvesting, revealing the castle’s darker truths.

**Kitchen**: A brief encounter with a man resembling Cyrus.

## Encounters:

* **Social Encounter – Vampire Court**: The party navigates introductions, veiled threats, and subtle power plays.
* **Investigation – Wine Cellar**: Zorpheus and Sasha uncover a disturbing scene of vampiric feeding.
* **Espionage – Sasha’s Tip**: A clue about the organ’s sixth pedal may unlock secrets later.

## Plot Points:

* **Sasha’s Clue**: The sixth pedal on the organ may reveal a hidden mechanism or passage.
* **Spy in the Castle**: Sasha appears willing to help the party from within.
* **Amber Temple**: Strahd’s recommendation may be a trap—or a path to power.
* **Escher’s Betrayal**: His connection to Doru raises suspicions.
* **Blood Rituals**: The wine cellar confirms the castle’s gruesome practices.
