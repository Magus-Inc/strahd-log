## Summary:
We came to Castle Ravenloft in the black carriage, having solved the puzzle chest and claimed what it held: **velvet-trimmed gloves**, **wolf-trimmed cloaks** and a **chest of holding**.

**Rahadin** and **Anastrasya** greeted us and ushered us into the castle’s grand halls. The air was tense and theatrical, a strange mix of elegance and dread.

We met **Escher**, a blond man with a doubtful past, once a friend of **Doru** and now thought to have betrayed him. Aera noticed that several women dressed as brides looked plainly uncomfortable, a sign of deeper unrest.

**Volenta** and **Ludmilla** were there too, rounding out the eerie company. Anastrasya introduced **Sasha** and Escher with a biting remark. Sasha trembled at her words, and I sensed she might be willing to inform for us, perhaps even to spy.

A masked servant came in, shaking, his fingers cut off at the first knuckle. He offered us a **purple drink** while the vampires drank **red wine**. Then he spilled red wine on Volenta’s white dress, and there was a stir.

Zorpheus and Sasha offered to clean it up and fetched mops from the depths of the castle. In the **kitchen** they saw a man who looked like **Cyrus**, and in the **wine cellar** they found a woman hanging upside down, being drained of her blood.

Sasha quietly passed Zorpheus a cryptic tip:

> **“Sixth pedal from the left on the organ.”**

Later **Strahd** himself offered us a tour, and recommended we visit the **Amber Temple**, far to the west.

## Key Locations:

**Castle Ravenloft – Grand Hall**: Lavish and unsettling, full of vampires and uneasy guests.

**Wine Cellar**: A grim scene of blood harvesting, the castle’s darker truth.

**Kitchen**: A glimpse of a man who looked like Cyrus.

## Encounters:

* **The Vampire Court**: Introductions, veiled threats and quiet struggles for power.
* **The Wine Cellar**: Zorpheus and Sasha found a vampire’s feeding.
* **Sasha’s Tip**: The organ’s sixth pedal may open secrets later.

## Loot:

* **Velvet-Trimmed Gloves**: Elegant and possibly enchanted.
* **Wolf-Trimmed Cloaks**: Stylish and warm.
* **Chest of Holding**: A magical container, opened by solving its puzzle.

## Notable NPCs:

* **Rahadin**: Strahd’s chamberlain, cold and formal.
* **Anastrasya**: A sharp-tongued vampire who holds Sasha under her thumb.
* **Escher**: A blond vampire, suspected of betraying Doru.
* **Sasha**: A trembling servant, perhaps a spy, who told Zorpheus a secret.
* **Masked Servant**: Maimed and terrified; his spilled wine set off a stir.
* **Volenta & Ludmilla**: Vampire brides, present at the reception.
* **Strahd von Zarovich**: Our host, who offered a tour and cryptic advice.

## Plot Points:

* **Sasha’s Clue**: The sixth pedal on the organ may open a hidden mechanism or passage.
* **Spy in the Castle**: Sasha seems willing to help us from within.
* **Amber Temple**: Strahd’s suggestion may be a trap, or a path to power.
* **Escher’s Betrayal**: His tie to Doru makes us wary.
* **Blood Rituals**: The wine cellar confirms the castle’s gruesome ways.

## Session Highlights:

* **“Sixth pedal from the left on the organ.”** — Sasha’s whisper to Zorpheus.
* **“Spy in castle. Seek a meeting to discuss an opportunity. Find the victim amidst the court. Burn this — A”** — Arrigal’s note from before, now echoed in Sasha’s manner.
* The masked servant’s trembling, maimed hands, an unforgettable picture of fear.
* The wine on Volenta’s dress, an accident that nearly turned to violence.
* Our careful restraint in the vampire court, getting through danger with good manners.
