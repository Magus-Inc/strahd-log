## Story

### Guests of Castle Ravenloft

We came to Castle Ravenloft in the black carriage, having solved the puzzle chest and claimed what it held: velvet-trimmed gloves, wolf-trimmed cloaks and a chest of holding.

![Castle Ravenloft on its cliff, the drawbridge spanning the chasm from the road, the village in the valley below](/images/castle-ravenloft-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026). Not official art.*

**Rahadin** and Anastrasya greeted us and ushered us into the castle’s grand halls. The air was tense and theatrical, a strange mix of elegance and dread.

We met Escher, a blond man who was once a friend of Doru and now thought to have betrayed him. We had already heard his story: he was a bard from Barovia who joined Doru’s revolt as his lieutenant, then slipped away from the mob in the catacombs not long before Strahd fell on them. Van Richten later caught him, a golden-haired vampire spawn by then, as a Vistana brought him a kidnapped girl, and left him scarred. Aera noticed that several women dressed as brides looked plainly uncomfortable, a sign of deeper unrest.

**Volenta** and Ludmilla were there too, rounding out the eerie company. There was tension between the brides, and it showed. Anastrasya introduced Sasha and Escher with a biting remark. Sasha trembled at her words, and I sensed she might be willing to inform for us, perhaps even to spy. Arrigal’s note had told us there was a spy in the castle, and I wondered if she was the one he meant.

### Two Wines

A masked servant came in, shaking, his fingers cut off at the first knuckle and his eyes wide with fear. He carried two drinks. He poured a purple one for us, and the vampires drank a red one. We soon worked out that the red was not for us to touch, and we took it to be blood. Then his trembling hands spilled red wine on Volenta’s white dress, and there was a stir.

### Below the Hall

Zorpheus and Sasha offered to clean it up and went for mops. Sasha took him down by a hidden way, a door that opens from the great organ, with stairs behind it that lead down to the kitchens.

![Sasha, a castle servant with a candle, whispering by the great organ](/images/sasha-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026). Not official art.*

Sasha quietly told Zorpheus how to open it:

> **“Sixth pedal from the left on the organ.”**

In the kitchen Zorpheus saw a man who looked like Cyrus, the Belviews’ patriarch who was taken from the Abbey of Saint Markovia to Castle Ravenloft. Past the kitchen, in the wine cellar they found a woman hanging upside down, being drained of her blood. We think the red “wine” upstairs came from her, and that she was the masked servant’s wife.

### Strahd’s Invitation

Back in the hall, Strahd himself offered us a tour, and recommended we visit the Amber Temple, far to the west.

## Key Locations

* **Castle Ravenloft – Grand Hall**: Lavish and unsettling, full of vampires and uneasy guests.
* **Wine Cellar**: A grim scene of blood harvesting, the castle’s darker truth.
* **Kitchen**: A glimpse of a man who looked like Cyrus.
* **The Great Organ**: Its sixth pedal from the left opens a hidden door to the stairs down to the kitchens.

## Encounters

* **The Vampire Court**: Introductions, veiled threats and quiet struggles for power.
* **Two Wines**: Purple for us, red for the vampires, and we kept our hands off the red.
* **The Wine Cellar**: Zorpheus and Sasha found a vampire’s feeding.
* **Sasha’s Tip**: The organ’s sixth pedal opens the hidden way down.

## Loot

* **Velvet-Trimmed Gloves**: Elegant and possibly enchanted.
* **Wolf-Trimmed Cloaks**: Stylish and warm.
* **Chest of Holding**: A magical container, opened by solving its puzzle.

## Notable NPCs

* **Rahadin**: Strahd’s chamberlain, cold and formal.
* **Anastrasya**: A sharp-tongued vampire who holds Sasha under her thumb.
* **Escher**: A blond vampire, suspected of betraying Doru.
* **Sasha**: A trembling servant, perhaps a spy, who told Zorpheus a secret.
* **Masked Servant**: Maimed and terrified. His spilled wine set off a stir, and we think his wife is the woman in the cellar.
* **Volenta & Ludmilla**: Vampire brides, present at the reception.
* **Strahd von Zarovich**: Our host, who offered a tour and cryptic advice.

## Plot Points

* **Sasha’s Clue**: The sixth pedal on the organ opens a hidden way down to the kitchens, a back route through the castle.
* **Spy in the Castle**: Sasha seems willing to help us from within.
* **Amber Temple**: Strahd’s suggestion may be a trap, or a path to power.
* **Escher’s Betrayal**: His tie to Doru makes us wary.
* **Blood Rituals**: The wine cellar confirms the castle’s gruesome ways.
* **The Servant’s Wife**: The woman drained in the cellar may be the masked servant’s wife.

## Session Highlights

* **“Sixth pedal from the left on the organ.”** — Sasha’s whisper to Zorpheus.
* **“Spy in castle. Seek a meeting to discuss an opportunity. Find the victim amidst the court. Burn this — A”** — Arrigal’s note from before, now echoed in Sasha’s manner.
* The masked servant’s trembling, maimed hands, an unforgettable picture of fear.
* The red wine spilled on Volenta’s white dress.
