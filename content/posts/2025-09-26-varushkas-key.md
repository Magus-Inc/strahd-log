## Summary:

Our night in the guest tower of Castle Ravenloft became a nightmare that loops. Every time we went through the trap door or the front door, we came back to the guest room. We fought off the things in the room that came alive and attacked us, then went into the third loop.

Zorpheus came in through the front door and heard a ticking, beating sound under the bed. Hiding there was a little man with a face painted like a jack-o’-lantern: **Pidlwick**. I made my eyes burn red with a small prayer and tried to frighten him. It did not work. Pidlwick tried to throw me down a hundred-foot drop, then vanished.

Aera found a hook in the clock, and with it we went through the trap door again. Out the window and across the rooftop we spotted a closet. Aera shot an arrow trailing a rope into it, and we tied ourselves to one another with a second rope to steady the crossing. Bats attacked us on the roof, sounds like Rahadin came from behind, and a **black dragon** burst out of the guest room. After a tense moment or two we all made it into the closet and up through its trap door.

On the bed above lay a woman in a white dress and apron, with two crimson punctures on her neck and half of a moon-shaped necklace. She looked like the woman who had been hanging in the closet. She is **Varushka**, a servant of the castle.

Varushka told us her story. Ten days ago, the day the sky turned red and the mists rose, she saw a great darkness speaking with the king in his chambers, heard a thousand whispering voices, and glimpsed a flash of amber in the air. Since then the lord of the castle has come to her room and fed on her again and again. She was too frightened to say his name, though she confirmed it is Strahd. When she tried to flee she was caught and locked in the guest suite. The key is in the desk of **Rahadin**, the chamberlain, in his office far below near the kitchens and the servants’ hall, past the Hall of Bones and the Kingsmen Hall. She has given up hope of escape, but she is determined that Strahd will never make her a monster like him. Her half-locket was a gift from **Katarina**, someone she once hoped to share a future with and now believes she will never see again.

Through the trap door once more, we landed in the servants’ quarters, deeper in the castle. Down the stairs we found three more corpses, and someone called out that **Valysnia is a traitor**. In the **Hall of Bones** a dragon’s head hangs on the wall.

Aera slipped into Rahadin’s office and found Strahd in the middle of feeding on someone. Still she found the key in the desk and stole it. Back at the door, Olfin turned the key. Spikes shot out and pierced his hand, but the door unlocked.

A spectral woman floated before us, surely Varushka as she is now. She whispered “No one leaves,” and we each felt a pain in our necks. She told us we might have found the key where she buried it, but she would never set us free. Olfin showed her the half pendant from the oak tree. Weeping, she let go of her hold on the key and departed.

We woke to see Varushka’s shadow on the wall with no one there to cast it. The shadow moved, the guest closet unlocked, and she beckoned us through the trap door. There we found a **potion of fire breath**, **two potions of healing**, a rolled-up parchment, a small satin pouch and a copy of the **Seer** Tarokka card. The note, in large simple letters, apologizes for never finding the courage to make real the future the card once promised. The pouch holds a **diamond ring with a gold band** worth 150 gold.

Aera climbed higher still and saw a hovering, misshapen red thing: a **crystal heart** some ten feet across. She nearly fell from the stairs, and **Strahd caught her**. He was not pleased that a guest had left her suite before dawn, but he let her live, and she ran back to us. Strahd sent us out of the castle in his carriage, and as we left we saw his **three brides** watching from a window.

The carriage will take us to one place of our choosing. We need only say where.
