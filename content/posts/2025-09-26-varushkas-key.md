# Varushka's Key

*Written from Chris's notes for this session; there is no recording.*

## Characters:

* **Aera shan Ravanór**: A cunning Wood-elf Rogue adept at stealth and navigating complex situations.
* **Malron Lichtstrahl**: A Reborn Cleric of Lathander with a connection to spirits and the undead.
* **Zorpheus**: Mysterious wanderer and bard drawn to fate and prophecy.
* **Olfin Darning**: A Hexblood Paladin navigating the balance of light and shadow through their oath.

## Summary:

The night in Castle Ravenloft's guest tower has turned into a nightmare that loops: every trip through the trap door or the front door brings the party back to the guest room. They fight off the objects in the room that come to life and attack them, then enter the third loop.

Zorpheus comes in through the front door and hears a ticking, beating noise under the bed. Hiding there is a small man with a face painted like a jack-o'-lantern: **Pidlwick**. Malron uses Thaumaturgy to turn his eyes red and tries to intimidate him. It fails. Pidlwick tries to throw Malron down a 100-foot drop, then disappears.

Aera finds a hook in the clock, and the party uses it to go through the trap door again. Out the window, across the rooftop, they spot a closet. Aera shoots an arrow trailing a rope into it, and the party ties themselves to each other with another rope to steady the crossing. Bats attack on the rooftop, sounds like Rahadin come from behind, and a **black dragon** bursts out of the guest room. After a tense moment or two, everyone makes it into the closet and up its trap door.

On the bed above lies a woman in a white dress and apron, with two crimson puncture wounds on her neck and half of a moon-shaped necklace. She looks like the woman who had been hanging in the closet. She is **Varushka**, a servant of the castle.

Varushka tells her story. Ten days ago, the day the sky turned red and the mists rose, she saw a great darkness speak with the king in his chambers, heard a thousand whispering voices, and glimpsed a flash of amber in the air. Since then the lord of the castle has come to her room and fed on her blood again and again. She is too frightened to say his name, though she confirms it is Strahd. When she tried to flee she was caught and locked in the guest suite. The key is in the desk of **Rahadin**, the chamberlain, in his office far below near the kitchens and servants' hall, past the Hall of Bones and the Kingsmen Hall. She has given up hope of escape, but she is determined that Strahd will never make her a monster like him. Her half-locket was a gift from **Katarina**, someone she once hoped to share a future with and now believes she will never see again.

Through the trap door once more, the party lands in the servants' quarters, deeper in the castle. Down the stairs they find three more corpses, and someone calls out that **Valysnia is a traitor**. In the **Hall of Bones** a dragon's head hangs on the wall.

Aera sneaks into Rahadin's office and finds Strahd in the middle of feeding on someone. She still finds the key in the desk and steals it. Back at the door, Olfin turns the key; spikes shoot out and pierce his hand, but the door unlocks.

A spectral woman floats before them, likely Varushka as she is now. She whispers "No one leaves," and the party feels a pain in their necks. She tells them they may have found the key where she buried it, but she will never set them free. Olfin shows her the half pendant Liren had found earlier in a tree. Weeping, she lets go of her hold on the key and departs.

The party awakens to see Varushka's shadow on the wall with no one to cast it. The shadow moves, the guest closet unlocks, and she beckons them through the trap door. There they find a **potion of fire breath**, **two potions of healing**, and a rolled-up parchment, a small satin pouch and a copy of the **Seer** Tarokka card. The note, in large simple letters, apologizes for never finding the courage to make real the future the card once promised. The pouch holds a **diamond ring with a gold band** worth 150 gp.

Aera climbs higher still and sees a hovering, misshapen red object: a **crystal heart** about 10 feet across. She nearly falls from the stairs, and **Strahd catches her**. He is not pleased that a guest left her suite before dawn, but he lets her live, and she runs to rejoin the others. Strahd sends the party out of the castle in his carriage, and as they leave they see his **three brides** in a window.

The carriage will take them to one place of their choosing; they need only say where.

**The party reaches Level 7!**
