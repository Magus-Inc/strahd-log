## Story

### Van Richten’s Chest

We found a locked wooden chest nestled in the bottom of the great oak tree with “R V Richten” carved into it. Aera was able to pick the lock and found the following within:  
\- 20 silver crossbow bolts  
\- 2 holy water  
\- 1 healing potion  
\- 2 healers kits  
\- 20 pieces of silver

### Strahd Stops His Carriage

We continued walking along the river until we met up with the road again and a cliff which prevented us from following the river further. Just then some of us heard a carriage coming along the road so we all hid near the road. The carriage came with wolves or dire wolves with yellow eyes escorting it. It stopped along the road, just where we were hiding. Out stepped a tall gaunt aristocrat wearing a red brooch and a Longsword.

The man, or creature, was none other than Count Strahd von Zarovich and he called out to [Ireena Kolyana](/wiki/ireena-kolyana/), looking directly at where she was hiding. He looked directly at where each of us was hiding as he talked to us. He made it clear that he would not give many warnings if we disrespected him, and his seven dire wolves looked eager to tear us apart.

![Strahd on a misty forest road at night with two dire wolves](/images/strahd-von-zarovich-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini, October 2026). Not official art.*

Strahd was upset about some vassal of his, which I first thought was some peasant and a case of mistaken identity, but later came to realize that he was talking about the Durst Manor and their supply of grain from the [windmill](/wiki/durst-mill/). He indicated that he has plenty of wine and the vineyard was not of concern to him.

Strahd demanded that Ismark follow his will, and it seemed he was going to take Ireena away with him. I’m still not sure how, but we convinced him to allow her to stay with us a bit longer, hopefully long enough to find some sanctuary for her. He also forgave us for our past transgressions, and he claimed to be no liar, but that he found us curious and would study us for a while. Weeble almost pushed his luck and nearly found out if it would be the wolves that tore him to shreds or the Longsword of Strahd. Strahd and his wolves then left us alone by the road.

### A Bird in Distress

We continued on our journey and after a while we heard a bird flying towards us, making calls of distress. Weeble put it to sleep with a spell, fearing it would alert other minions of Strahd to our position. Olfin felt pity on the bird that had fallen out of the sky, as he saw it was injured, pierced by silver barbs, and had probably been genuinely in need of help, so he laid hands on the bird to heal it.

### The Strix and the Dire Wolf

Next thing we knew there was a giant artificial bird creature flying towards us. It was a Strix followed by three swarms. As we fought them off Schlopkht found himself separated from the party and encountered a Dire Wolf, which only watched from a distance. Perhaps Strahd is testing us.

After the battle Olfin attempted to place a lock of hair on the bird but it escaped him. Weeble put it to sleep again, and Olfin tied his lock of hair around the birds leg. It must be some kind of Hexblood sign of life debt. I must be sure to avoid finding myself in such a predicament with Olfin. I’m not sure a Hexblood is much better than a vampire, paladin or not.

### Tents by the Lake

We continued along the wagon path until we found a clearing by a lake. There were five tents there with wagons and horses, along with a large tent. Perhaps this is the Vistani encampment with [Madam Eva](/wiki/madam-eva/)

## Key Locations

* **The great oak**: A locked chest nestled at its foot.
* **The road by the cliff**: Where Strahd’s carriage stopped beside our hiding places.
* **A clearing by a lake**: Five tents, wagons, horses and a large tent. Perhaps the Vistani camp of Madam Eva.

## Encounters

* **Strahd’s carriage**: Seven dire wolves, and the Count calling to Ireena.
* **The wounded bird**: Pierced by silver barbs. Weeble put it to sleep and Olfin healed it.
* **A strix and three swarms**: Fought off together.
* **A dire wolf**: It only watched Schlopkht from a distance.

## Loot

* **R V Richten’s chest**: 20 silver crossbow bolts, 2 holy water, a healing potion, 2 healer’s kits and 20 pieces of silver.

## Notable NPCs

* **Strahd von Zarovich**: Looked straight at each of our hiding places, forgave our past transgressions, and will study us.
* **Ireena Kolyana**: Allowed to stay with us a while longer.
* **Madam Eva**: Perhaps waiting at the camp by the lake.

## Plot Points

* **Strahd’s vassal**: His anger is about the Durst Manor and its grain from the [windmill](/wiki/durst-mill/). The vineyard is no concern of his.
* **Ireena stays with us**: Long enough, we hope, to find her sanctuary.
* **Strahd’s warnings**: He will not give many.
* **Olfin’s lock of hair**: Tied around the bird’s leg, a sign of life debt.

## Session Highlights

* Weeble very nearly pushing his luck with Strahd.
* Olfin chasing down the bird he had just healed.
