# A Gilded Cage and the Blueprint of Shadows

## Characters:

* **Aera shan Ravanór (Liz)**: A Wood-elf Rogue and expert infiltrator. Having reached Level 7, she has mastered **Evasion**, allowing her to shrug off the worst of dragon breath or magical explosions. She is currently caring for a pseudodragon companion.
* **Malron Lichtstrahl (Chris)**: A Reborn Cleric of Lathander and Spirit Medium. His body serves as a vessel for an ancient Elven spirit, and his divine connection to the Morninglord has grown even stronger as the party reaches new heights of power.
* **Shabasta (Grant)**: A Bard of fate and mystery. Now at Level 7, he is expanding his arcane repertoire with tactical magic like **Silvery Barbs** and **Dissonant Whispers**, though he remains deeply focused on the prophetic nature of their journey.
* **Olfin Darning (Seth)**: A Hexblood Paladin who carries the **Symbol of Ravenkind**. As a primary frontline defender, his resilience is legendary, and he is a prime candidate for the party’s new tactical buffs.
* **Krusty – Ralph Kristofferson (Cole)**: A newly arrived Wizard with a background in academic arcana. He has recently mastered 3rd-level spells, bringing the destructive power of **Fireball** and the utility of **Fly** and **Haste** to the group.

## Summary:

The session began with a tense recap of the party’s final morning at Castle Ravenloft. Following Aera’s solo reconnaissance of the **Heart of Sorrow** and her chilling encounter with Count Strahd, the group was promptly escorted from the guest quarters by Rahadin. Under the watchful eyes of the castle’s gargoyles, the party—joined by their allies **Ezmerelda d’Avenir** and \*\*Ireena Kolyana\*\*—departed by carriage.

During the journey back to **Vallaki**, the party underwent a period of significant growth, with everyone reaching **Level 7**. Upon arriving in the safety of the town, the group immediately pivoted to a deep-dive tactical session. Using intelligence gathered from various sources, they began mapping out a daring heist. The plan centers on a window of opportunity where Strahd is expected to be away from the castle, allowing the group to infiltrate the catacombs and secure vital relics and allies.

**Note:** Much of the morning involved “meta talk” regarding the transition to Level 7. The players discussed the mechanical impact of **Evasion** and the ethics of using **Silvery Barbs** (which the DM noted is often treated as a higher-level spell due to its potency). A lighthearted sidebar occurred regarding the Canadian game **Crokinole**, comparing its mechanics to curling.

## Key Locations:

**The High Tower (K18/K57)**: The site of the **Heart of Sorrow**. The party discussed using the staircase here as a potential route to the crypts, though it is heavily guarded.  
**The Catacombs (K84)**: The primary objective of the upcoming heist. It contains numerous crypts, including those of **King Dostron** (34) and **Saint Markovia** (40).  
**Sergei’s Tomb (K85)**: A well-lit area blocked by a portcullis, noted as a significant point of interest for the group.  
**The Hall of Bones**: Located on the basement floor, this is the suspected resting place of the **Argynvost Skull**.  
**Vallaki**: The party’s current base of operations and the site of their heist rehearsals.

## Encounters:

* **Social: The Morning Eviction**: Rahadin (phonetically “Rod Dean”) ensured the party left Ravenloft without delay, maintaining a cold, professional demeanor.
* **Narrative: The Carriage to Vallaki**: A period of travel and reflection where the party discussed their recent discoveries and planned for their increased capabilities at level 7.
* **Tactical: The Heist Briefing**: A non-combat encounter where the party analyzed map codes and room descriptions to identify hazards and entry points.

## Loot (Heist Objectives):

**(No new loot was acquired this session; however, the party finalized a list of items and people to “acquire” during the heist:)**

* **Argynvost’s Skull**: To be retrieved from the Hall of Bones.
* **Icon of Ravenloft (Icon of Dawn’s Grace)**: Believed to be hidden within the crypts.
* **The Spear of the Huntress**: Located in the south crypts.
* **Strahd’s Gemstones**: Previously acquired stones that the party plans to use for **Locate Object** during the heist.

## Notable NPCs:

* **Strahd von Zarovich**: The Master of Ravenloft. His recent “mercy” toward Aera has left the party wary but emboldened to strike while he is away.
* **Rahadin**: The Count’s chamberlain who facilitated the party’s exit.
* **Ezmerelda d’Avenir**: A famed monster hunter currently traveling with and advising the party.
* **Ireena Kolyana**: The object of Strahd’s obsession, currently under the party’s protection in Vallaki.
* **Emil Tornescu**: A werewolf prisoner the party intends to rescue from the South Dungeon.
* **Gertruda**: Mary’s daughter, confirmed to be located in the King’s Bedchamber.

## Plot Points:

* **Infiltration Intelligence**: The party identified several key zones for the heist:
* **K1 (Courtyard)**: Access may be restricted; need an alternate entry if the drawbridge is up.
* **K7/K8 (Entries)**: Dragon statues and gargoyles are confirmed as active sentries.
* **K14/K15 (Chapel Area)**: The Hall of Faith is noted as “creepy,” while the Chapel is marked for exploration.
* **K16/K17**: Potential vertical paths leading either up to the spires or down to the chapel depths.

* **The Heart’s Pulse**: The discovery of the Heart of Sorrow remains the most significant revelation, though the party has decided to prioritize the heist’s recovery goals before attempting to destroy it.

## Session Highlights:

* **“Straying from the Path”**: Aera’s recap of Strahd’s warning set a somber tone for the session’s start.
* **Level 7 Upgrades**: The celebration of new powers, particularly Aera gaining **Evasion** and Krusty bringing **Fireball** to the table.
* **The Fingernail Gambit**: A meta/in-game hybrid plan where the party discussed “tagging” Strahd to track his movements during the heist.
* **Note:** The “Crokinole Tangent” served as a brief mental break from the gloom of Barovia, with players discussing the finesse required for the board game.
