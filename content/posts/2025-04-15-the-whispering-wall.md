## Story

### Winter Splinter Falls

We hesitated a moment too long on Yester Hill, and Ludmilla called up the mists and stepped back through them, out of our reach. Then, with fire upon fire, we stopped the druids from summoning Winter Splinter. The great statue fell on Veyren and knocked him senseless, and I woke him with a word of healing.

Note: The fight on Yester Hill ran over two nights, 4/8 and 4/15; the 4/8 session had no post of its own. The party reached level 6.

We destroyed Winter Splinter and took the gemheart from it, but at a heavy cost: it burst apart as it died, and nothing was left of Veyren’s body.

### Muriel’s Secret

Then Muriel confessed. She is a wereraven, and it was Elvir Martikov, her betrothed, who gave her the curse. It lets her heal and fly, but dark magic and silver can kill her. Elvir never came back to her after the attack on the castle. She did not lose control and attack Ludmilla, and she cannot pass the curse to us.

### “The Way Back Comes but Once”

We went to the Whispering Wall to meet Strahd and claim our reward. On the way we saw a white fortress on a hill. We walked past Strahd and his steed, Beucephalus, whose saddlebags were empty now. He congratulated us on beating the forest folk and on how we handled Ludmilla, though he warned that she will not forget, and he congratulated us on winning the wager. The wall, he said, is the ancient tomb of a god, and it made the mist. It may show those who enter their deepest desires. For Strahd it shows a white fortress above a city of church bells. He dared us to go in and meet our desires. We could be lost in the wall if we were entranced, he warned, and the way back would come but once. Lesser men often lose themselves there. Each of us who came back safely would get one more gift, a shard of the wall’s own power.

Three of us took the dare:  
\- Zorpheus heard the prophecy confirmed.  
\- I learned that my reborn nature ties me to an ancient line of elven kings.  
\- Gumbo felt the thrill of victory, cheered on by an unseen crowd.

Each of us heard a whisper from behind: “The way back comes but once.”  
I left because I wanted to learn whether the stories I had heard were true. Gumbo left because he did not believe he had truly earned those cheers yet, though he believes he can earn them in the real world. Zorpheus was so taken with the prophecy that he could not remember why he should leave. At the very last moment his head cleared, and he rushed through the gate just as it dissolved.

Ireena went in too, because most of us did, and came out with tears on her cheeks. She will likely tell us her tale, but not while Strahd is near.

### Strahd’s Gifts

Strahd gave us gemstones of two kinds: gray stones that lead to what we desire, and silver stones that carry us a few steps in a breath of mist. Each of us chose one. Then he brought out a moth-shaped artifact, the Twilight Crest, full of powerful magic.

### Kavan’s Legends

Kavan, we thought, might have more of the story, so we went back to his cairn. He told us how his people worshipped the Seeker, the Weaver and the Huntress. The three gemstones were the Weaver’s. He spoke too of the wand he wanted, which we helped him recover. Nearly a thousand years ago Kavan went into the Whispering Wall, which is the corpse of the Devourer that the Ladies killed. The servants of the Seeker are wereravens. The mountain folk and the forest folk came from a split in the first folk, when their loyalties divided. We must travel to Zoldoph, and for that we need the mountain folk’s help: a landmark where two waterfalls run together marks the way in, and we need a token of the Ladies to enter. There is also a spear we can recover to help us, in the tomb of King Dostron in the eastern mountains, at the Hill of Ravenloft.

### Minofaustus’s Market

On the way back to the winery we met a traveling merchant, Minofaustus, whose wagon is a magical market full of rare treasures. He warned us not to haggle too hard, and not to steal. His strangest offer was a discount if we would take Zorpheus off his hands; it seems our rogue was once a thief.

Deals were struck:  
\- Gumbo paid gold and took Zorpheus as his servant, along with a Cloak of Elvenkind.  
\- Liren and I got Gleaming Half Plate armor.  
\- Aera traded for a Shades Ring, but must repay Minofaustus with ten percent interest, another 500 gold owed.

## Key Locations

* **Whispering Wall**: A mysterious tomb that makes Barovia’s mist. It shows visions of desire, and many souls are lost to it.
* **Kavan’s Cairn**: An old place of worship, where Kavan told us the legends of the Seeker, the Weaver and the Huntress.
* **Hill of Ravenloft**: The resting place of King Dostron, where a legendary spear waits.
* **Minofaustus’ Magical Market**: A traveling merchant’s wagon brimming with enchanted things, for a price.

## Encounters

* **Strahd’s Challenge**: An eerie invitation to enter the wall and be enlightened, or be lost to it forever.
* **The Wall’s Test**: – Zorpheus heard that the prophecy is real. – I learned that my reborn nature ties me to a line of elven kings. – Gumbo was cheered on by an unseen crowd. – Each of us heard a woman whisper: **“The way back comes but once.”** – Zorpheus nearly stayed trapped, but came to himself and escaped just in time.
* **Minofaustus’ Market**: – Gumbo bargained away his gold for a servant (Zorpheus) and a **Cloak of Elvenkind**. – Aera took on a debt for a **Shades Ring**. – Liren and I bought **Half Plate** armor.

## Loot

* **Gemstones from the Wall**:
* **Gray Stones**: Lead to objects of desire, casting **Locate Object** when crushed.
* **Silver Stones**: Enable **Misty Step** as a bonus action when crushed.
* Selections:
    * Malron and Liren choose silver.
    * Gumbo, Aera, and Zorpheus choose gray.
* **Twilight Crest**:
    * 3 charges, regenerates one at dawn.
    * **Day**: Cast **Aid**.
    * **Night**: Cast **Spirit Guardians** (necrotic).
    * **Full Charge**: Cast **Innervation**, prevents recharging for three days.
* **Merchant’s Wares**:
* **Cloak of Elvenkind** (Gumbo)
* **Half Plate** (Liren, Malron)
* **Shades Ring** (Aera, pending debt repayment)

## Notable NPCs

* **Strahd von Zarovich**: Gave us the gems as our reward and led us through the Wall’s challenge.
* **Kavan**: Told the old legends of his people’s split and of the need to recover the Spear of the Huntress.
* **Minofaustus (Merchant)**: A traveling trader with a magical market, whose deals come with interest.

## Plot Points

* The Whispering Wall is the tomb of a god, and it makes Barovia’s mist.
* The gemstones Strahd gave us answer to the bearer’s desires.
* Kavan told of old allegiances that split the first folk into mountain and forest clans.
* To reach Zoldoph we need the mountain folk’s help and a token of the Ladies.
* Minofaustus offered an unexpected trade, magic for carefully counted coin.
