## Story

### Davian’s Hiding Place

It was evening when we entered the cloaked figure’s hiding place and found Davian Martikov, father of Urwin Martikov. Davian told us the forest folk, druids, and their blights, along with Baba Lysaga, whom some call Lysa, have driven his family from the Wizard of Wines vineyard. That Lysa was Lady Fiona Wachter’s mentor, the one Lady Fiona angered when she left her teaching.

![The Wizard of Wines winery among misty vineyards, blights lurking at the edge](/images/wizard-of-wines-ai.jpg)

*AI-generated image (Google Gemini).*

He laid out what waits ahead: four druids, eight needle blights, three greater strix and eight vine blights. The druids’ leader carries a magical staff that commands these creatures, and it must be destroyed or stolen. He advised us to go underground into the cellar to find it.

The tunnel to the cellar runs through a cavern full of brown mold. Fire only feeds it, but cold kills it, and we must keep to the middle of the dirt path to come through unharmed.

Davian told us of his family’s grief. Ten years ago, while Urwin was on watch, something valuable was stolen from the winery: a gemstone, a prized heirloom. Then three months ago Davian’s younger son, Elvir, joined a march on Castle Ravenloft from Barovia and died in the castle, and his death shattered the family. We were told of an emergency cache of healing supplies in a barrel in the northwest corner of a storeroom on the winery’s main floor. And the cellar is made of wood, so we must not burn it down.

### Wolf Teeth and Brown Mold

Muriel stayed behind to guard our way out while we went through the tunnel. We found a box marked with a snowflake, hanging from a rusted iron hook. Inside were three winter wolf teeth, enchanted by the mountain folk, that can loose a freezing blast or shield the bearer from cold.

With one of the teeth we blasted our way past the mold and reached the cellar. Our rogues scouted ahead and spotted eight twig blights and a druid.

### A Mouse Among Druids

Veyren became a mouse and overheard a druid torturing a raven for word of something lost. The druid sensed him, called out to the mouse and demanded to talk.
