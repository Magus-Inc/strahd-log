## Summary:

After a long rest at the Burgomaster’s house, and a running fight with scarecrows and dire wolves on the road, we climbed the winding road out of Krezk toward the Abbey of St. Markovia. At the north gate Otto and Zygfrek Belview, two of the Abbot’s mongrelfolk, met us and anxiously asked whether we had seen their brother Cyrus since he was sent to Castle Ravenloft to be “perfected.” We know Cyrus is little more than Strahd’s servant, but Shabasta told them what they wanted to hear: Cyrus is nine feet tall, two men across, the strongest man alive, and so perfect that Strahd wants to keep him. They believed every word and were overjoyed.

Note: The table had not played in about two months. The DM posted a picture of the abbey on the Discord and showed off a box of normal-sized minis he had been given. The party is still seven players, "eight in heart." Shabasta's Deception was a 16.

Before we went in, we remembered the warning of the shard in the icon we carry: “Tread carefully and ready your steel, for the Morning Lord’s rays flare only brighter when they perceive darkness before them.” We agreed to keep the icon hidden from the Abbot for now. In the courtyard (S12) we met Clovin Belview carrying bowls of cold porridge to the padlocked shed. Olfin told him the same tale of Cyrus, and the grateful Clovin kept his promise of a reward. He led us up into the tower (S17) and pulled a black cloth off a stand to show two sets of living bone wings, one feathered like a giant eagle’s and one like a bat’s. Olfin took the eagle wings and Aera the bat wings.

Note: Olfin's roll was a 23. Command word brainstorming followed. "I'm Batman" was the early favorite, and Olfin's "America!" carried the night. The table also briefly argued over whether a paladin or a rogue should be the one to fly.

We found the Abbot in the asylum (S15), a lightless corridor of cells whose prisoners cackle and curse behind locked doors. A handsome young man in monk’s robes stood halfway down the hall with a prayer book under his arm, scolding Vasilka, the “perfect bride” he is building for Strahd, for tending the blighted mongrelfolk on the very day of her “operation.” Behind them loomed the flesh golem we once gave a crown of flowers. The Abbot soothed Vasilka with talk of the Morning Lord’s grace and of everyone’s proper place, then turned to us: “Visitors, welcome back to the Abbey of St. Markovia. What’s your reason for being here?”

Our reason was grim. In Vallaki, at the Blue Water Inn, Baron Dmitri Krezkov of Krezk had begged us to stop the Abbot, who means to kill his wife Anna Krezkova tonight and take her heart for Vasilka. I remembered what Ezmerelda told us: only by defeating the Abbot and unlocking his soul with the Icon of Dawn’s Grace can his corruption be cleansed and his mind restored. We talked of luring the Abbot away from the golem, but the corridor was tight and the cells might hold allies for either side. Aera took rope from her pack, dressed it up by illusion as a new crown of flowers, and walked up and gave it to the golem.

Note: Long above-the-table debate about distractions, illusions and whether a construct counts as a creature. Crusty rolled a 17 on History and learned that flesh golems understand their creator's language but cannot speak. Aera's illusion was Silent Image.

Olfin ended the talking. He told the golem, “We’re here to destroy your master. Will you help us?” and struck at the Abbot with a shining blow. The holy light barely seemed to touch him. Light poured from the Abbot: towering white wings unfurled, his robes fell away to marble-white flesh, golden gauntlets and a golden sarong, and he rose to nearly seven feet. “If you will not obey, then I will make you.” The Abbot had become Ethereal. Vasilka fled in terror.

Aera opened with her moon-touched blade, struck deep where he was unguarded and slipped away. Ethereal flew at her and loosed a ray of light from his hands. Shabasta lifted Aera’s spirit with a song and set Ethereal’s golden gauntlets glowing hot. I spotted a lever that opens the cells, and Olfin later pulled it, freeing four weak, blighted mongrelfolk. Ethereal smashed me in the face, though Lathander’s flare spoiled his aim, and Crusty answered with fire while his owl distracted the angel.

Ethereal seized Olfin and carried him thirty feet into the air. Then the flesh golem joined the fight on our side, hurling a chalice at the Abbot and soaking his face in wine. From the ground Aera shot him with her short bow, a terrible wound. Ethereal flew over her and dropped Olfin on top of her. Aera, lifted by Shabasta’s song, dodged aside, and Olfin took the whole fall.

Ethereal answered with a burst of divine light. I could not resist it and was charmed, and fell on my face to grovel before him, until the next ray of light broke the spell. When Ethereal reached for a mighty healing touch, Crusty undid the spell, and the Abbot gave up trying to heal himself. Shabasta sang to Olfin and kept the gauntlets burning, until Ethereal seized him too, turned his hand into a mace, beat him in the air and dropped him from thirty feet. I laid a ward against evil on myself to guard against more charms. Olfin whispered “America!” to unfurl his eagle wings, drank a potion of fire breathing and took to the air.

Note: Rules talk mid-fight: Malron set the table straight that a concentration save is DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher, not 10 plus half. The DM explained Legendary Resistance and confirmed Ethereal's Wisdom, Dexterity and Charisma saves are all +10.

The golem kept throwing whatever it could find: candlesticks, then a whole table, which shattered against the ceiling. A gale from Ethereal’s wings struck Shabasta senseless, and he was slipping away before my word of healing brought him back. Crusty hit with a bolt of lightning, and Olfin breathed fire and struck with wrath.

Note: The DM revealed that the golem was always going to side with the party because of Vasilka's original crown, but their kindness is what brought it into the fight.

Then Aera’s aim was true once more. Remembering that the Abbot wanted Anna’s heart, she shot him through his own. His body drifted slowly to the ground and I rushed in with the Icon of Dawn’s Grace. But the form began to change: his back arched, the feathers shook off into bat wings, and he became a beast with the head of a lion, the body of a ram, the wings of a bat and the tail of a crocodile. “The Fallen Star.” His second form stands ready, at full strength.

Note: The first phase took about an hour, so the DM called it there rather than run until midnight. The combat dice: Aera's sneak attack for 26, a crit for 58, and her second natural 20 for the shot through the heart. Olfin's opener was a Shining Smite and his last a Wrathful Smite; Shabasta cast Heat Metal; Malron's Investigation was an 18, his flare was Warding Flare and his heal was Healing Word; Crusty used Fire Bolt, Lightning Bolt, and Counterspell against a 4th-level heal (an Intelligence check against DC 14).

## Key Locations:

**Krezk**: The walled village where we have been staying, with a long rest at the Burgomaster’s house.

**North Gate of the Abbey (S6)**: Where Otto and Zygfrek Belview asked about Cyrus and Shabasta convinced them he has been “perfected.”

**Abbey Courtyard (S12)**: Where Clovin Belview carries porridge to the padlocked shed.

**Tower and Belfry (S17)**: Above the asylum; Clovin’s hidden stand held the two sets of bone wings.

**The Asylum (S15)**: A lightless, foul-smelling corridor of cells holding the blighted mongrelfolk. A lever opens the cells. Where we fought Ethereal.

## Encounters:

Shabasta’s tale of Cyrus’s “perfection” fooled Otto and Zygfrek Belview. Olfin told it again to Clovin Belview and won us the wings.

Aera gave the flesh golem a rope crown disguised by illusion, and Olfin asked it to help destroy its master. Later it joined the fight.

The Abbot, become Ethereal. He seized us, flew and dropped us, loosed rays of light, charmed us with a burst of radiance and battered us with a gale from his wings. Aera’s two great shots, the second through his heart; his gauntlets glowing hot; Lathander’s flare and healing word through me; Olfin’s flight and fire breath; and the golem hurling a chalice, candlesticks and a table. Ethereal fell, then rose in a second form, the Fallen Star.

## Loot:

* **Bone Wings of Flying (eagle)**: Olfin. Modified Wings of Flying: an action and a whispered command word (“America!”) unfurl animated bone-and-feather wings for one minute (60 ft fly speed). Usable once per day. Requires attunement.
* **Bone Wings of Flying (bat)**: Aera. Same properties as the eagle set.
* **Potion of Fire Breathing**: Olfin drank one he already carried during the fight.

## Notable NPCs:

**The Abbot / Ethereal**: The Abbey’s master, who appears as a handsome young monk. He is building Vasilka as Strahd’s bride and planning to take Anna’s heart for her. When struck he revealed his celestial form, Ethereal: “If you will not obey, then I will make you.” Beaten once, he rose again as the Fallen Star.

**Vasilka**: The Abbot’s flesh-built “perfect bride” for Strahd. Scolded for tending the mongrelfolk; fled when the fight began.

**The Flesh Golem**: The Abbot’s hulking servant. Wears the crown we gave it long ago, took Aera’s new rope crown and turned on its master, throwing a chalice, candlesticks and a table.

**Otto and Zygfrek Belview**: Mongrelfolk at the north gate, eager for news of Cyrus. They believed Shabasta’s lie.

**Clovin Belview**: The mongrelfolk who carries the porridge; he gave us the bone wings for the news of Cyrus.

**Cyrus Belview** (mentioned): Sent to Castle Ravenloft to be “perfected,” and in truth serving Strahd.

**Baron Dmitri Krezkov and Anna Krezkova** (mentioned): The noble couple who lead Krezk. Dmitri found us at the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki and begged us to stop the Abbot from killing Anna and taking her heart.

**Ezmerelda** (mentioned): Told us the Abbot can only be cleansed by defeating him and unlocking his soul with the Icon of Dawn’s Grace.

## Plot Points:

* The Abbot means to kill Anna Krezkova tonight and give her heart to Vasilka, Strahd’s intended bride.
* As Ezmerelda said, defeating the Abbot and using the Icon of Dawn’s Grace on him may cleanse his corruption and restore his mind.
* The icon’s warning came true: the Morning Lord’s rays flared brighter in the face of darkness.
* The flesh golem is our ally now. We already talk of taking it with us across Barovia.
* The freed mongrelfolk may not trust us; I doubt they believed the lie about Cyrus.
* Ethereal’s second form, the Fallen Star (lion, ram, bat, crocodile), waits for us at full strength.

Note: The doubt about the mongrelfolk came from the DM, who hinted they may not have believed the lie.
