## Summary:
Oogway tried to buy a cloak of invincibility from the wandering merchant, but could not, and settled for some healing potions.

Back at the Wizard of Wines we learned the history of the holy symbol of Ravenkind and its tie to Strahd. Davian gave us an obsidian whistle, and we set out for Krezk, meeting vampire spawn on the way.

## The Reading:

*The cards, in short:*

* **The Darklord:** the master of shadows, a beast straining at its chains.
* **The Six of Stars, the Evoker:** the wild, untamed power we seek.
* **The Artifact:** the token that is the key to power; "Divinity's heart" waits somewhere.
* **The Innocent:** a raven-haired maid with twilit eyes is one way to the token.
* **The Broken One:** another way, through sacrifice; the Whispering Wall awaits a tribute.

## Key Locations:

**Wizard of Wines**: Davian told us the history of the holy symbol of Ravenkind and its tie to Strahd.

**Krezk**: We met the Krezkovs and learned of the werewolf curse on Dmitri Krezkov and his family. His wife gathers wolfsbane to hold the curse back and keep him calmer. The curse passes down the generations, and we do not know which of Dmitri’s children, if any, carry it.

**Abbey**: Someone has taken over the abbey and is building a flesh golem out of corpses, and it needs the heart of a willing victim. The abbot has been spying on the Krezkovs in the shape of Dmitri’s aunt. He talked Dmitri out of the wolfsbane potion, and Dmitri did not know it was helping him. He stopped taking it, turned into a werewolf and killed his sister that night. The abbot has promised to bring the sister back if the mother, Anna, gives up her heart a month from now. Their son has run off into the woods.

## Encounters:

Three vampire spawn on the road to Krezk, all destroyed.

## Loot:

**Obsidian Whistle**: Given by Davian as thanks for recovering the gem the winery needs to make wine. How it works is on the [Obsidian Whistle](/wiki/obsidian-whistle/) page.

## Notable NPCs:

**Davian**: Leader of the Keepers of the Feather, wary of dark magic; gave us an obsidian whistle shaped like a raven.

**Muriel**: Told us Strahd went to Madam Eva for a reading before we did.

**Dmitri Krezkov**: A werewolf by a curse passed down the generations; his wife gathers wolfsbane to hold it back.

**Anna Krezkov**: Dmitri’s wife, who gathers wolfsbane to hold back the curse.

**The Abbot**: The mysterious figure who has taken over the abbey and is building a flesh golem that needs the heart of a willing victim.

## Plot Points:

We learned the history of the holy symbol of Ravenkind and its tie to Strahd. Davian gave us an obsidian whistle, and we set out for Krezk, meeting vampire spawn on the way. In Krezk we learned of the werewolf curse on Dmitri Krezkov’s family, and the abbot’s dark work at the abbey: the flesh golem, and his manipulation of Dmitri.

## Session Highlights:

Beating the vampire spawn on the road to Krezk. Learning that Strahd had his own reading from Madam Eva, and the history of the holy symbol of Ravenkind. The sad story of Dmitri Krezkov and the abbot’s manipulation.
