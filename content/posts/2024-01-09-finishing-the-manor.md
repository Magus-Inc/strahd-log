Aera put the amber shard into a heavy wooden chest with clawed iron feet. A skeleton in leather armor stuck out of it, and it was full of books that crumbled at a touch.

In the chest was a letter:

> My most pathetic servant,  
> -  
> I am not a messiah sent to you by the Dark Powers of this land. I have not come to lead you on a path to immortality. However many souls you have bled on your hidden altar, however many visitors you have tortured in your dungeon, know that you are not the one who brought me to this beautiful land. You are but a worm writhing in my earth.  
> -  
> You say that you are cursed, your fortune spent. Your husband took solace in the bosom of another woman, sired a bastard son, and drove you to abandon love for madness. Cursed by darkness? Of that I have no doubt. Save you from your wretchedness? I think not. I much prefer you as you are.  
> -  
> Your dread lord and master,  
> -  
> Strahd von Zarovich

From the letter we concluded that the monster must be [Elisabeth Durst](/wiki/elisabeth-durst/).

The attic has a hallway with a door held shut by a padlock. Other doors creaked around us, and we could not tell whether they were opening on their own.

One room held a bed, a desk, a music box and a doll. It reminded all of us of the doll Rose held in a portrait we had seen earlier.

[Aera shan Ravanór](/wiki/aera-shan-ravanor/) quietly said “Rose”, and we heard the patter of small feet.

[Schlopkht](/wiki/schlopkht/) opened the music box, trying to muffle any sound it made. Inside were a rusted hunting knife and a key that looked to match the padlock, and two parchments: a crude map showing an altar connected to a shrine connected to quarters, and another about recruitment.

A chamber held a stove and a large trunk under a sheet. In the trunk was a corpse with wounds like those of the spirit on the third floor. Under another sheet was a mirror. Schlopkht saw Elisabeth Durst's reflection in it and felt a cold touch on his shoulder. He broke the mirror and wrapped a shard of it in cloth to take with him.

Another bedroom held nothing of interest, so we unlocked the last door and found the spectral figures of Rose and Thorn. There was a bricked-up window, a toy chest with windmills painted on its side, and a dollhouse that is a perfect replica of the manor, with miniatures of everything in it. There were also two skeletons, one holding a doll. We concluded the children had been left there to starve. Rose mended a toy Thorn had broken with a little spell.

From Rose we learned that the nursemaid who looked after Walter was not [Klara](/wiki/klara/), but we did conclude that Walter was Klara's son. Rose asked [Weeble Wobleston](/wiki/weeble-wobleston/) to promise to bury their bones in the garden, and in return she showed us a secret stairway down to the basement. Then Thorn asked whether he and Rose could come with us as spirits joined to our bodies. Thorn joined with [Olfin Darning](/wiki/olfin-darning/), and Rose with Aera.

We went down the staircase for what seemed three floors or more, and on for what felt like hours, until we reached a dirt tunnel. Somewhere ahead was a sound like chanting.

There was a crypt oozing pus, with something that marked it as Walter's.

Down some stairs, near a few alcoves, was a well with a rope and pulley. Aera lit a stone and dropped it. It fell thirty feet, splashed and went out. Perhaps a creature took it.

A footlocker in that room held a journal of the cult's victims, some of whom had been fed to Walter. By then we believed the monster's name was Walter.

The chanting grew louder as we went on. In one stretch of floor there were no footprints at all, a likely sign of a trap. We set it off with a spectral hand, and it opened onto a pit of sharpened stakes. We jumped the pit into a new room with gnawed bones and a mirror.

When we disturbed the bones, a great worm dropped on us. It looked to be the remains of Gustav Durst, and it tried to fall on Schlopkht, then lashed at us with tentacles and beak. After a long fight [Schlopkht](/wiki/schlopkht/) was knocked out, and Aera shot the creature through the beak for the killing blow.
