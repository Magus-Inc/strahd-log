### Continue on Road to Vellaki * Road ends at iron gates with wolves’ heads impaled on them.

### Refugee Camp * Ireena is enraged and heartbroken at the sight of the refugee camp. * [Schlopkht](https://curseofstrahdowlbear.obsidianportal.com/characters/schlopkht) tries to persuade Ireena to feed the dream pies to the refugees to help them sleep and relax. * Refugees arrived at the gate some days ago but are not allowed to enter. They tried to enter by force, but Izek, a guard, summoned fire to ward the refugees off. The town Baron won’t even come out. Wolves and bats have attacked the camp. Refugees are unwilling to risk heading back home. 25% are addicted to dream pastries. Many are cold to Irena and unwilling to accept her offer of help, blaming her brother and father for their misfortune. They believe a red-haired woman has cursed them. * Ireena becomes enraged by the Baron’s callousness and heads off with Aera.

### Entering Vellaki * Heavy iron chains and padlock keep the gate closed. * One guard has a silvered spear, the other silver crossbow bolts. * We have our teeth checked and are tested with garlic. * Rules for entering: \* 1 gold piece toll to enter. \* Must surrender any silver to the town. * Schlopkht tries to convince the guards to accept pies as a bribe instead of the normal payment requirement. * We each pay one gold to enter. * Guards recommend the Blue Water Inn and the meat pie there.

### In Town * Guards mention that a weird man with long ears is in town on a carnival wagon. * Festival of Blazing Sun in three days. * Be careful what you tell old lady Morantha who sells the pies. * Streets are empty except for one cloaked figure walking away from us. * Several ravens on the roof of the inn, which depicts a blue waterfall. * Colorful dressed man with a big hat is inside the inn. He is a half-elf. Suspect he is the person the guards mentioned. * Irwin at the bar welcomes us into the inn. Married to Danica who runs the inn. * Order wine and a meat pie. * The bard is almost like a ringmaster and very entertaining. Ric Tavio goes upstairs. * There was some unease about the Baron and criticizing them. * See two boys crouched at the balcony in front of the door, dangling a stuffed bat over the bar to mess with Carl and Nicolai. * Carl and Nicolai are against the Baron. Their mother is a sensitive subject. The boys Brom and Bray have good masks. * We rent a room in the inn for one gold each. * Be careful in Vellaki due to the harsh law and political climate. * Barkeep gave a parcel from under the table to Ric Tavio who tucked it under his cloak and walked out of the inn. * Ask about Blinsky toys. Ric Tavio says he is a secret agent of the Keepers of the Feather, a secret society against Strahd. * We lose Ric Tavio in the crowd. * Procession in town results in everyone going silent, led by Baron and Izek. We think Izek pushed an old woman into the mud. Her son Udo has been imprisoned. She is then also taken to the stocks. * Festival of the Sun will be in four days.
