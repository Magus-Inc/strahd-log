## Story

### Toward the Count’s Heights

As the sun sank behind the jagged peaks of the Balinoks, we stood at a turning point. We were leaving the safety of the Blue Water Inn for the heights of the Count’s domain. Everything hung on a rare chance, the master of the house away from home, and so we would no longer wander and ask questions. We would strike.

### Stanimir’s Caravan

We still had twelve hours left of the ward against the touch of death that Strahd had granted us. The road from Vallaki is eight and a half miles. Near the Tser Pool Crossroads we met a Vistani caravan, with the elder Stanimir, Radu and Elizabella telling stories. We were wary of each other, but civil. Stanimir knew what we had done, laying Varushka’s spirit to rest among other things, and he gave Aera a Clairvoyance Stone to help us get inside. He also showed us a gift he must deliver himself to Lady Fiona Wachter: a flat, round stone crest engraved with a three-pointed star, the sign of the Ladies Three. By Madam Eva’s telling, its three points stand for foresight, resilience and courage.

Note: The party briefly debated a “Trojan Bunny” strategy, or stealing the Vistani crest, before settling on diplomatic neutrality.

### Strahd Rides Out

At the edge of the precipice we watched Strahd von Zarovich ride out of the castle on his nightmare, Beucephalus. With the master gone, Aera made herself invisible and Crusty gave her the power to fly. She soared across the chasm and came up behind the wights on the ramparts. With a conjured hand she picked the alarm horn from the first wight’s pocket, then took the second wight’s horn as well. It nearly went very wrong when she tried a running somersault dropkick to send one of them off the edge. She stumbled and bounced off the guard, and the kick undid her invisibility. The fight broke out, and we destroyed the wights. Aera poured oil on the drawbridge chains to quiet them, and we lowered the drawbridge and reached the servants’ entrance.

Note: She rolled a 1 on the somersault. The DM gave Aera back a Luck Point afterward, saying the sentries were “low-level annoyances” next to what waits deeper in.

Inside, everything lay under dust. A weathered, brittle book stood open on a desk beside an inkwell and quill, the ink still fresh, and every page headed with the same request: register for your own convenience and that of your next of kin. It was filled with names. Slumped against the heavy table in the middle of the room lay a withered corpse with four punctures in its neck, drained of blood not long ago, and a dry trail of blood ran from it to the stairs going down.

## Key Locations

* **The Blue Water Inn**: Our base, and the last place of any safety in Vallaki.
* **Tser Pool**: The Vistani camp, where we met the caravan at the crossroads.
* **The Chasm and Drawbridge**: A thousand-foot drop and a fifty-foot gap, Ravenloft’s first defense.
* **The Servants’ Entrance (K1-K2)**: A quiet way in, opened with the password “Ikame Dastra’s memory.” Inside are a register for “next of kin” and a withered corpse.

## Encounters

* **Stanimir, Radu and Elizabella**: Vistani storytellers; a civil meeting that ended with a Clairvoyance Stone for Aera.
* **The Parapet Wights**: A quick fight, begun by Aera’s failed somersault. Picking the horns from their pockets first kept the whole castle from being roused.

## Loot

* **Clairvoyance Stone**: A palm-sized stone with 3 charges to cast the Clairvoyance spell. The item text is on the [Clairvoyance Stone](/wiki/clairvoyance-stone/) page.
* **Silvered Crossbow and 20 Silvered Bolts**: I passed them to Shabasta to give us more reach.

## Notable NPCs

* **Stanimir**: A Vistani elder and messenger of Madam Eva, still friendly for what we have done.
* **Strahd von Zarovich**: Seen riding out of the castle. His absence was the whole of our chance.

## Plot Points

* **The Three-Pointed Star**: A crest meant for Lady Fiona Wachter. Its design hints at a tie to the ancient fanes of Barovia.
* **The Servants’ Register**: A grim ledger in K1 where the staff “register for next of kin.” The withered corpse beside it says the registering is a death warrant.

## Session Highlights

* “Fly, you fools!” — Crusty, sending us aloft.
* **The “Trojan Bunny”**: A plan, set aside, to sneak into the castle inside a gift or a carriage.
* “The cows have teeth” — A chilling remark on the hungry nature of Barovia’s creatures.
* **The Somersault**: Aera’s flying dropkick at an undead sentry, fumbled in midair.
