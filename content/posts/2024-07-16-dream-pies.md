### Journey North

We continued north, skirting around Tser Pool and climbing a cliff.

### Watch Tower

We came upon a ruined watch tower, three stories tall and covered in moss and ivy, with two fresh graves and an animal’s fresh corpse nearby. Barovian refugees lie buried there, slain by wolves, and close by was a dead dire wolf riddled with spears and crossbow bolts. We left the tower alone and went on our way. Who knows what riches or disaster waited inside.

### Tser Falls Bridge

A revenant guarded the bridge and asked whether we served *him*. He named himself [Sir Godfried William](/wiki/sir-godfried-william/), a knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon, banished by his commander, Vladimir Horngaard.

![Portrait of a bridge revenant](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/7db64b11c71d88572ddc6cd06b888976/images/Bridge%20Revenant.png)

### The Fork in the Road

The road split: east to Castle Ravenloft, west to Vallaki. An open gate there put us all on edge.

### Ancient Monument

We found a six-foot stone slab carved with a three-pointed star and a raven, an ancient monument to the Seeker and the Ladies Three.

### The Windmill

At the windmill we met [Morgantha](/wiki/morgantha/), a fiend who sells Dream Pastries and lives there with her daughters, Bella and Offailia. The cat ate one of her pies and fell asleep for five hours, so [Aera](/wiki/aera-shan-ravanor/) bought Morgantha’s cart for five gold to carry him. Morgantha warned that the Vallaki guards confiscate silver, so we hid our silver things in the cart.

### Ravens

Ravens began to follow us, and soon there were hundreds in tow. Ireena, in a stupor, asked to see the lake, but we moved on.

### Wolves

Eight wolves attacked us, one of them perhaps a werewolf. The ravens swarmed that one. [Schlopkht](/wiki/schlopkht/) nearly died but came to in time to rejoin the fight. Aera floated a dream pie to one of the wolves with a conjured hand, and it took that wolf out of the fight.
