## Story

### Graves at the Watch Tower

We continued north, skirting around Tser Pool and climbing a cliff.

We came upon a ruined watch tower, three stories tall and covered in moss and ivy, with two fresh graves and an animal’s fresh corpse nearby. Barovian refugees lie buried there, slain by wolves, and close by was a dead dire wolf riddled with spears and crossbow bolts. We left the tower alone and went on our way. Who knows what riches or disaster waited inside.

### Sir Godfrey at the Bridge

A revenant guarded the bridge and asked whether we served *him*. He named himself [Sir Godfrey Gwilym](/wiki/sir-godfrey-gwilym/), a knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon from Argynvostholt, to the west. He raised his voice against his commander, Vladimir Horngaard, and was banished here to await an order that will never come.

### The Seeker’s Stone

The road split: east to Castle Ravenloft, west to Vallaki. An open gate there put us all on edge.

We found a six-foot stone slab carved with a three-pointed star and a raven, an ancient monument to the Seeker and the Ladies Three.

### Morgantha’s Dream Pies

At the windmill we met [Morgantha](/wiki/morgantha/), a fiend who sells Dream Pastries and lives there with her daughters, Bella and Offalia. The cat ate one of her pies and fell asleep for five hours, so [Aera](/wiki/aera-shan-ravanor/) bought Morgantha’s cart for five gold to carry him. Morgantha warned that the Vallaki guards confiscate silver, so we hid our silver things in the cart.

![Morgantha, an old woman at her pastry cart on a forest road, the windmill behind her](/images/morgantha-ai.jpg)

### Hundreds of Ravens

Ravens began to follow us, and soon there were hundreds in tow. Ireena, in a stupor, asked to see the lake, but we moved on.

### A Pie for the Wolf

Eight wolves attacked us, one of them perhaps a werewolf. The ravens swarmed that one. [Schlopkht](/wiki/schlopkht/), asleep on the cart, nearly died when a wolf bit him, but it woke him in time to rejoin the fight. Aera floated a dream pie to one of the wolves with a conjured hand, and it took that wolf out of the fight.
