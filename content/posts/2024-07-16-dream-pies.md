### Journey North * The party continues north, navigating around the Tser Pool and ascending a cliff.

### Watch Tower Encounter * Discover a ruined three-story watch tower covered in moss and ivy, with two fresh graves and an animal’s fresh corpse nearby. * Barovian refugees are buried here, slain by wolves. There is a dead dire wolf nearby riddled with spears and crossbow bolts. * We decide to leave the tower alone and continue on our journey, who knows what riches or disaster would have awaited us in the tower.

### Tser Pool/Falls Bridge * Encounter a Revenant guarding the bridge, who asks if we serve him. * The Revenant, [Sir Godfried William](/wiki/sir-godfried-william/), claims to be a Knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon, banished by his commander, Vladimir Horngaard.

![](https://publish-01.obsidian.md/access/7db64b11c71d88572ddc6cd06b888976/images/Bridge%20Revenant.png)

### Road Split * Find a split in the road: east to Castle Ravenloft, west to Velaki. * An open gate makes the party feel uneasy.

### Ancient Monument * Discover a six-foot stone slab with a three-pointed star carving and a raven, an ancient monument to the Seeker and the Ladies Three.

### Windmill Encounter * Meet [Morgantha](/wiki/morgantha/), a fiend selling Dream Pastries, living at the windmill with her daughters, Bella and Offailia. * [Aera](/wiki/aera-shan-ravanor/) buys her cart for five gold to carry the cat that ate a pie and fell asleep for five hours. * Hide silver items in the cart as Morgantha warns about Vellaki guards confiscating silver.

### Raven Interest * Ravens start following the party, with hundreds soon in tow. * Ireena, in a stupor, asks to see the lake but the party moves away.

### Wolf Attack * Eight wolves, including a possible werewolf, attack the party. * Ravens swarm the werewolf. * [Schloptk](/wiki/schlopkht/) nearly dies but wakes up in time to join the fight. * Aera uses mage hand to feed a dream pie to a wolf. It manages to take that wolf out of the fight.
