## Story

We set out for St. Markovia’s Abbey, believing Ezmerelda would be there and could help us on our quest. One thing from Madam Eva’s reading was now plain: the Holy Symbol of Ravenkind is the card of the Myrmidon, the five of swords. A switchback road climbs four hundred feet up the mountain, and the air grew colder as we climbed, with snow near the top.

### Otto and Zygfrek at the Gate

The abbey sits on a wide ledge in the mountains. Iron gates barred the road but were unlocked. The two guards, Otto and Zygfrek, bore parts of animals: scales, fur, paws or tails. Zygfrek is a brooding cynic; Otto is all eager, clumsy goodwill. They told us Ezmerelda has only one leg. From the grounds we could see the village far below, and we learned the abbey’s saying: “May her light cure all ail.”

### The Belviews

Inside we met Clovin, and learned that all three are Belviews, of one family. Lepers come to the abbey for help. The Belviews aged too fast and suffered, and Strahd tricked the Abbot into helping them by replacing their failing body parts with the parts of animals. It seems to have driven them a little mad. The Abbot sometimes abducts villagers for more experiments, hoping to cure their ailments. We found a disc engraved with a sun, perhaps the Morning Lord’s.

### A Bride for Strahd

Then we met a young man wearing the Morning Lord’s symbol: the Abbot himself, who wants this to be a place of healing. His skin glows a little. He first met Strahd four hundred years ago, and on his second meeting learned that Strahd is cursed. He believes Strahd lacks love and companionship, and he showed us Vasilka, a woman in a red dress, who he thinks will be the perfect companion for Strahd and so lift the curse on the land.

### Vasilka’s Necklace

The Abbot went to fetch Marzena, a Belview who seems able to fly, and to reach her he became an eagle and took to the sky. Vasilka had made a necklace of flowers, and she told us of another creature like her in the east wing, where she is not allowed to go. She thinks of it as her sibling, since the Abbot made them both, and wanted it to have the necklace as a gift. I took the necklace and promised to deliver it.

### The Golem in the East Wing

The Abbot took us to the east wing, where a seven-foot flesh golem stood with no light in its eyes. He told it to take us to Ezmerelda, and it led us to an office. We passed scarecrows on the abbey walls. The golem would not enter the barracks, but pointed us in, and I gave it Vasilka’s garland. It stared at us in silence.

### Ezmerelda

Inside, a half-finished chalk circle lay in the middle of the floor, and a Vistani woman sat by it in two boots of different colors, one of them copper, perhaps not a boot or a leg at all. A magical rapier and a hand-axe lay on the table beside her. Ezmerelda left her studies with Van Richten three months ago; she alone could follow him through the mists to Barovia. She came to the abbey because Krezkov warned her of the Abbot, and she means to look into his growing madness. Three days ago she thinks she found Ilya, the Krezkovs’ son, in a cave by Lake Baratok, where a pack of wolves or werewolves lairs. She has a twelve-hour window to rescue him. The Abbot, we learned, is some kind of angelic being, tainted by Strahd, which is why he is not himself.

### The Séance

She held a séance to speak with the spirits of the abbey. When it ended we had a hint: a silver statuette hidden in Castle Ravenloft can cure the Abbot. Cyrus, the Belviews’ patriarch, was taken to Castle Ravenloft, and if we bring him back to the abbey we will be well rewarded. Ezmerelda laid out her plan to get into the den with care, and took us to her wagon.

### Bianca’s Confession

There we met [Bianca](/wiki/bianca-werewolf/), who lives in the woods nearby with her husband Kiril. She had taken a lyre from the wagon and was playing a song by the tower, and had taken the perfume too. She fell in love with Kiril Stoyanovich, who has been full of rage since Strahd came back, perhaps because Strahd holds power, perhaps because Kiril is a werewolf. We aren’t sure. Bianca is a werewolf too, and Kiril, the Black Terror, leads the pack. Prisoners like Ilya are kept in Mother Night’s shrine at the back of the cave, and the old leader is left there as well. Four werewolves stay behind to guard; we think six remain when the main pack is out hunting. Werewolves serve Strahd. Ezmerelda once tracked Kiril, and he devoured her leg. Baba Lysaga made him stronger still, and Ludmilla helped him with a pack of dire wolves, until he was strong enough to take the pack.

## Key Locations

* **St. Markovia Abbey**: Once sacred, now twisted by dark experiments, looking down on the valley from its perch in the mountains.
* **Ezmerelda’s Wagon**: Hidden near the abbey, holding supplies, clues and unexpected visitors.
* **Lake Baratok Cave**: Home to werewolves and to Ilya, who was taken there. Dangerous, and urgent.

## Encounters

* **The Belviews**: We met Otto, Zygfrek and Clovin, grotesquely changed by the Abbot’s misguided attempts to cure them.
* **The werewolf den**: What we learned says we must go carefully to get into the cave and rescue Ilya.
* **The séance with Ezmerelda**: The spirits of the abbey whispered of a silver statuette in Castle Ravenloft that could cure the Abbot.

## Loot

* **Necklace of Flowers**: A gift from Vasilka to her sibling in the East Wing. I carried it there and gave it to the flesh golem.
* **Magical Rapier & Hand-Axe**: Weapons laid near Ezmerelda during the séance.
* **Holy Symbol Disc**: Engraved with a sun, perhaps the Morning Lord’s.

## Notable NPCs

* **Ezmerelda**: A skilled monster hunter, seeking answers about the Abbot’s growing madness.
* **The Abbot**: An angelic being tainted by Strahd, who believes Vasilka can break the land’s curse.
* **The Flesh Golem**: The Abbot’s seven-foot servant in the east wing, Vasilka’s “sibling”. I gave it her garland of flowers.
* **Kiril Stoyanovich**: The Black Terror, the ruthless leader of the werewolf pack, empowered by Baba Lysaga.
* **[Bianca](/wiki/bianca-werewolf/)**: A conflicted werewolf tied to Kiril, who told us something of the pack.

## Plot Points

* Ezmerelda plans to break into the werewolf den within twelve hours to rescue Ilya.
* The Abbot believes Strahd lacks love, and is making Vasilka to save him.
* The spirits hint at a silver statuette in Castle Ravenloft that could cure the Abbot’s corruption.
* Cyrus Belview may hold the key to a reward if we rescue him from Ravenloft.

## Session Highlights

* “May her light cure all ail”, the eerie saying of the Abbey.
* The flesh golem and its silent stare.
* My promise to deliver the flower necklace, a rare kindness amid the madness.
* Ezmerelda’s harrowing past: her lost leg and her vendetta against Kiril.
* [Bianca](/wiki/bianca-werewolf/)’s confession, her loyalty torn between love and survival.
