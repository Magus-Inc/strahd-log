## Story

### The Rider Without Light

We saw a skeletal rider on a horse wearing chain mail and holding a rusty lantern that sheds no light. Some of us think he might be the skeleton of the poor soul we found along the road a few days earlier. He paid us no mind but rode to the south along an old hunting path. We split up and followed him at a distance. [Weeble Wobleston](/wiki/weeble-wobleston/) and I trailed further back, and the main group followed the rider from thirty feet or so.

### Back to the Main Road

This went on for over half an hour before we decided nothing good can come of it and we should head back to the main road. At this point we grouped up again and started north as one group. After some time I noticed some movement in the forest and suggested to [Schlopkht](/wiki/schlopkht/) to sneak around and investigate while we kept going north.

### A Toll We Didn’t Pay

It turned out to be five men dressed much like Vistani guards, with one as a leader. They tried to extort a toll from us to be allowed back on the main road. [Aera shan Ravanór](/wiki/aera-shan-ravanor/) tried bribing them with a smaller amount and as they rebuffed the attempt Weeble put one to sleep with a spell, one of the ones further back. This kicked off a fight where the leader tried to escape after he was injured, but we were ruthless and Weeble put him to sleep as he ran. His minions were even more ruthless though, as one threw a knife at the leader, probably in an effort to wake him, but it impaled him and finished him off for us. So much for interrogating him.

We looted the dead and found gems worth ten or fifty gold apiece.
