# Adventure Log: Shadows and Steel—The Ravenloft Infiltration Plan

## Characters:

* **Aera shan Ravanór (Liz)**: Wood-elf Rogue (Level 7). Having mastered the art of Evasion, she is now a ghost amidst the flames, capable of emerging unscathed from dragon breath or reckless Fireballs. Her pseudodragon companion (discovered in Argynvostholt) remains her eyes in the dark, vital for scouting.
* **Malron Lichtstrahl (Chris)**: Reborn Cleric of Lathander/Spirit Medium. As the vessel for an ancient Elven spirit (the result of a failed resurrection), he remains the group’s tether to the light and the only thing standing between the party and the encroaching rot of the Grave.
* **Shabasta (Grant)**: Bard of Fate (Level 7). Having crossed into 4th-level spells, he can use Dimension Door to bypass physical impossibilities. His mastery of Silvery Barbs allows him to reach into the strands of time to pluck away a foe’s success.
* **Olfin Darning (Seth)**: Hexblood Paladin. Wielder of the Symbol of Ravenkind. His Aura of Devotion now projects a ten-foot sanctuary of sanity, ensuring his allies cannot be charmed by the Count’s honeyed lies.
* **Krusty – Ralph Kristofferson (Cole)**: Wizard (Level 7). A newcomer commanding Polymorph and Greater Invisibility. His ability to reshape the forms of his companions transforms the party into a pack of apex predators.

## Summary:

The survivors gathered beneath the flickering lamps of the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki, the memory of the Count’s wine still bitter on their tongues and the terror of their “failed” dinner crystallizing into cold resolve. The session was a somber ritual of preparation, dominated by the meticulous “leveling” of abilities.

Amidst the sharpening of blades, the air was thick with meta-tactical debates. The group parsed 5th Edition rules as ancient law, specifically the “overpowered” nature of Silvery Barbs to negate the Count’s strikes. A brief respite of “Canadian politeness” occurred during a discussion of Crokinole, yet the focus remained on the proactive heist, synthesizing intelligence from the dissolved spawn Doru and the maps of Rudolph van Richten to exploit Strahd’s 14-day hunting cycles.

## Key Locations:

**K1 (Front Courtyard)**: The 35-foot chasm remains the primary gatekeeper. So What? Without a mechanical solution or magical flight, the party is bottlenecked at the entrance, vulnerable to the heights.

**K7/K8 (Entryways)**: The dragon and gargoyle statues are sentient watchers. So What? Stealth is mandatory; these sentinels likely serve as a silent alarm system for the castle’s denizens.

**K15 (The Chapel)**: A site of former sanctity now defiled. So What? The “armored corpse” noted by Doru is a dynamic threat—it was “gone the next day,” suggesting a powerful, wandering guardian.

**K84 (Catacombs)**: A labyrinth of the dead targeting Crypt 34 (King Dostran) and Crypt 40 (St. Markovia). So What? These specific crypts house the artifacts required to break the land’s curse; distraction elsewhere is a waste of time.

**K85/K86 (Tombs of Sergei and Strahd)**: Sergei’s tomb is an unnatural beacon; Strahd’s is the ultimate target. So What? Expected to be vacant during the new moon, but the magic of the crypts may harbor his presence if the party lingers.

**K88 (Tomb of King Barov & Queen Ravenovia)**: Rahadin’s sanctuary. So What? To enter here is to invite a confrontation with Strahd’s chamberlain, turning a heist into a bloodbath.

## Encounters:

[Tactical/Puzzle] (The Infiltration Strategy): The party weighed the “Arrow and Rope” approach against Krusty’s 4th-level magic. Using Greater Invisibility and Dimension Door, the party plans to bypass gate towers entirely.

[Logistical/Puzzle] (The Extraction): Discussion on the Dragon’s Skull. The party devised a plan to use Enlarge/Reduce to shrink the skull (or the Heart of Sorrow) to fit within the 500-pound dimensional limit of the Chest of Holding.

## Loot:

**Symbol of Ravenkind**: A legendary relic held by Olfin Darning, essential for the Aura of Devotion and fighting vampires.

**Chest of Holding**: A 500-pound capacity dimensional vessel identified as the key to smuggling massive relics out of the castle.

**Van Richten’s Maps**: Provided by the hunter; these strip Ravenloft of its greatest defense: the ability to swallow the lost in its corridors.

## Notable NPCs:

**Doru (Deceased)**: Though clinically dissolved in the river’s running waters, his posthumous intelligence provides the foundation for the party’s map.

**Esmeralda d’Avenir & Irina**: Currently in Vallaki; they represent the reserve force capable of holding the perimeter during the dive.

**Strahd von Zarovich**: The predator whose 14-day cycle provides the window of opportunity; the party aims to strike while he is hunting away from the den.

**Van Richten**: The hunter whose contribution of the castle maps has turned a suicide mission into a calculated heist.

## Plot Points:

**Argynvost’s Skull (Priority One)**: Returning this to the “Shining Beacon” at Argynvostholt will ignite a light that pierces the Barovian gloom, granting a permanent boon to the resistance.

**Retrieve the Icon of Dawn’s Grace**: A silver statuette that is the only cure for the Abbot’s madness; without it, Krezk remains under a celestial’s broken rule.

**Recover the Spear of the Huntress**: A shard of the “Ladies Three” ancient power; a necessary prerequisite for facing the Count.

**Rescue Emil Tornescu & Gertruda**: Freeing the werewolf leader could shatter Strahd’s alliance with the wolf-kin, while saving Gertruda from the King’s Bedchamber fulfills a moral imperative.

## Session Highlights:

**The Vampiric Axolotl**: A vivid visual for the Heart of Sorrow—a 10-foot red crystal heart with a sliver of darkness swirling within.

**Canadian Politeness**: A meta-commentary on Crokinole, suggesting the party should be “polite” while flicking enemies into the castle chasm.

**“Ghost In, Fire Out”**: The consensus approach—use stealth and magic to secure objectives, but prepare for a “guns blazing” exit if the undead awaken.
