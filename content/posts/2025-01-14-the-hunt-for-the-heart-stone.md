## Story

### How to Take a Heart Stone

Victor told us there are two ways to get a heart stone from a hag: she gives it willingly, or we kill her. Willingly means a deal, and a hag keeps only to the letter of her word and drives a hard bargain. Hags tend to gather in covens of three, which makes them stronger, and a hag can use her heart stone to escape into the ethereal realm before she dies. A binding circle raised before the fight would stop them escaping. But hags can dispel almost anything within their coven, so Victor must come with us to counter their dispelling, or the circle will collapse.

Victor showed us a picture of a binding circle, though he was unclear how to make one. Just then Stella gestured from the mirror to get our attention: for the circle to work, the hags must be named by name. A binding circle around the Wachter estate keeps Stella out, and she suspects her mother knows how to make one. We talked Victor into letting us bring his mirror, and into coming with us.

### Lady Fiona Lowers the Circle

At Wachterhaus Olfin drew Lady Fiona into conversation. She was cold toward Victor. We asked her to lower the circle. When she was young her parents were killed, and she decided a malevolent spirit was to blame, so she used magic learned from her mentor, Lysa, to raise the binding circle and keep that spirit out. A miniature scale model of the manor anchors the circle. She lowered the spell, and a flower floated down into her lap, Stella’s favorite flower. Then Stella drew a crescent, the shape of the birthmark on Luna, the cat. Lady Fiona seemed convinced that Stella was there, and that Victor may not be to blame. We realized the Gallows Speaker is most likely the malevolent spirit that killed Lady Fiona’s family.

A binding circle can keep the hags out or hold them in. We must catch all three at once, or the others will hunt down whoever escapes. Lady Fiona suggested we save the children before we take on the hags, and we talked of splitting up to save the children and get the contract at the same time.

### “Let Me In”

We rested and made ready for the journey. In the night someone scratched words on the window: “Let me in.” When Lady Fiona joined us, her pet imp familiar showed itself, and she brought four fanatical companions to help her with the binding circle.

### The Forest Folk

On the way we met a druid of the Forest Folk, hermits of the Svalich Woods who shun the towns. They hold Yester Hill, at the southwest edge of the valley, sacred, and they worship Strahd as a god for his power over the land and weather.

![A beaten hag lies by the windmill's pie cart, and her black onyx heart stone, an egg-sized polished oval, has slipped from her robe onto the mud](/images/heart-stone-ai.jpg)

Lady Wachter first learned of the druids as a student of her mentor, Lysa, a woods-witch of the Berez swamps who worshipped Mother Night, goddess of darkness, trickery and the occult. Lady Wachter never heard Mother Night’s “voice”, as Lysa called it, and took up the faith of Ezra soon after she came back to Vallaki.

Lysa did not take her leaving lightly. A proud woman who thought of her students as her children, she took Lady Wachter’s return to Vallaki as a betrayal, and Lady Wachter has been banished from Berez ever since. Her house’s “debt” to Strahd is older still: centuries ago, when the traitor and assassin Leo Dilisnya murdered the husband of her ancestor, Lady Lovina Wachter, and tried to kill Lovina too, Strahd defended her and hunted Dilisnya down. House Wachter has been loyal to Strahd ever since.

### The Plan

Then we laid our plan, working through how many hags there are, how to learn their names, where the children are, how to rescue them and how to fight the hags. Bella and Offalia are up in the attic with the children, and Morgantha is at the bottom, cooking. Victor will help us with his magic: a step through the mist, flight, and invisibility. Shabasta will distract the hags while our rogues sneak into the tower, free the children and take the names from the gnarled arm on the third floor, then climb out from the third floor.

## Key Locations

* **Wachter Estate**: A grand manor ringed by a binding circle that keeps out malevolent spirits. Here we sought Lady Fiona’s help and learned more about binding circles.

## Encounters

* **Lady Fiona**: We talked her into lowering the binding circle, and the truth came out about the malevolent spirit and her daughter Stella.
* **The hags, still to come**: We mean to trap them with a binding circle so they cannot escape.
* **The druid**: A druid of the Forest Folk watched us from a tree. Why it stopped us to spy, I cannot say.

## Loot

* **Mirror of Victor**: The mirror Stella speaks to us from. We brought it along, and Victor came too, to counter the hags’ power to undo magic.
* **Binding Circle Knowledge**: How to make and use a binding circle to trap the hags.

## Notable NPCs

* **Victor**: A learned wizard who gave us crucial help, including the mirror.
* **Lady Fiona Wachter**: The lady of the manor, who lowered the binding circle and told us of the malevolent spirit.
* **Stella**: Lady Fiona’s daughter, whose presence showed itself in small signs and gestures.

## Plot Points

* **The Heart Stone**: A hag gives it up willingly in a deal, or it is taken from her body.
* **Binding Circle**: Without one the hags will escape into the ethereal realm.
* **Malevolent Spirit**: The Gallows Speaker is the spirit that caused the deaths of Lady Fiona’s parents.
* **The Children**: We must free the children the hags hold before we face them.
