# Depths of Ravenloft

**Chronicle of the Larder’s Ransom: The Sanctuary Sprint and the Torture Chamber’s Threshold**  
Shadows of the Larder: The Drowning Cells of Ravenloft

The air of Castle Ravenloft has changed utterly. We have come down from the wind-swept heights of the spires, where we saw the crystal malice of the Heart of Sorrow, into the cramped and lightless bowels of the castle’s foundations. Forty feet down, in the Larders of Ill Omen, we walk through rot and stagnant, ice-cold water. This is a harder road, one that will cost us dearly, as the grand halls above give way to the flooded, black-mirrored passages of the Drowning Cells.

## Characters:
* [Shabasta](/wiki/shabasta/)
* [Aera shan Ravanór](/wiki/aera-shan-ravanor/)
* [Olfin Darning](/wiki/olfin-darning/)
* [Malron Lichtstrahl](/wiki/malron-lichtstrahl/)

## Summary:

We are at a turning point, and the end of all this is coming into view. Our great aim for the fight with Strahd is the Sunsword, a blade of pure sunlight, though it lies hidden in the Amber Temple, a place we have not yet mapped.

Our first steps in the Larders were a comedy of errors with the strange servant Cyrus Belview. We tried to win his favor with the wooden spoon we found in the Bone Hole, only to learn we had brought him an ordinary spoon. His lost heirloom, he told us, is “bigger than normal” and quite “lonely.”

Note: [Meta details] The "Spoon Comedy" was a moment of levity at the table. The DM clarified that the item from the bone hole was the "wrong spoon", and the players had a self-deprecating laugh about their "fool's errand" of treating random kitchenware as vital quest items.

The right spoon lay at the far end of a corridor packed with forty motionless zombies. Olfin made a desperate dash for it. Disguised as one of the plague-spreading dead, he wrapped himself in Lathander’s sanctuary and a ward against evil and charged into the horde. It worked beautifully: as Olfin snatched the “lonely” spoon, I took hold of him with an invisible conjured hand and the power of my mind and yanked him back through the doorway, and we slammed the steel-banded door just as the zombies began to stir.

Note: [Meta details] The DM was genuinely shocked that this worked, given the massive risk. That led to a table-wide talk about OSR (Old School Essentials) rules for future games to cut down on "crunch", and the DM's new "Heroic Moment" homebrew.

Note: Olfin's spells were Sanctuary and Protection from Evil and Good; Malron's pull was Mage Hand plus Telekinetic.

We went on down into the Drowning Cells, wading through three to five feet of murky water, roped together to keep from losing one another. The rope was tested at once. Olfin stepped on a hidden plate, air and water burst around him, and the rope in my hands went slack. He had been carried off into an iron maiden in the torture chamber. The rest of us, swimming or treading water, followed Aera, who got us past the next traps to the cells, where we heard a prisoner’s teeth chattering.

## Key Locations:

* Larders of Ill Omen (Map 11): A cold, damp foyer of rotting debris and “blinded beans.” It holds the elevator trap and is the doorway to the depths.
* The Serpent’s Wing / South Tower Stair: A vital way up and down. Cyrus’s map, a few simple boxes and lines, shows that this stair joins the Dungeons to the Garrison, the Main Entry, the Royal Apartments, and the Witch’s Coven on the fifth floor.
* The Drowning Cells: A hallway where the water lies like black mirrored glass, broken only by the steady chattering of teeth. With iron bars under the water and water five feet deep, every step wears you down.
* The Torture Chamber: A fifty-foot square stage of horrors. Chains hang like thick black web over racks and iron maidens. A balcony draped in red velvet looks down on it all, with two thrones beneath a mocking lamp.

## Encounters:

* Cyrus Belview: We traded him the right, “lonely” spoon for a map of the Serpent’s Wing. Despite his lazy-eyed suspicion, and our earlier ruining of his stew, he stayed a willing source of information.
* The Hall of Forty Zombies: A terrible gamble. Olfin’s disguise and my unseen pull got us past a fight that would likely have killed us all, so many were they, plague-spreaders among them.
* The Teleporting Step: Aera had found every trap so far, but this one took Olfin by his weight. It reset itself, and we went on through the flooded hall one short.
* Emile Torenescu: We found the werewolf prisoner. He is broken in body, worn to the edge of collapse and bound by a silver thorn collar. The key is held by Volinta, and forcing the lock would drive the thorns through him.

Note: Aera's Sleight of Hand had rolled a 26 earlier. Emile has three levels of exhaustion, and forcing the collar deals lethal piercing damage.

## Loot:

* Skull of Argynvost: The party successfully shrunk the 20-ton skull and secured it within the “Chest of Holden” (a pun on the standard Chest of Holding), a strategic victory for their long-term quest.
* Candle of Long Resting (Homebrew): Given by the DM to counter Ravenloft’s “crunchy” difficulty. Lighting this candle grants the benefits of a long rest during the duration of a short rest (one-time use).
* The Heirloom Spoon: The “lonely,” oversized spoon returned to Cyrus.
* The “Wrong” Spoon: An ordinary kitchen spoon from the bone hole, kept now as a funny reminder of our mistake.

* Holy Symbol of Ravenkind: The DM shared the item's full rules this session (charges, Dawn's Embrace, Light of Hope, Sun's Blessing). See [Symbol of Ravenkind](/wiki/symbol-of-ravenkind/).

## Notable NPCs:

* Cyrus Belview: The deformed, eccentric servant who cares most for his missing heirloom. A pitiful but dangerous fixture of the castle’s service halls.
* Emile Torenescu: A werewolf prisoner of Strahd, kept for weeks in the flooded cells. He could be an ally, if we survive the fight for his key.
* Volinta (The Bride): A cruel dweller in the castle, who appeared on the balcony of the torture chamber. She mocked us for coming uninvited, and stands beside a twenty-gallon jar of oil, threatening to set the water alight and turn the chamber into a fiery trap.

## Plot Points:

* A new chapter begins, and our eyes turn to the Amber Temple.
* The silver thorn collar sets a clock running: we must beat Volinta and free Emile before exhaustion or the coming fight claims him.
* The South Tower Stair gives us a clear, if dangerous, way up to the castle’s royal heights and the witches’ coven.

## Session Highlights:

* The Unseen Rescue: My mind’s pull hauling Olfin out of a hall of forty zombies, a moment the DM called a “statistical miracle.”
* The Lonely Spoon: The wrong spoon, and the hunt for one described as “lonely.”
* The Vanishing: The rope going slack in the dark water as Olfin was carried off to the torture chamber.
* The Bride’s Welcome: Volinta appearing on the balcony, the start of a “meaty fight” with burning oil and drowned zombies.
