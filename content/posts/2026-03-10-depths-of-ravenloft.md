# The Adventure Log: Depths of Ravenloft

**Chronicle of the Larder’s Ransom: The Sanctuary Sprint and the Torture Chamber’s Threshold**  
Shadows of the Larder: The Drowning Cells of Ravenloft

The atmospheric weight of Castle Ravenloft has undergone a profound metamorphosis. The party has transitioned from the soaring, wind-swept heights of the spires—where they witnessed the crystalline malice of the Heart of Sorrow—into the claustrophobic and lightless viscera of the castle’s foundations. Descending forty feet into the Larders of Ill Omen, the party now navigates a landscape defined by decay and stagnant, ice-cold water. This descent marks a shift into a more punishing, resource-intensive phase of their journey, as the grand architecture of the upper floors gives way to the submerged, black-mirrored halls of the Drowning Cells.

* [Shabasta](/wiki/shabasta/)

## Summary:

This session functioned as a pivotal transition, closing the chapter on Act 3 and turning the party’s eyes toward the campaign’s endgame. Strategically, the group has identified the Sunsword—a “lightsaber” of solar energy—as their primary objective for the confrontation with Strahd, though they are aware it lies hidden within the Amber Temple, a location they have yet to map.

The initial exploration of the Larders was characterized by a comedy of errors involving the eccentric servant Cyrus Belview. The party attempted to curry favor by returning a wooden spoon found in the Bone Hole, only to realize they had presented him with a mundane utensil. Cyrus clarified that his lost heirloom is “bigger than normal” and quite “lonely.”

Note: [Meta details] The “Spoon Comedy” served as a moment of levity above-the-table. The DM clarified that the item from the bone hole was the “wrong spoon,” leading to a self-deprecating discussion among the players about their “fool’s errand” in identifying random kitchenware as vital quest items.

To rectify the mistake, the party located the correct spoon at the terminus of a corridor packed with forty unmoving zombies. Olfin initiated a high-stakes tactical sprint. Disguising himself as a “Zombie Plague Spreader,” he layered Sanctuary and Protection from Evil and Good upon himself and charged into the horde. The operation became a masterpiece of cooperation: as Olfin snatched the “lonely” spoon, Malron utilized an invisible Mage Hand and a Telekinetic (TK) pull to yank the Paladin back through the doorway, slamming the steel-banded door shut just as the zombies began to react.

Note: [Meta details] The DM expressed genuine shock at the success of this maneuver, noting the massive tactical risk involved. This led to a table-wide discussion of OSR (Old School Essentials) rules for future games to mitigate “crunch,” and the DM’s implementation of the “Heroic Moment” homebrew. To underscore the session’s themes of betrayal and high stakes, the DM shared a real-world anecdote about his boss discovering a million-dollar theft by a suicidal friend, illustrating the heavy atmosphere of Ravenloft.

The descent continued into the Drowning Cells, where the party waded through three to five feet of murky water. To maintain cohesion, they tethered themselves together with ropes. This precaution was immediately tested when Olfin triggered a hidden pressure plate. An “explosion of air and water” surged around him, and the rope in Malron’s hands went limp—Olfin had been teleported away into an Iron Maiden in the torture chamber. The remaining party members, now swimming or treading water, were led by Aera, who successfully bypassed subsequent traps before reaching the cells where they heard the chattering teeth of a prisoner.

## Geography of Dread: Key Locations

* Larders of Ill Omen (Map 11): A cold, moist foyer containing rotting debris and “blinded beans.” It houses the elevator trap and serves as the threshold for the descent.
* The Serpent’s Wing / South Tower Stair: A vital vertical artery. Cyrus’s map—a series of simple boxes and lines—revealed that this stair connects the Dungeons to the Garrison, the Main Entry, the Royal Apartments, and the Witch’s Coven on the fifth floor.
* The Drowning Cells: A hallway where the water surface resembles “black mirrored glass,” disturbed only by the rhythmic chattering of teeth. The submerged iron bars and 5-foot water levels transform simple movement into a struggle against exhaustion.
* The Torture Chamber: A 50-foot square “theatrical stage” of horror. Hanging chains resemble thick black web strands over a room filled with racks and iron maidens. A balcony draped in red velvet overlooks the carnage, where two thrones sit beneath a mocking lamp.

## Tactical Log: Encounters & Conflicts

* Social: Cyrus Belview: The party successfully exchanged the correct “lonely” spoon for a map of the Serpent’s Wing. Despite his lazy-eyed suspicion and the party’s previous “stew-ruining” antics, Cyrus remained a passive source of information.
* Hazard: The Hall of 40 Zombies: A high-risk gamble. Olfin’s “Plague Spreader” disguise and Malron’s TK assistance allowed the party to bypass a combat that likely would have resulted in a TPK given the sheer volume of enemies and the presence of Zombie Plague Spreaders.
* Trap: The Teleportation Step: Despite Aera’s previous Sleight of Hand successes (rolling a 26), this magical displacement was triggered by Olfin’s weight. The trap reset itself, leaving the party to navigate the submerged hallway a member short.
* Social: Emile Torenescu: The discovery of the werewolf prisoner. Emile is physically broken, suffering from three levels of exhaustion and bound by a “Silver Thorn Collar.” The party learned that the collar’s key is held by Volinta and that any attempt to force the lock would result in lethal piercing damage to the wearer.

## Acquisitions & Artifacts: Loot

* Skull of Argynvost: The party successfully shrunk the 20-ton skull and secured it within the “Chest of Holden” (a pun on the standard Chest of Holding), a strategic victory for their long-term quest.
* Candle of Long Resting (Homebrew): A vital resource provided by the DM to counter Ravenloft’s “crunchy” difficulty. Lighting this candle grants the benefits of a long rest during the duration of a short rest (one-time use).
* The Heirloom Spoon: The “lonely,” oversized spoon returned to Cyrus.
* The “Wrong” Spoon: A mundane kitchen item recovered from the bone hole; currently serves as a comedic relic of the party’s misidentification.

* Holy Symbol of Ravenkind: Ryan shared the item's full rules this session (charges, Dawn's Embrace, Light of Hope, Sun's Blessing). See [Symbol of Ravenkind](/wiki/symbol-of-ravenkind/).

## The Cast of Ravenloft: Notable NPCs

* Cyrus Belview: The deformed, eccentric servant whose primary concern is his misplaced heirloom. He remains a pathetic but dangerous fixture of the castle’s service halls.
* Emile Torenescu: A werewolf prisoner of Strahd who has spent weeks submerged in the cells. He remains a potential ally, provided the party can survive the coming battle to retrieve his key.
* Volinta (The Bride): A cruel inhabitant of the castle who appeared on the torture chamber balcony. Mocking the party for their lack of an invitation, she stands poised by a 20-gallon jar of oil, threatening to ignite the water’s surface and transform the chamber into a fiery trap.

## Chronicles of Destiny: Plot Points & Highlights

Plot Points:

* The transition into Act 4 has begun, shifting the focus toward the Amber Temple.
* The “Silver Thorn Collar” has introduced a ticking clock: the party must defeat Volinta to free Emile before the exhaustion or the upcoming combat claims him.
* The discovery of the South Tower Stair provides a clear, albeit dangerous, path to the castle’s royal heights and the witch’s coven.

Session Highlights:

* The Invisible Rescue: The cinematic collaboration where Malron’s Telekinetic pull saved Olfin from a hallway of 40 zombies, a moment the DM described as a “statistical miracle.”
* The Lonely Spoon: The table’s humor regarding the “wrong spoon” and the subsequent hunt for a utensil described as “lonely.”
* The Vanishing: The sudden tension of the rope going limp in the dark water as Olfin was teleported into the clutches of the torture chamber.
* The Bride’s Welcome: Volinta’s dramatic reveal on the balcony, signaling the start of a “meaty fight” involving flammable oil and submerged zombies.
