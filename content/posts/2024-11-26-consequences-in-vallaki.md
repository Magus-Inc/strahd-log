**Characters:**  
- **Liren:** Dragonborn Barbarian  
- **Gumbo:** Half-orc Rogue  
- **Aera:** Elf Rogue  
- **Malron:** Reborn Cleric  
- **Shabasta:** Porcupine Bard  
- **Olfin:** Hexblood Paladin

**Summary:**  
We gave the bones to Father Lucian so he could bless them and renew the ward, and Ireena is to stay at the church under its protection.

Back at the Blue Water Inn we found the doors shut, the shutters closed and a window broken. Gumbo worked the latch open from inside with a conjured hand, and there was Urwin sweeping up glass. Izek Strazni, the Baron’s enforcer, had ransacked the inn when he learned Ireena was in town, and stolen some coins. Urwin was only relieved we were safe. We offered gold to help put things right.

A messenger came from Fiona Wachter with an invitation for Aera to dine the next night, the same night as the blessing.

We had a choice to make: go to the dinner, look into Izek, or see to the trouble at Blinsky’s. Olfin sent a lock of his hair to Ireena so he could speak with her and warn her about Izek. Overnight bats wheeled about as the full moon drew near, and Aera shot some of them outside the window.

At Blinsky’s we found the shop on fire and Blinsky unconscious. When he came to, he told us Izek had done it after a doll went missing. Shabasta rallied a crowd to put out the fire and demand justice from the Baron. Blinsky mentioned he had recently sold a noise toy to Henrik.

We went on to the mansion, and Shabasta disguised himself as Blinsky to send the crowd home and collect money for repairs. The crowd was confused and drifted off without giving much. Vallakovich questioned Shabasta, who faltered, and the Baron had his guards shoot at him. Vallakovich made it plain he is still Baron.

Then we went to Wachter Haus, where the invitation got us in. We were shown to a dining room and offered wine or water as we liked.

**Key Locations:**  
1. **Blue Water Inn:** Ransacked by Izek; we helped Urwin.  
2. **Blinsky’s:** Set on fire by Izek; the crowd gathered there.  
3. **Wachter Haus:** Where the dinner invitation led us.

**Encounters:**  
- **Urwin and Fiona Wachter’s messenger:** News and an invitation.  
- **Izek’s menace and Shabasta’s disguise:** A crowd stirred up and sent home again.  
- **The fire at Blinsky’s:** A consequence of our earlier pilfering.

**Loot:**  
- **Nothing taken.**

**Notable NPCs:**  
- **Father Lucian:** Preparing the blessing.  
- **Urwin:** Innkeeper, hurt by Izek’s raid.  
- **Izek Strazni:** The Baron’s enforcer, with a flaming demon arm.  
- **Fiona Wachter:** Sent the dinner invitation.  
- **Blinsky:** His shop burned by Izek; sold a noisemaker toy to Henrik.  
- **Vallakovich:** The Baron, asserting his authority.

**Plot Points:**  
1. The ward is to be renewed with the bones.  
2. Izek ransacked the inn searching for Ireena.  
3. We had to choose between the dinner, Izek and Blinsky’s.  
4. Blinsky’s burned and the crowd demanded justice.

**Session Highlights:**
