# The Boon, the Bride, and the Beating Heart

## Characters:
* [Aera shan Ravanór](/wiki/aera-shan-ravanor/)
* [Gumbo](/wiki/gumbo/)
* [Malron Lichtstrahl](/wiki/malron-lichtstrahl/)
* [Zorpheus](/wiki/zorpheus/)
* [Olfin Darning](/wiki/olfin-darning/)

## Summary:

The party continues their uneasy stay at Castle Ravenloft. After suspecting that Aera may have been bitten by Strahd, the vampire lord cryptically remarks:

> **“The cows have teeth after all.”**

He then grants the party a boon: **resistance to necrotic damage for 36 hours** (two long rests). Moments later, he reveals that none of them were marked—he was merely toying with them.

Strahd begins a **grand tour of the castle**, during which Malron spots **pulleys running up and down a second-floor hallway and a mannequin dressed like Strahd** that seems to be attached to them—perhaps a cheap trick to scare visitors.

Meanwhile, **Sasha pulls Zorpheus aside**, asking:

> **“Do you intend to steal from Castle Ravenloft?”**

She pleads for the party to **rescue Gertruda**, who is being held in the Keep. Gertruda was once **Doru’s fiancée**, and Ismark had sent her out to recover after she was lost on the road to Vallaki.

Sasha shares her tragic past—her husband died, and she longs to see Strahd fall and the morning light return. She informs the party of a **rear servants’ entrance** they can use when Strahd is away, though she cannot unlock it herself.

> **“I come in Dostron’s memory.”** — Sasha reveals this as Strahd’s oldest password, known only to Rahadin.

She is sure the party will have to face Rahadin if they sneak in while Strahd is out.

Later, **Escher escorts Zorpheus upstairs**, rejoining the rest of the party. **Rahadin enters with a chained prisoner**, his head covered by a sack.

> **“The prisoner waits your judgment, my lord.”**

Gumbo is handed a sword and tasked with delivering punishment, though the nature of the crime and the prisoner’s identity remain unclear.

Suddenly, a **bolt of lightning strikes the south gate tower**, disabling the bridge and requiring repairs. The party returns to the grand entry for tea while Strahd investigates the damage.

**Diabola** informs them:

> **“The lightning struck a room upstairs. The bridge is disabled and repairs will take until morning.”**

The party is offered **guest quarters**, with assurances that no servant will lay hands on them if they remain in their rooms. Sasha guides them up three floors to a tower guest room with a single bed.

**Rahadin warns**:

> **“Do not leave your chambers until morning. Dark fiends roam the castle at night.”**

**Escher is assigned to guard the door**, and the room is locked with a key Rahadin holds.

Inside, **Gumbo searches a clock** and finds a **map indicating that the beating heart of the castle lies in the north tower**.

The party attempts to stay awake, but all are mysteriously forced into unconsciousness. When they awaken, **something has changed**…

## Key Locations:

**Castle Ravenloft – Guest Tower Room**: The party is confined here overnight, guarded by Escher.  
**Castle Ravenloft – Grand Entry**: Tea is served while Strahd investigates the lightning damage.  
**Castle Ravenloft – Second Floor**: Malron spots pulleys and a Strahd mannequin, perhaps a cheap scare.  
**Rear Servants’ Entrance**: Sasha reveals its location for future infiltration.  
**North Tower**: Rumored location of the castle’s beating heart.

## Encounters:

* **Social Encounter – Sasha’s Plea**: She asks Zorpheus to rescue Gertruda and shares her tragic past.
* **Moral Dilemma – The Prisoner**: The party is asked to judge and punish a chained figure.
* **Mystery – Forced Sleep**: Despite efforts to remain awake, the party is magically subdued.

## Loot:

* **Necrotic Resistance Boon**: Granted by Strahd, lasts 36 hours.
* **Map Hidden in Clock**: Reveals the location of the castle’s beating heart.
