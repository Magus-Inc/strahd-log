## Summary:

Our uneasy stay at Castle Ravenloft went on. We had come to suspect that Strahd had bitten Aera, and the vampire lord answered cryptically:

> **“The cows have teeth after all.”**

He then granted us a boon: **resistance to necrotic harm for a day and a half**, two nights' rest. Moments later he told us that none of us had been marked after all. He was only toying with us.

Strahd began a **grand tour of the castle**. On the second floor I spotted **pulleys running up and down a hallway and a mannequin dressed like Strahd** that seemed to be attached to them, perhaps a cheap trick to scare visitors.

Meanwhile, **Sasha drew Zorpheus aside** and asked him:

> **“Do you intend to steal from Castle Ravenloft?”**

She pleaded with us to **rescue Gertruda**, who is held in the Keep. Gertruda was **Doru’s fiancée**, the one Ismark sent us to recover after she was lost on the road to Vallaki.

Sasha shared her sad history: her husband died, and she longs to see Strahd fall and the morning light return. She told us when we could sneak in while Strahd is away, and of a **rear servants’ entrance** at the side of the castle, though she cannot let us in herself.

> **“I come in Dostron’s memory.”** Sasha says this is Strahd’s oldest password, and only Rahadin is old enough to remember it.

She is sure we will have to face Rahadin if we sneak in while Strahd is out.

Later, **Escher brought Zorpheus upstairs** to the rest of us. **Rahadin entered with a chained prisoner**, a collar at his throat and a sack over his head.

> **“The prisoner waits your judgment, my lord.”**

We were made to judge him, and Gumbo was handed a sword to carry out the punishment, though we never learned the crime or who the prisoner was.

Suddenly **lightning struck the south gate tower**, disabling the bridge. We went back to the grand entry for tea while Strahd saw to the damage.

**Diabola** told us:

> **“The lightning struck a room upstairs. The bridge is disabled and repairs will take until morning.”**

We were offered **guest quarters**, with the promise that no servant would lay hands on us so long as we stayed in our rooms. Sasha led us up three floors to a tower guest room with a single bed.

**Rahadin warned us**:

> **“Do not leave your chambers until morning. Dark fiends roam the castle at night.”**

**Escher was set to guard the door**, and Rahadin locked us in and kept the key.

Inside, **Gumbo searched a clock** and found a **map showing that the beating heart of the castle lies in the north tower**.

We tried to stay awake, but one by one we were all pulled into unconsciousness. When we woke, **something had changed**…
