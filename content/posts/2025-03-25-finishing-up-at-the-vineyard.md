## Summary:
The druids worship Lorgoth and are hunting for a missing stone that matters to their rites. They warned us that Winter Splinter, a ruinous thing, is coming to tear down everything in its path. In the room grew a tree like the one on Yester Hill, where the druids are making ready for a ritual.

The druids had caught ravens, believing them spies for the shadow lord. One druid boasted that he held the shadow lord’s staff. We cut him down and destroyed the staff.

Then the forest folk attacked, and their hatred for Davian showed. He belongs to the Keepers of the Feather, who oppose Strahd. The Keepers guide and shelter rather than fight openly, and the ravens are part of how they work. Urwin Martikov is one of them too.

Davian told us of his younger son, Elvir, who defied his father’s warning and joined Doru’s doomed assault on Castle Ravenloft.

The winery depends on three green gemstones whose magic makes the vines grow. One was lost or stolen ten years ago. Baba Lysaga stole the second three weeks ago, and the druids took the third, so the wine has stopped altogether. Davian believes we can win back the druids’ gem on Yester Hill, but warned that we are not strong enough to face Baba Lysaga.

He suspects the druids mean to summon Winter Splinter, and urged us to reach Yester Hill before dawn to recover the gem.

After a long rest we were woken two hours before dawn and set out. Maren has secretly arranged to meet a contact named Kavan, whose reputation Davian does not like. Swarms of ravens flew with us, and Olfin tied a token to one of them.

Veyren read druidic runes telling of Kavan the Blood Drinker, and Mira, who heals herself of any wound, called Kavan up by dripping her blood. Kavan appeared, holding a long wooden spear.

We felt something watching us from the forest, a powerful and ancient presence with a crimson light. When we looked, there was a shape in the shadows, studying us.

We suspect a gemstone is hidden in a statue’s chest, perhaps tied to summoning Winter Splinter. Radiant magic slows and burns the tree on Yester Hill, which will help us.

Sverog offered Kavan a wand in exchange for a powerful weapon. In the middle of it all, Strahd rode up on his fiery steed and greeted us ominously.

## Key Locations:

**Yester Hill**: The druids’ ritual site, where the tree grows in Winter Splinter’s shape.  
**Forest**: Dark woods full of crimson-lit mysteries.

- The druids worship Lorgoth and are preparing to summon Winter Splinter.  
- The winery’s three gems are bound up with Baba Lysaga and the druids.

- Kavan’s motives and his tie to Mira hint at more intrigue.  
- Strahd’s arrival means a reckoning is near.
