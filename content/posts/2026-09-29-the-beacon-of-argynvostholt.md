## At a Glance:

* **A warning from the dead:** A revenant messenger tells the party Commander Hornguard will order his knights to hunt them down by noon tomorrow.
* **The road to Argynvostholt:** A three-hour run from Krezk, a vampire-drained corpse and a false trail.
* **Puppet strings:** A revenant in the foyer, bound by crawling black wire, paralyzes Aera before Crusty's Fire Bolt takes its head off.
* **The skull returns:** Shabasta and Aera teleport into the mausoleum, and with Zora's help Aera mounts Argynvost's skull without a fight.
* **Your watch has ended:** Argynvost rises, Vladimir Hornguard finds his rest, and the knights of the Order are freed.
* **The beacon:** The party swears the Order's oath, Olfin's longsword is blessed, and Argynvost burns as a silver beacon over Barovia.

## Summary:

As the party wakes, a revenant guard named Harkus Grishenko finds them, sent to summon them to speak. He gravely passes on a message from a phantom warrior: Commander Vladimir Hornguard has ordered every knight of the Order of the Silver Dragon back to Argynvostholt by noon tomorrow. Hornguard is enraged by the party's incursion into Castle Ravenloft, sees Strahd as a necessary evil, and is expected to command his knights to destroy the party at any cost. The revenant apologizes for the harm he may be forced to do them if they meet again. Has the party spoken with Sir Godfried William and learned of his mission? They have. Then, he urges, if they hold the skull, they must fulfill the dragon's prophecy and return it to the cemetery before Hornguard releases his full wrath.

The party debates sneaking in or simply arriving fast. Ryan lays out the road: from Krezk through the Raven River crossroads it is about three hours to Argynvostholt. They decide to book it.

Running at double speed, they make their perception checks at disadvantage and come across a desiccated corpse in the middle of the road: fancy clothing, no money, nobody of importance, and bite marks on the neck where it has been drained. Malron gives it last rites while the others keep watch. Halfway through the second leg they realize they have been following a false trail and lose an hour backtracking, but they still reach Argynvostholt before dusk.

Note: "Desiccated" set off a long table debate (desecrated? dried out? those little packets?) until Ryan read the definition aloud. Malron's verdict: "Fed on by a vampire. Got it."

Thick fog covers the floor of Argynvostholt's foyer. At the foot of the once-grand staircase stands a single revenant in patterned chainmail with a rusted longsword, red eyes glinting behind a beaten helmet. Strands of black spiked wire wrap its limbs and crawl over its flesh like puppet strings. "May you greet your deaths with honor and courage," it rasps, "as our general has commanded."

Note: The party's initiative rolls were bad enough that Ryan, who had planned to let them dispatch it quickly, made them fight it out in theater of the mind.

The revenant goes first. It fixes Aera with its glare; she fails the Wisdom save with a 13 and is paralyzed, and frightened after that. It hits Olfin for 14. Crusty's Scorching Ray, with his owl distracting the foe for advantage, lands two rays for 13 fire damage. Malron touches the paralyzed Aera and casts Protection from Evil and Good on her. Shabasta's Ray of Frost hits for 8. Olfin hits twice for 13 and 10, and when the revenant tries to walk away toward Shabasta, Olfin's Sentinel attack hits for 7 more and stops it in its tracks, forcing it to keep fighting the tank. Freed of the paralysis, Aera shoots with advantage and sneak attack for 20. Crusty's Fire Bolt adds 18, the revenant shrugs off Malron's Sacred Flame, and Shabasta's Chromatic Orb burns it for 10. The revenant hits Olfin again for 10 as Olfin rolls a 1 on his second swing, Aera hits once more, and Crusty finishes it with a Fire Bolt for 15: "Just like Kamehameha," it takes the revenant's head off. Before it collapses, the revenant warns them that Commander Hornguard waits for them in the cemetery: "May the Morning Lord protect you."

Note: Ryan pointed out afterward that the black wire was the clue. Catching that flavor text, the party could have cut the strings and freed the revenant instead of killing it.

The skull belongs in the mausoleum at the back of the cemetery, behind a high wall, and Hornguard is waiting. The party argues it out: directly, sneakily, a sniper on the guard tower, a zip line, Crusty polymorphing someone into a T-Rex to break through the mausoleum wall. They settle on "sneaky but direct": the others will approach Hornguard as the distraction while Aera slips around with the skull. Zora flies to Aera and gestures wildly, trying to shape a skull with her claws, but Aera can't understand her and even Crusty, who speaks Draconic, gets only simple gestures and squeaks.

Then Shabasta offers a shortcut: the mausoleum is only 50 feet away, and he has Dimension Door. Ryan warns that a teleport can go wrong and leave them stuck on another plane. Shabasta, cheerfully: "Some of you may die." He takes Aera with him anyway. They arrive inside the mausoleum and need a stealth roll of 19 or higher; Shabasta rerolls with his inspiration coin and makes it with a 21.

Vladimir Hornguard is in the mausoleum with them, monologuing at the rest of the party: "You're too dangerous to be left alive. Your antagonism to von Zarovich is a threat I shall not tolerate." Then Zora flies in and harries his face. While he is distracted, Aera climbs the wall with the skull and sets it on its mount, a straight Dexterity check of 26. Black shadows swirl from the torches and dark flames spread across the cemetery in a ring, but because Aera befriended a pseudodragon long ago, and because of Zora's distraction and the skull reaching its place first, the party avoids the fight with Hornguard entirely.

Silver light rises through the mist toward the skull, and pricks of light flare in its great eyes. With a roar like thunder, a bank of silver mist bursts from the mausoleum and an enormous ghostly dragon coalesces in the sky: Argynvost. Vladimir drops his greatsword and stares upward. "My knights, what has this land done to you?" the dragon rumbles. "You have forsaken courage for fear and faith for despair, and so forgotten me." Vladimir, weeping, swears they never gave up the faith. The dragon reaches out its clawed hand to offer him rest, but Vladimir stops: "What have I left, if not the suffering of the one who took all I once loved?" Argynvost turns to the party to help.

Shabasta speaks: "Although your efforts to prevent us from doing this were good-hearted, we knew that you truly wanted this done. And so we did it in the same heart that you had." Vladimir places his hand in the dragon's palm. "From this until the end of days." With a single claw on the knight's cheek, Argynvost tells him, "Your watch has ended," and Vladimir crumbles away like fine ash, his empty armor falling beside his sword.

Note: Someone immediately called "Dibs" on the armor.

The revenants haunting Argynvostholt dissolve into mist, the spirits of the Order's knights are freed from their corpses, and from the chapel comes a clatter of chains. Sir Godfried's spirit takes shape, restored to the noble, golden-haired knight he was before, his scarlet eyes now piercing blue. He kneels to the party: "Hail, friends. Hail, Lord Argynvost." The dragon thanks them; his knights are saved, and his long-waning hope is rekindled. He was woken weeks ago by distant dragon song from beyond Barovia, and he senses a grand alignment approaching. He asks whether any of them carries an amber shard. Aera does, and he warns her to beware how fiercely she gazes into its abyss.

Through Sir Godfried, the party learns more of the Amber Temple. Weeks ago a dusk elf named Kasimir Velikov came to Argynvostholt seeking its history, location and contents. Godfried warned him away from the darkness within but sensed magic, intelligence and desperation in him, the kind the temple preys on. The temple lies near the peak of Mount Ghakis at the end of a trail long lost; Kasimir may have rediscovered it, and may guide them there.

Note: The table weighed making a beeline over the river and up the mountain and decided they would probably die. They got lost last time even following the road.

Argynvost offers a boon to one melee weapon for restoring his skull. Shabasta has his special spear and Aera her meat cleaver, so the blessing goes to Olfin's plain longsword until they find the Sunsword. Argynvost asks if they understand the evil they face and the trials ahead; they say they have endured too much to stop. He asks them to bear the legacy of the Order of the Silver Dragon, and they swear its oath after Ryan, line by line: "With honor as my shield and truth as my guide. In the shadow of evil, I shall be the light. In the face of despair, the beacon of hope... From this to the end of days."

Note: When asked to take the oath, the table wanted it in writing and a lawyer first.

His spirit, he warns, will burn as a beacon to tell the valley the final conflict is at hand, but not for long: perhaps half a cycle of the moon, about seven days, before it is consumed. They must make haste. A chorus of thousands of distant voices rises, the silver flames devour the dragon's form, and the blaze surges like a comet into the manor's tallest tower. For an instant the land for miles is painted silver. The light finds Sasha alone on the rooftops of Castle Ravenloft, her face twisted with sudden hope; Kavan's spirit at his lonely cairn, lifting his head in remembrance; and Lady Fiona Wachter on the sagging doorstep of her manor, tears on her cheeks. The beacon still shines over the valleys.

While it shines, the party and every creature that opposes Strahd gains +1 to AC and +1 to saving throws for as long as they remain in Barovia. Crusty is now at AC 19.

Note: Ryan: "We're now in the final act." The Amber Temple, the Sunsword, and Strahd. The party is level 8; the campaign runs to level 10 and Ryan levels by milestone, not XP.

## Key Locations:

**Road from Krezk to Argynvostholt**: About three hours by way of the Raven River crossroads. A vampire's victim lies in the road, and a false trail costs the party an hour.

**Argynvostholt, the foyer**: Fog over the floor and a lone revenant on the grand staircase, bound by black wire.

**Argynvostholt, cemetery and mausoleum**: Behind a high wall at the back of the grounds. Where Hornguard waited, and where Argynvost's skull now rests on its mount.

**Argynvostholt's tallest tower**: Now home to the silver beacon of Argynvost's spirit.

**Amber Temple**: Near the peak of Mount Ghakis, at the end of a long-lost trail.

## Encounters:

Social: Harkus Grishenko, a revenant messenger, warns the party of Hornguard's coming order and urges them to return the skull.

Exploration: A drained corpse on the road; a false trail costs an hour.

Combat: A wire-bound revenant in the foyer. It paralyzes Aera and hits Olfin twice; Olfin's Sentinel pins it, and Crusty's Fire Bolt takes its head off.

Stealth and social: Shabasta's Dimension Door, Zora's distraction and Aera's climb put the skull in place without a fight; Shabasta's words help convince Vladimir Hornguard to accept his rest.

## Loot:

* **Olfin's longsword, blessed by Argynvost** (stats as Ryan posted them on Discord after the session): a +1 magic weapon with 3 charges, all regained at dawn.
    * **1 charge, bonus action:** silver flames wreathe the blade for 1 minute. Each attack deals an extra 1d4 cold damage, and on a critical hit the target must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution save or be paralyzed until the start of the wielder's next turn.
    * **2 charges, bonus action:** cast *beacon of hope*.
    * **3 charges, action:** a blast of silver flame, a 15-foot cone for a melee weapon or a 30-foot line for a ranged one. Each hostile creature in it makes a DC 20 Constitution save, taking 58 (13d8) cold damage on a failure or half as much on a success. Once used, it can't be used again for one year.
* **Beacon of Argynvost**: While the beacon shines, +1 to AC and +1 to saving throws for the party and every creature that opposes Strahd, as long as they remain in Barovia.
* **Vladimir Hornguard's armor and greatsword**, left where he fell.

## Notable NPCs:

**Harkus Grishenko**: A revenant guard of the Order who delivers the warning and apologizes for the harm he may be ordered to do.

**Vladimir Hornguard**: Commander of the Order of the Silver Dragon, who chose vengeance against Strahd over rest. "What have I left, if not the suffering of the one who took all I once loved?" At last he takes the dragon's hand and turns to ash.

**Argynvost**: The silver dragon, restored as a spirit by the return of his skull. He frees his knights, blesses Olfin's longsword and burns himself into a beacon. "Your watch has ended."

**Sir Godfried William**: Freed from his revenant body and restored to the knight he was. He shares what he knows of Kasimir and the Amber Temple.

**Zora**: Aera's pseudodragon, whose distraction made it possible to mount the skull without a fight.

**Kasimir Velikov** (mentioned): A dusk elf who sought the Amber Temple and may be able to guide the party there.

**Sasha, Kavan and Lady Fiona Wachter** (glimpsed): Touched by the beacon's light across Barovia.

## Plot Points:

* Vladimir Hornguard is at rest and the knights of the Order of the Silver Dragon are free.
* The party has sworn the oath of the Order and now carries its legacy.

* A grand alignment approaches: distant dragon song woke Argynvost weeks ago.
* Argynvost warns Aera about the amber shard she carries.
